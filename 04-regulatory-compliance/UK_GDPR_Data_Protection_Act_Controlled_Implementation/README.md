# Manual 41 — UK GDPR / Data Protection Act Controlled Implementation

Practical controlled implementation guidance for UK data protection governance using the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, including amendments from the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, with PECR and ICO material maintained in their proper legal/guidance layers.

## Controlled editions
- English: `controlled/en/MANUAL_41_CONTROLLED_EN.md`
- Neutral Latin American Spanish: `controlled/es-419/MANUAL_41_CONTROLLED_ES-419.md`
- Brazilian Portuguese: `controlled/pt-BR/MANUAL_41_CONTROLLED_PT-BR.md`

## Publication artifacts
Exact provenance-bound DOCX/PDF files are retained under `publication/en`, `publication/es-419`, and `publication/pt-BR`. These artifacts must not be regenerated after candidate freeze without a new provenance and QA cycle.

## Release controls
Publication requires source/version reverification, trilingual structure/parity QA, exact candidate provenance, document/PDF inspection, exact-byte staging, workflow-security validation, predecessor publication, and final registry reconciliation.
