# Appendix V — AI Evidence Register

## Evidence-quality criteria

Evidence should be:

- relevant to the requirement or control;
- complete for the applicable period and system version;
- attributable to an accountable source;
- authentic and protected from unauthorized alteration;
- understandable without unsupported assumptions;
- consistent with other records;
- retrievable within required timeframes;
- retained and disposed of lawfully.

## Evidence categories

- inventory and ownership;
- applicability, role, and classification;
- risk and impact assessments;
- data governance and lineage;
- technical documentation;
- validation and testing;
- transparency and human oversight;
- logs and monitoring;
- vendor and contract records;
- incidents, complaints, and corrective action;
- training and competence;
- conformity and regulatory correspondence;
- audit and assurance.
