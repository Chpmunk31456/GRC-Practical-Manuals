## Cross-cutting findings

1. All ten appendices have a single identified source and no competing corrected masters were found.
2. Appendices K–N and R–T require corrected masters because the current templates do not consistently distinguish binding actor-specific duties from organizational readiness practices.
3. Appendices O, P, and Q can remain canonical after adding legal-status headers and explicit modality statements.
4. Every deadline field must require the exact provision, actor, event starting the clock, recipient, application date, and source. No universal incident or change deadline should be implied.
5. Conformity-readiness material must distinguish internal readiness, internal-control conformity assessment, notified-body involvement, product-sector assessment routes, and authority review.
6. Technical documentation and post-market monitoring duties must be mapped to the actual provider/system classification; voluntary use by deployers or other actors should be labelled as governance practice.
7. No deletion is authorized by this audit.

## Required corrective actions

- Create corrected masters for K, L, M, N, R, S, and T.
- Add legal-status and modality headers to O, P, and Q or create corrected masters if final editorial integration requires uniform filenames.
- Cross-check K against Article 11 and Annex IV.
- Cross-check L against Articles 43, 47–49 and applicable product legislation.
- Cross-check M against Article 72.
- Cross-check N against Article 73 and the current consolidated reporting rules.
- Cross-check Q against Article 4.
- Cross-check R against Articles 13, 26, and 50.
- Cross-check S and T against Article 25 and the statutory substantial-modification framework.
