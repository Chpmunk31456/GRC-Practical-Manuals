# Manual 27 — Data Governance & Privacy Engineering Controlled Implementation

This directory contains the controlled source and eventual publication package for Manual 27.

The published NIST Privacy Framework Version 1.0 is the stable NIST framework baseline for this release. Privacy Framework 1.1 remains an Initial Public Draft / forthcoming update and is change-watch material only until NIST publishes a final version. NISTIR 8062 remains a published privacy-engineering reference for privacy risk management and the engineering objectives of predictability, manageability, and disassociability.

Jurisdiction-specific privacy laws, regulations, regulator guidance, contractual obligations, records requirements, and organizational policy are maintained as separate source layers. This manual does not imply that any one jurisdiction's privacy rights, consent rules, transfer mechanisms, breach rules, retention requirements, or automated-decision requirements apply universally.

Controlled publication languages are English, Spanish (es-419), and Portuguese (pt-BR). Project translations are unofficial unless separately documented otherwise. Publication remains sequential behind published Manual 26 and is governed by the repository's fail-closed source, localization, rendering, accessibility, integrity, provenance, workflow-security, substantive-review, and release-state controls.
