## Controlled source note

The current central authoritative-source validator does not yet allow `www.imda.gov.sg`. Do not bypass that control. Add IMDA to the central registry only after a reviewed allowlist change.

## Release-source rules

- Reverify current IMDA framework versions before release.
- Distinguish governance guidance and assurance tools from binding legal obligations.
- Verify any AI Verify testing claims against current official materials.
- Treat the Agentic AI framework as a current operational governance reference, not certification.
