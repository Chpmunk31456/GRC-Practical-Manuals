# EU AI Act Manual — Publication Release Checklist

## Release status

A deliverable must be labeled as one of:

- Draft
- Reviewed English master
- Translation draft
- Reviewed translation
- Publication candidate
- Published release

No draft may be represented as a reviewed or published edition.

## Content completeness

- [ ] Chapters 1–138 are present and in correct order.
- [ ] Appendices A–Z are present and in correct order.
- [ ] Front matter, legal notice, scope statement, acknowledgements, and version information are complete.
- [ ] Table of contents matches final headings and page numbers.
- [ ] Cross-references resolve correctly.
- [ ] No placeholder text, duplicated section, or abandoned figure remains.

## Legal and citation review

- [ ] Every chapter has a completed legal-audit record.
- [ ] Every appendix has a completed legal-audit record.
- [ ] Article, annex, recital, role, date, deadline, retention, and penalty references are verified.
- [ ] Binding law is distinguished from guidance, standards, and recommended practice.
- [ ] Known legal issues are corrected or explicitly disclosed.
- [ ] Official sources are current as of the stated review date.

## Editorial review

- [ ] Terminology follows the approved style guide.
- [ ] Legal modality uses must, should, and may consistently.
- [ ] GlobalWay examples are internally consistent and clearly fictional.
- [ ] Controls, evidence, audit tests, metrics, and checklists use parallel structure.
- [ ] Spelling, grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and hyphenation are reviewed.
- [ ] Tables are readable and consistently formatted.

## Graphics and accessibility

- [ ] Every included figure appears in the graphics register.
- [ ] Legal claims and article references are approved.
- [ ] Captions are accurate and consistently numbered.
- [ ] Alt text is present and meaningful.
- [ ] Contrast and non-color cues are adequate.
- [ ] No rejected, duplicated, mixed-language, or typographically defective asset remains.

## Translation review

For Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese editions:

- [ ] Translation began from the frozen English master.
- [ ] Defined terms and legal roles follow the approved glossary.
- [ ] Article and annex references match the English master.
- [ ] Tables, captions, labels, and alt text are translated.
- [ ] A second-pass linguistic and legal-consistency review is complete.
- [ ] No English fragments remain except intentional official names or citations.

## DOCX production

- [ ] Styles are used consistently rather than manual formatting.
- [ ] Heading hierarchy is correct.
- [ ] Automatic table of contents updates successfully.
- [ ] Headers, footers, version, and page numbers are correct.
- [ ] Tables repeat header rows where appropriate.
- [ ] Images are anchored and sized consistently.
- [ ] Accessibility metadata and document language are set.
- [ ] File opens without repair warnings in supported office software.

## PDF production

- [ ] PDF is generated from the approved DOCX or controlled source.
- [ ] Bookmarks and links work.
- [ ] Fonts render correctly without substitution defects.
- [ ] Text is searchable and selectable.
- [ ] No content is clipped, missing, overlapped, or blank.
- [ ] Tables and graphics remain legible.
- [ ] Page count and table of contents are correct.
- [ ] Accessibility properties are reviewed to the extent supported by the production workflow.

## Page-by-page visual inspection

- [ ] Cover and front matter
- [ ] Table of contents
- [ ] Every chapter opening
- [ ] Every appendix opening
- [ ] Every table spanning pages
- [ ] Every figure and caption
- [ ] Headers and footers
- [ ] Final page and back matter

## Repository and release controls

- [ ] All release files are committed to the authorized branch.
- [ ] Source files and generated deliverables are clearly separated.
- [ ] File names, versions, and language codes are consistent.
- [ ] No confidential, personal, temporary, or local-system file is included.
- [ ] Branch comparison has been reviewed.
- [ ] Pull-request description documents scope, testing, known limitations, and review status.
- [ ] Owner authorization is obtained before merge or external release.
