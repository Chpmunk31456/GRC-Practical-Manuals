**Verification date:** 1 September 2026  
**Status:** CONTROLLED DEVELOPMENT / SOURCE WATCH

## Controlled framework state

- AI RMF 1.0 remains the controlled baseline for GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE unless NIST publishes a superseding final revision.
- NIST AI 600-1 is treated as the GenAI profile extending AI RMF risk management for generative-AI risks and actions.
- Repository release preparation must reverify NIST's revision status immediately before publication.
- Draft, proposed or revision-in-progress material must not be represented as a final superseding framework.

## Operating mapping

Manual 49 will operationalise:

**GOVERN → MAP → MEASURE → MANAGE**

as an enterprise AI-risk operating cycle, with GenAI-specific risk treatment layered through NIST AI 600-1, including evaluation/TEVV, content and information integrity, data/privacy/security, human-AI configuration, third-party dependencies and incident/monitoring concerns.

## Release-source rules

- Use NIST primary publications as the authority for framework/profile content.
- Distinguish framework guidance from legal obligations and certification standards.
- Reverify any NIST AI RMF revision status immediately before candidate freeze and again before publication.
- Do not claim that alignment with NIST AI RMF or NIST AI 600-1 establishes legal compliance or certification.
