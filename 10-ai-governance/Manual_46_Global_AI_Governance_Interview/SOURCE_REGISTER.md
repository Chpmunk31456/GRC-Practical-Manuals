## Release-source rule

Before Manual 46 is promoted from controlled development to release candidate:

- Recheck every time-sensitive legal/regulatory date against primary/official sources.
- Confirm whether any referenced draft, proposal, consultation or code has been superseded.
- Record verification date and reviewer/evidence.
- Distinguish binding law, standards, voluntary frameworks, codes of practice and explanatory guidance.
- Do not claim legal compliance, certification or regulator endorsement from framework alignment alone.
