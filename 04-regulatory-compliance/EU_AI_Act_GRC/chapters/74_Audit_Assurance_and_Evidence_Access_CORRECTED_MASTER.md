# Chapter 74 — Audit, Assurance, and Evidence Access

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 74 draft language.

## Requirement

Organizations must obtain sufficient access to information, records, testing results, and responsible personnel to evaluate whether an AI supplier and supplied system continue to meet applicable legal, contractual, risk, security, and operational requirements.

## Plain-English explanation

An audit right that cannot be exercised, produces only marketing material, or excludes critical subcontractors is not an effective control. Evidence access should be proportionate to risk and may include direct audit, independent assurance, regulator-facing documentation, or a combination of methods.

## Assurance design

Define:

1. evidence required by system and supplier risk tier;
2. acceptable independent assurance standards and scope;
3. direct-audit rights and reasonable notice periods;
4. access to technical, security, data-governance, incident, and performance records;
5. rights to review subcontractor and hosting dependencies;
6. remediation timelines and verification;
7. emergency access following a serious incident or regulatory request;
8. confidentiality, trade-secret, and security protections;
9. escalation where evidence is incomplete, stale, inconsistent, or withheld.

## GlobalWay example

GlobalWay accepts an independent assurance report for routine controls but retains direct access rights for serious incidents, unresolved findings, regulator requests, and material model changes. Evidence gaps trigger restricted deployment until resolved.

## Control activity

Internal Audit, Compliance, Security, Procurement, and Legal must define a coordinated assurance plan for material AI suppliers. Assurance results must be mapped to specific control requirements, tracked to closure, and reflected in supplier risk ratings.

## Evidence

- assurance plan;
- contractual audit rights;
- independent reports and scope statements;
- evidence requests and responses;
- findings and remediation plans;
- closure validation;
- management escalation records;
- updated supplier risk rating.

## Audit test

Select a sample of material suppliers. Verify that assurance scope covered the relevant risks, evidence was current and complete, exceptions were tracked, remediation was independently validated where appropriate, and denied access triggered escalation.
