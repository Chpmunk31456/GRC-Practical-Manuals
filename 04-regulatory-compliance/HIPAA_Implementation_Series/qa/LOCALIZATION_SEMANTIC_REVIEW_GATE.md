# Manual 06 — Localization & Semantic Review Gate

Status: PRE-STAGED / FAIL-CLOSED

Languages: English controlled source, Spanish `es-419`, Brazilian Portuguese `pt-BR`.

Required semantic review covers current-law versus proposed-rule boundaries, covered entity/business associate terminology, protected health information (PHI), electronic protected health information (ePHI), risk analysis/risk management, administrative/physical/technical safeguards, incident/breach assessment, workforce/vendor duties, evidence, audit and remediation language.

The HIPAA Security Rule NPRM must remain clearly labeled proposed-not-current-law until an official final HHS rule changes the controlled baseline.

Reviewer record must include reviewer, date, decision, evidence, findings and remediation. Material English or legal-baseline changes reopen affected localized review. Automated translation/parity checks do not constitute legal or semantic approval.
