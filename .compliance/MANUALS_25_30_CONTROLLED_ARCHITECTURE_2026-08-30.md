# Manuals 25–30 — Controlled Downstream Architecture

This advances downstream preparation only. It does not publish any manual and does not bypass sequential release order, authoritative-source verification, localization, accessibility, provenance, workflow-security, or genuine-human substantive review where explicitly required.

## Manual 25 — ISO 22301 Business Continuity Management
Controlled boundary: distinguish ISO copyrighted requirements from project implementation guidance; preserve certification vs implementation distinctions; verify current edition/amendments before release; map context, leadership, BIA, risk, strategy, plans, exercises, evaluation, and continual improvement into a 32-chapter controlled implementation structure. Require unofficial-localization labeling and source-status recheck before publication.

## Manual 26 — Incident Response & Cyber Crisis Management
Controlled boundary: integrate NIST incident-response guidance, CISA operational guidance, crisis governance, legal/regulatory notification overlays, evidence preservation, communications, recovery, lessons learned, and executive decision rights without converting guidance into universal legal requirements. Preserve jurisdiction-specific reporting distinctions and material-change revalidation.

## Manual 27 — Data Governance & Privacy Engineering
Controlled boundary: separate governance, records/data lifecycle, data quality, classification, minimization, retention, deletion, privacy engineering, data lineage, access, sharing, PETs, accountability, and evidence architecture. Treat NIST Privacy Framework/privacy-engineering guidance and jurisdictional privacy law as separate source layers.

## Manual 28 — AI Privacy & Automated Decision Governance
Controlled boundary: separate AI privacy engineering, profiling/ADM governance, transparency, explainability, contestability, human oversight, bias/fairness, data protection impact assessment, model/data lifecycle, monitoring, and jurisdiction-specific automated-decision rules. Preserve EU/US/LATAM legal-source distinctions and avoid claiming a single universal ADM rule.

## Manual 29 — Software / AI Supply Chain and Component Assurance
Controlled boundary: cover NIST SSDF, SBOM/component provenance, secure build/release, dependency and model/component assurance, supplier governance, vulnerability management, signing/attestation, CI/CD security, AI model/data/component supply-chain risk, and incident/change control. Distinguish standards/guidance from contractual or regulatory obligations.

## Manual 30 — Enterprise GRC Integration & Crosswalks
Controlled boundary: provide an integration layer across Manuals 01–29 without asserting false equivalence between frameworks. Crosswalks must identify source, version, mapping rationale, directionality, confidence, gaps, and non-equivalence. Include enterprise risk taxonomy, control library, evidence model, issue/remediation, metrics, assurance, regulatory change, policy hierarchy, and executive reporting.

## Shared release gates for 25–30
1. Current authoritative-source verification and version/effective-date record.
2. Controlled 32-chapter English master.
3. Exact English freeze before localization.
4. Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions labeled as unofficial project localizations.
5. Structure and trilingual parity QA.
6. Reproducible six-binary DOCX/PDF candidate generation.
7. Rendered/page/accessibility inspection.
8. Genuine-human substantive review wherever repository controls explicitly require it.
9. SHA-256 provenance, manifest, durable staging, workflow-security and release-package QA.
10. Predecessor published and final catalog/release-registry reconciliation.

Sequential publication remains mandatory: 25 after 24, 26 after 25, continuing through 30.
