# Manual 11 — GDPR Privacy and Data Protection Controlled Implementation

**Status:** Controlled development / training-grade successor to the repository's earlier published GDPR manual.

**Controlled source language:** English (`en`)

**Planned publication languages:** English, neutral Latin American Spanish (`es-419`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`)

## Purpose

Manual 11 provides a practical, training-grade implementation route for the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It is designed for privacy, security, legal, compliance, audit, data-governance, product, engineering, procurement, and business teams that need to translate GDPR obligations into accountable operating processes and evidence.

The historical GDPR publication in the repository remains unchanged. Manual 11 is the controlled-series successor and uses the same fail-closed release discipline as Manuals 01–10.

## Controlled legal/source baseline

- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — binding primary GDPR legal baseline;
- Regulation (EU) 2025/2518 — related binding procedural rules for GDPR enforcement;
- final EDPB guidelines and recommendations as non-binding official guidance;
- draft/public-consultation EDPB material only as source-state watch, never as final guidance.

See [source verification](./qa/SOURCE_VERIFICATION_2026-08-27.md).

## Training-grade implementation scope

Manual 11 covers:

- territorial/material scope and applicability;
- controller, processor, joint-controller, representative, and DPO roles;
- GDPR principles and accountability;
- lawful bases and special-category/criminal-offence data conditions;
- children and vulnerable-person considerations;
- transparency and privacy notices;
- data-subject rights and request operations;
- automated decision-making and profiling;
- records of processing activities and data inventory;
- privacy by design/default;
- DPIA and high-risk processing governance;
- security of processing;
- personal-data breach assessment and notification workflows;
- processor/subprocessor governance;
- international transfers and transfer-risk governance;
- retention, deletion, minimisation, and data-quality controls;
- DPO independence and governance;
- supervisory-authority engagement and enforcement readiness;
- AI, web scraping, anonymisation, pseudonymisation, blockchain, research, and other emerging-technology privacy issues;
- evidence, audit readiness, metrics, corrective action, and continuous improvement;
- scenario-based exercises and practitioner training.

## Legal and assurance boundaries

Manual 11 must not:

- claim to provide organization-specific legal advice;
- automatically determine GDPR applicability, controller/processor status, lawful basis, special-category condition, DPIA sufficiency, breach reportability, transfer legality, DPO necessity, or automated-decision legality;
- present EDPB draft consultation documents as final guidance;
- treat standards, certifications, contractual terms, or repository QA as proof of GDPR compliance;
- replace competent legal/privacy judgment or supervisory-authority decisions.

## Production standard

The manual will use the project standard: complete controlled English master, `es-419` and `pt-BR` localization drafts, implementation paths, accessible instructional graphics, source verification, technical/privacy/legal editorial review, human semantic review, DOCX/PDF generation, page-level QA, accessibility review, repository/workflow security review, manifest/checksums/provenance, exact-head reconciliation, and Final Human Release Approval.

## Release order

Manual 11 remains downstream controlled development and must not bypass Manuals 07–10 in the publication sequence.
