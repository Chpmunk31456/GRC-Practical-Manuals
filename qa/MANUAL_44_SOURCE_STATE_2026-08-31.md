# Manual 44 — NIST SSDF / SP 800-218 release-time source state

**State:** downstream prepublication source refresh.  
**Verified baseline:** **NIST SP 800-218, SSDF Version 1.1** remains final. **SP 800-218A** (July 2024) is final as the Generative AI / dual-use foundation-model community profile. **SP 800-218 Rev.1 / SSDF Version 1.2** was published as a draft on December 17, 2025 and must not be represented as final.

## Controlled boundary
- Final SSDF 1.1, final SP 800-218A, draft SSDF 1.2, NIST guidance, acquisition requirements, supply-chain controls, internal SDLC practices, and contractual obligations remain distinct.
- Draft practices may be change-watch inputs but cannot silently replace final SSDF 1.1 requirements/recommendations.
- AI-specific practices from SP 800-218A augment rather than erase the core SSDF lifecycle.

## Release requirement
Reverify NIST SSDF publications status at candidate freeze and fail closed if Rev.1/Version 1.2 becomes final before publication. Project translations must be marked unofficial even where NIST provides separate official translations of source publications.
