## 6. Workstreams

Track at minimum:

- governance and accountability;
- legal applicability and role mapping;
- inventory and classification;
- prohibited-practice controls;
- risk and fundamental-rights assessment;
- data governance;
- technical documentation and testing;
- human oversight and transparency;
- vendor and contract management;
- monitoring, incidents, and complaints;
- conformity and regulatory readiness;
- training and AI literacy;
- audit, remediation, and continuous improvement.

## 9. Programme metrics

- inventory coverage;
- systems classified;
- prohibited-use reviews completed;
- high-risk systems with current evidence;
- overdue controls and remediation;
- vendor reviews completed;
- training completion;
- incidents and complaints;
- audit findings and validated closure;
- readiness and maturity scores.

## 11. Completion criteria

The programme is not complete merely because documents exist. Completion requires demonstrated operation of controls, current evidence, accountable ownership, tested escalation, independent validation, and funded continuous improvement.
