# Appendix P — AI Contract-Clause Checklist

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master. Contracts allocate operational responsibilities and remedies but do not change statutory actor roles, remove legal duties, or make an unlawful use lawful.

## 1. Parties, service, and roles

Document legal entities, actual activities, intended purpose, system/model/version, jurisdictions, branding, modification rights, AI Act roles, data-protection roles, product roles, and reassessment triggers.

## 2. Clause families

Address as applicable:

- scope, intended purpose, approved and prohibited uses;
- actor-role and responsibility schedule;
- applicable-law and regulatory cooperation;
- technical documentation, instructions, conformity, declaration, registration, marking, and evidence delivery;
- data provenance, lawful use, privacy, training use, retention, deletion, confidentiality, and intellectual property;
- accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, accessibility, human oversight, transparency, logging, and monitoring;
- serious incidents, security/privacy events, complaints, investigation, evidence preservation, and corrective action;
- advance notice and control of model, data, feature, subprocessor, hosting, policy, and intended-purpose changes;
- substantial-modification and provider-role reassessment;
- audit, testing, assurance, regulator access, remediation, and flow-down rights;
- suspension, disablement, withdrawal, recall, transition, portability, continuity, and secure exit;
- warranties, indemnity, liability, insurance, and remedies proportionate to risk.
