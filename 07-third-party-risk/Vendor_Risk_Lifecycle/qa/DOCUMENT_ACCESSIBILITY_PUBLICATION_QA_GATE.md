# Manual 08 — Document, Accessibility & Publication QA Gate

Status: PRE-STAGED / FAIL-CLOSED

Required before publication:
- accessible DOCX and PDF candidates for every approved language;
- page-level visual QA for lifecycle workflows, due-diligence tables, supplier evidence models, fourth-party/concentration content, incident/exit procedures and figures;
- alt text/accessibility verification for all instructional graphics;
- metadata, language tags, reading order, links and bookmarks verified;
- release manifest and checksums tied to the exact reviewed commit;
- assurance boundaries preserved so questionnaires or supplier attestations are not represented as sufficient evidence by themselves.

Any substantive source, translation or supplier-risk boundary change invalidates affected artifacts and requires regeneration/re-review. Final Human Release Approval remains mandatory.
