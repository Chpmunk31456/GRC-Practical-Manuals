# Manual 21 — OT / ICS Security Pre-stage

Status: PRE-STAGED; preparation only.

## Source families to verify

Use current authoritative/public guidance from NIST SP 800-82, CISA industrial-control-system resources, ISA/IEC 62443 family references, sector-specific directives where applicable, and MITRE ATT&CK for ICS as supporting threat knowledge. Release-time version verification is mandatory.

## Controlled architecture target

Prepare a 32-chapter implementation manual spanning governance, zones/conduits, asset inventory, engineering workstations, PLC/DCS/HMI environments, remote access, identity, segmentation, monitoring, vulnerability/change management, safety interactions, incident response, backup/recovery, vendor access, supply chain, compensating controls, evidence design, maturity paths and audit readiness.

## Boundaries

Do not imply that generic IT controls alone are sufficient for operational technology. Distinguish safety, availability, engineering constraints, regulatory obligations and cybersecurity objectives. Respect copyright/licensing boundaries for IEC/ISA materials.

## Ahead-of-time deliverables

Prebuild source/version watch, chapter architecture, evidence schema, localization terminology, graphics/diagram plan, candidate-generation requirements, rendered QA requirements, checksums/provenance, and release-state prerequisites.

## Handoff rule

When Manual 20 advances into active build, Manual 21 advances immediately behind it and Manual 22 moves into pre-stage.
