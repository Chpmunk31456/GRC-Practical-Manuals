## 2. Monitoring objectives

Define objectives for:

- accuracy and reliability;
- subgroup and fairness performance;
- safety and fundamental-rights risk;
- human-oversight effectiveness;
- cybersecurity and misuse;
- transparency and complaint handling;
- vendor and model changes;
- legal and regulatory compliance.

## 4. Data collection

Document:

- production logs and telemetry;
- sampled inputs and outputs;
- override and escalation records;
- complaints and appeals;
- incidents and near misses;
- security alerts;
- vendor notices and release information;
- drift and performance tests;
- audit and assurance findings.

## 6. Trigger events

Trigger immediate review after:

- serious incident or credible harm allegation;
- significant performance degradation;
- subgroup disparity;
- material complaint trend;
- security compromise;
- vendor model or service change;
- intended-purpose or population change;
- new jurisdiction;
- legal or regulatory development;
- material audit finding.

## 7. Corrective actions

Possible responses include:

- enhanced human review;
- threshold or configuration change;
- retraining or replacement;
- scope restriction;
- user notice update;
- vendor escalation;
- temporary suspension;
- withdrawal or recall;
- regulatory notification where required.
