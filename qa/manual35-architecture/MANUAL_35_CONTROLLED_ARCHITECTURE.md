**State:** controlled architecture only; not a publication candidate.  
**Baseline:** HITRUST CSF v11.8.0, with release-time reverification of current HITRUST CSF and Assurance Program advisories.

## Chapter design requirements

Every chapter must distinguish: (a) project-authored implementation guidance, (b) public HITRUST version or program metadata, (c) proprietary or licensed HITRUST framework/assurance content that must not be reproduced without permission, (d) assessor or certification activities controlled by the official HITRUST program, and (e) organization-specific implementation choices. The manual must not imply that use of this repository, completion of a readiness activity, or passing internal checks confers HITRUST certification or validated-assessment status.

Legacy v9.x, v10.x, or earlier v11.x assumptions must not be silently reused. Any inherited guidance must be reconciled to v11.8.0 and current Assurance Program advisories before inclusion.

## Evidence-object model

Reusable evidence objects should include: scope statement; system inventory; organizational-factor record; risk-analysis record; applicability/tailoring rationale; policy index; procedure index; implementation-evidence register; sample-population record; testing-readiness checklist; corrective-action record; shared-responsibility matrix; third-party assurance record; cloud/service-provider mapping; privacy/security integration map; authoritative-source mapping; MyCSF workflow record; assessment lifecycle record; readiness findings log; assessor request/response log; quality-review record; evidence-retention index; version-transition assessment; change-impact register; certification-claim approval record; metrics dashboard; executive-review record; provenance manifest; exact-hash substantive-review record.

## Localization and review boundary

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions must preserve assessment-type distinctions, scope and applicability language, evidence expectations, certification and assessor boundaries, proprietary-content restrictions, version-transition conditions, and the fact that project translations are not HITRUST-authorized translations. Terminology and semantic review must be bound to exact frozen source identities before publication.

## Release gates

Before publication: authoritative-source and advisory reverification; proprietary-content/license boundary review; controlled-English completion; exact English freeze; controlled es-419/pt-BR localization; structure/parity QA; reproducible six-binary DOCX/PDF candidate; workflow security; release-package QA; SHA-256 provenance; durable staging; exact-hash substantive human review where required; predecessor publication through Manual 34; final catalog and work-product release-registry reconciliation.
