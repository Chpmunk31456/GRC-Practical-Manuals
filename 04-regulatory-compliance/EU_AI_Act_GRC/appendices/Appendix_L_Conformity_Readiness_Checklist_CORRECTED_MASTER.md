## 3. Conformity route and formal outputs

Confirm, as applicable:

- internal-control or notified-body route;
- product-sector conformity procedure;
- harmonized standards/common specifications used and limitations;
- Annex IV documentation;
- EU declaration of conformity;
- CE marking;
- EU database registration;
- authorised representative, importer, distributor, and product-manufacturer responsibilities;
- preservation of conformity and version evidence.
