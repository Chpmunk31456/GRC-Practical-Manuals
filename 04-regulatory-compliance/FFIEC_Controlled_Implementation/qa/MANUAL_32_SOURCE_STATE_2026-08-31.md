# Manual 32 — FFIEC release-time source state

**Verified:** 2026-08-31

## Controlled supervisory baseline

Manual 32 uses the active Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council (FFIEC) Information Technology Examination Handbook and relevant FFIEC/member-agency supervisory material as a controlled examination-guidance baseline. It does not treat the FFIEC Handbook as a standalone federal statute or as a substitute for binding statutes, regulations, agency rules/orders, or institution-specific supervisory commitments.

Release-time verified handbook/resource state includes:
- FFIEC Management booklet (revised 2015): IT governance and IT risk management within enterprise governance.
- FFIEC Information Security booklet (revised 2016): information-security risk management, security operations, assurance, and testing.
- FFIEC Business Continuity Management booklet (revised 2019): enterprise resilience, continuity, recovery, testing, and communications.
- FFIEC Architecture, Infrastructure, and Operations booklet (issued 2021): architecture/infrastructure planning, governance, operations, security, resilience, and third-party dependencies.
- FFIEC Development, Acquisition, and Maintenance booklet (issued September 2024): development/acquisition planning, SDLC, maintenance/change management, project management, supply-chain risk, security, and resilience.

## Cybersecurity Assessment Tool status

The FFIEC Cybersecurity Assessment Tool (CAT) was sunset on August 31, 2025. FFIEC stated it would not update the CAT for newer resources such as NIST CSF 2.0 and CISA Cybersecurity Performance Goals, and indicated institutions may consider other government and industry resources. Manual 32 therefore does not present CAT as a current FFIEC assessment baseline, examination program, or required methodology.

## Source-layer boundary

Every implementation statement must distinguish:
1. FFIEC interagency examination guidance;
2. binding statutes/regulations and member-agency rules or orders;
3. member-agency statements and supervisory expectations;
4. institution-specific findings, commitments, and examination conclusions; and
5. organization implementation practice.

Applicability depends on institution type, charter, regulator, services, technology, risk profile, and facts. The manual does not determine legal compliance, safety-and-soundness conclusions, supervisory ratings, or examination findings.

## Publication boundary

Publication requires controlled trilingual completion, structure/parity QA, reproducible exact candidate generation, document/accessibility/render QA, immutable SHA-256 provenance, exact-byte staging, predecessor publication through Manual 31, and final catalog/release-registry reconciliation.
