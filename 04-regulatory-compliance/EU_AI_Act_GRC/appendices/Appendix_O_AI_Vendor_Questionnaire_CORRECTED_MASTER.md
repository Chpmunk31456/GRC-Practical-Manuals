# Appendix O — AI Vendor Questionnaire

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master. Vendor responses are assertions until supported by current, scope-matched evidence. Contract labels do not determine statutory actor roles.

## 1. Vendor and service identity

Record legal entity, product/service, version, hosting, support locations, model providers, subprocessors, critical dependencies, and responsible contacts.

## 2. Role, classification, and legal scope

Request evidence for:

- provider, GPAI-provider, importer, distributor, authorised-representative, product-manufacturer, downstream-provider, and other roles;
- intended purpose and supported/prohibited uses;
- Article 5, Article 6/Annex I or III, Article 50, GPAI/systemic-risk, and sector classification;
- applicable dates and transitional rules;
- conformity, declaration, registration, marking, and authority status where applicable.

## 3. System, model, and data evidence

Request architecture, versions, limitations, evaluation, data provenance, lawful acquisition, quality, representativeness, bias, privacy, retention, customer-data training, intellectual-property, and change-control evidence.

## 4. Safety, security, oversight, and accessibility

Request threat models, adversarial tests, vulnerability management, incident history, resilience, logs, human oversight, transparency, accessibility, language coverage, complaints, appeals, fallback, and continuity evidence.

## 5. Monitoring, changes, and incidents

Define performance/drift thresholds, serious-incident process, regulator cooperation, model/service/subprocessor changes, deprecation, corrective action, and affected-version identification.
