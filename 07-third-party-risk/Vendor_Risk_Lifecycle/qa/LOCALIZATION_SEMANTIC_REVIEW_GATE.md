# Manual 08 — Localization & Semantic Review Gate

Status: PRE-STAGED / FAIL-CLOSED

Languages: English controlled source, Spanish `es-419`, Brazilian Portuguese `pt-BR`.

Required semantic review covers intake/classification, due diligence, approval, contracting, onboarding, monitoring, evidence refresh, fourth parties, AI/model/API suppliers, concentration/resilience, incidents, remediation, renewal, reassessment, offboarding, data return/deletion and exit.

Localization must preserve distinctions between supplier assertion, independent evidence, contractual obligation, customer responsibility and residual risk.

Reviewer record: reviewer, date, decision, evidence, findings, remediation. Material English or source-baseline changes reopen affected localized review. Automated translation/parity checks do not constitute semantic approval.
