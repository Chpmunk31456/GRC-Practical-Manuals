# Appendix P — AI Contract-Clause Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist when procuring, licensing, integrating, hosting, distributing, or supporting an AI system.

## 1. Parties and legal roles

- [ ] Each party’s AI-regulatory role is defined.
- [ ] Data-protection and sector roles are defined.
- [ ] Responsibility for intended purpose and substantial modification is allocated.
- [ ] Authorized representative, importer, or distributor obligations are addressed where relevant.

## 2. Scope and permitted use

- [ ] Intended purpose is precisely described.
- [ ] Prohibited and restricted uses are listed.
- [ ] Users, jurisdictions, sectors, and populations are defined.
- [ ] Customer modification and configuration rights are controlled.
- [ ] White-labeling and branding implications are addressed.

## 3. Documentation and evidence

- [ ] Technical documentation is provided and kept current.
- [ ] Validation, testing, and performance evidence is available.
- [ ] Data-governance and provenance evidence is available.
- [ ] Logging, traceability, and retention support are defined.
- [ ] Conformity, declaration, registration, and certification evidence is provided where applicable.
- [ ] Evidence can be supplied to authorities within required timeframes.

## 4. Data rights and restrictions

- [ ] Data ownership and permitted uses are defined.
- [ ] Customer data is not used for training without explicit authorization.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, return, and portability are addressed.
- [ ] Data location and cross-border transfer controls are specified.
- [ ] Confidential, personal, and sensitive data protections are included.
- [ ] Licensing and intellectual-property rights are sufficient for intended use.

## 5. Security and resilience

- [ ] Security requirements and standards are defined.
- [ ] Vulnerability and patch obligations are included.
- [ ] Incident notification deadlines and content are specified.
- [ ] Business continuity and disaster recovery are addressed.
- [ ] Logging and forensic support are required.
- [ ] Exit, transition, and service-continuity support are included.

## 6. Model and service changes

- [ ] Advance notice is required for material changes.
- [ ] Model version, provider, subprocessor, location, and feature changes are covered.
- [ ] Customer approval or termination rights are defined.
- [ ] Prior versions and evidence can be preserved where necessary.
- [ ] Deprecation and migration support are specified.

## 7. Monitoring and incidents

- [ ] Performance, drift, and subgroup monitoring duties are assigned.
- [ ] Complaint, appeal, and serious-incident cooperation is required.
- [ ] Corrective-action and root-cause support is defined.
- [ ] Suspension, restriction, recall, and withdrawal support is included.

## 8. Human oversight and transparency

- [ ] User instructions and limitations are supplied.
- [ ] Human-review, override, and escalation functionality is supported.
- [ ] Transparency notices and localization support are addressed.
- [ ] Accessibility and language requirements are included.

## 9. Audit and regulatory cooperation

- [ ] Audit rights are proportionate and enforceable.
- [ ] Independent reports and attestations are supplied.
- [ ] Regulatory inquiries, inspections, and information requests are supported.
- [ ] Evidence preservation and legal holds are supported.
- [ ] Subprocessor compliance is flowed down.

## 10. Liability and remedies

- [ ] Warranties align with documented capability and limitations.
- [ ] Indemnities address data, IP, regulatory, and third-party claims.
- [ ] Liability caps reflect material AI risk.
- [ ] Service credits do not replace meaningful remedies.
- [ ] Termination rights exist for noncompliance, serious incidents, or unsupported changes.
