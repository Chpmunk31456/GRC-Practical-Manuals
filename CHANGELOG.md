## [1.11] — 1 August 2026

### Added

- Published the GRC Program Management and Operating Model Toolkit.
- Added six controlled English chapters covering charter and mandate, governance forums, decision rights and RACI, annual planning and prioritization, portfolio and capacity management, executive operating review, maturity assessment, continuous improvement, and assurance boundaries.
- Added six editable CSV tools for program chartering, operating-model roles, annual planning, initiative portfolio management, governance decisions, and maturity assessment.
- Added Latin American Spanish (`es-419`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) machine-assisted publication candidates.
- Added authoritative-source records, automated QA evidence, visual-review records, release notes, and SHA-256 registers.

### Validated

- English structure: 7 H1 and 33 H2 headings.
- Spanish structure: 7 H1 and 33 H2 headings, with 1,458 extracted PDF words.
- Brazilian Portuguese structure: 7 H1 and 33 H2 headings, with 1,400 extracted PDF words.
- DOCX ZIP integrity, searchable-PDF text, six CSV schemas, machine-assisted candidate notices, and full-page visual review passed for the generated packages.

### Changed

- Updated the root repository catalog and the Templates and Tools index.
- Advanced the repository version from 1.10 to 1.11.

### Limitations

- Native-language editorial approval, organization-specific legal or regulatory review, professional-standards interpretation, accessibility testing, formal assurance, and independent page-by-page human approval are not represented as completed.

## [1.10] — 1 August 2026

### Added

- Published the GRC Metrics and Executive Reporting Toolkit.
- Added six controlled English chapters covering metric governance, definitions and formulas, data lineage and quality, baselines and thresholds, executive and board reporting, lifecycle management, actions, and assurance boundaries.
- Added English Markdown, Microsoft Word, and searchable PDF editions.
- Added a 40-field Metric Definition Register, a 36-field KPI/KRI Review Worksheet, a 34-field Executive GRC Scorecard, and a 28-field Metrics Action and Decision Tracker.
- Added Latin American Spanish (`es-419`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) machine-assisted publication candidates in Markdown, DOCX, and searchable PDF formats.
- Added automated assembly reports, package QA records, SHA-256 checksums, release notes, and authoritative-source revalidation records.

### Validated

- English controlled assembly, DOCX ZIP integrity, searchable-PDF text, editable-tool field counts, and checksums passed automated validation.
- English PDF: 9 pages and 2,353 extracted words.
- Spanish package: 9 PDF pages, 2,848 extracted words, and 7 H1 / 45 H2 heading parity.
- Brazilian Portuguese package: 9 PDF pages, 2,782 extracted words, and 7 H1 / 45 H2 heading parity.
- Both localized editions passed protected-token restoration, DOCX ZIP integrity, searchable-PDF, heading-parity, and checksum checks.
- NIST SP 800-55 Volumes 1 and 2 were confirmed as the current December 2024 publications replacing withdrawn Revision 1.
- ISO 31000:2018 was confirmed as the currently published edition; ISO/CD 31000 Edition 3 remains under development.

### Changed

- Updated the root repository catalog and the Templates and Tools index.
- Advanced the repository version from 1.9 to 1.10.
- Promoted the GRC Metrics and Executive Reporting Toolkit from planned work to a published Section 08 resource.
- Corrected the Section 08 version marker and completed project-status language after merge.

### Limitations

- Native-language editorial approval, organization-specific legal or regulatory review, statistical validation, accessibility testing, formal audit assurance, and full page-by-page human visual review are not represented as completed.
