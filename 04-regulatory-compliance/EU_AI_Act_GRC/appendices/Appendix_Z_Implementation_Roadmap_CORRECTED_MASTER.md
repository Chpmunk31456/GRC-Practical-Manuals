## 2. Immediate legal-date control

Before using the roadmap, identify each applicable provision, actor, system/model, application date, transition rule, conformity dependency, and responsible owner. Internal milestones must be accelerated where a binding date occurs earlier.

## 3. Phase 1 — First 30 days: establish control

Recommended actions:

- confirm governance and escalation;
- verify current legal baseline and effective dates;
- identify and restrict prohibited or unresolved uses;
- create the initial AI/GPAI inventory;
- assign actor roles, system owners, and risk owners;
- triage high-risk, transparency, GPAI, privacy, security, and sector exposure;
- establish immediate incident, evidence-preservation, and legal-review processes;
- begin proportionate AI-literacy measures.

## 4. Phase 2 — Days 31–90: operationalize

Recommended actions:

- reconcile inventory and classification;
- complete priority applicability, role, prohibited-practice, high-risk, FRIA/DPIA, risk, and vendor reviews;
- establish control and evidence registers;
- approve human oversight, transparency, monitoring, change, incident, complaint, and corrective-action processes;
- remediate urgent documentation, security, data, accessibility, and contract gaps.

## 5. Phase 3 — Months 4–6: stabilize

Recommended actions:

- implement applicable high-risk and GPAI control baselines;
- complete priority technical documentation and validation;
- test change, continuity, serious-incident, and regulatory-response workflows;
- begin independent assurance;
- close critical findings and validate remediation.

## 6. Phase 4 — Months 7–12: mature

Recommended actions:

- scale across entities and jurisdictions;
- improve reliable monitoring and evidence collection;
- complete applicable conformity-readiness work;
- expand audit and operating-effectiveness testing;
- conduct regulatory-response simulations;
- approve the next roadmap and funded continuous-improvement plan.

## 8. Completion rule

Programme completion requires current legal mapping, accountable ownership, operating controls, version-linked evidence, tested escalation, validated remediation, and continuous monitoring. Documents or maturity scores alone do not establish legal compliance.
