# Manual 36 — Brazil LGPD Controlled Architecture

**State:** controlled architecture only; not a publication candidate.  
**Baseline:** Lei nº 13.709/2018 (LGPD) as currently in force, with current ANPD regulations and release-time reverification of the consolidated statute, binding ANPD instruments, applicable transition provisions, and explicitly claimed sector overlays.  
**Boundary:** LGPD statutory requirements, binding ANPD regulations, ANPD guidance/FAQs, adjacent Brazilian digital-law or sector obligations, contractual duties, and organization-specific implementation practices must remain distinct.

## Chapter design requirements

Every chapter must identify, as applicable: purpose; applicability; controlling source layer; accountable role; implementable procedure; operating trigger or frequency; required evidence object; evidence location; review/test method; exception/remediation path; reassessment trigger; and any decision that requires qualified legal, privacy, localization, accessibility, or specialist judgment.

The controlled English master must not present ANPD guidance or FAQ language as statutory text, silently fold sector or adjacent digital-law requirements into the LGPD, or state blanket jurisdictional conclusions without applicability analysis. The 2026 ANPD regulatory-agency status is a governance/regulator fact and must not be represented as an amendment that rewrites the LGPD baseline.

## Evidence-object model

Reusable evidence objects should include: applicability assessment; data-processing inventory; role/responsibility matrix; lawful-basis register; consent record; legitimate-interest assessment; children/adolescent processing assessment; data-subject request log; notice inventory; retention/deletion schedule; privacy-by-design checklist; security-control mapping; incident assessment and notification record; privacy-impact/risk assessment; international-transfer assessment; vendor/operator register; data-processing agreement checklist; subprocessor record; shared-responsibility matrix; cloud-processing inventory; automated-decision/profiling assessment; workforce privacy record; training evidence; physical-record inventory; small-agent applicability assessment; ANPD obligation register; sector-overlay matrix; evidence index; exception/remediation log; management dashboard; regulatory-change record; provenance manifest; exact-hash substantive-review record.

## Localization and review boundary

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions must preserve Brazilian legal terminology, LGPD role distinctions, lawful-basis language, data-subject-rights meaning, incident and international-transfer conditions, ANPD regulatory/guidance boundaries, sector-overlay distinctions, and organization-specific implementation choices. Portuguese localization must not imply that project-authored text is official ANPD wording. Spanish and Portuguese semantic review must be tied to exact frozen source identities before publication whenever a genuine non-deterministic semantic judgment is required.

## Release gates

Before publication: authoritative-source and current-law reverification; binding-regulation vs guidance boundary verification; controlled-English completion; exact English freeze; controlled es-419/pt-BR localization; deterministic structure/parity/link/metadata/accessibility QA; reproducible six-binary DOCX/PDF candidate; workflow-security and release-package QA; SHA-256 provenance; durable staging; exact-hash substantive human review only where a specific documented non-deterministic competency genuinely requires it; predecessor publication through Manual 35; and final catalog plus work-product release-registry reconciliation.

No routine or generic final human approval is added beyond the standing clean-candidate release authorization. A clean candidate with no unresolved error or issue and all applicable objective gates green advances automatically once predecessor order is satisfied, except for any specifically documented non-delegable human judgment that remains genuinely required.
