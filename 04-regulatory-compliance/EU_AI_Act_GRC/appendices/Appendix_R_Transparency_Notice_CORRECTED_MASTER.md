## 2. Module A — Human interaction with AI

State clearly and at the appropriate time that the person is interacting with an AI system, unless this is obvious to a reasonably well-informed, observant, and circumspect person in the circumstances or another statutory exception applies.

## 3. Module B — High-risk instructions and deployer information

Provide or operationalize applicable instructions, capabilities, limitations, accuracy, intended purpose, human oversight, input requirements, maintenance, logging, and other information required for the relevant provider/deployer relationship.

## 4. Module C — AI-generated or manipulated content

Record provider marking capability, machine-readable format, technical feasibility, robustness, interoperability, and deployer disclosure duties. Distinguish deepfake, public-interest text, and other synthetic-content cases and document exceptions.

## 5. Module D — Emotion recognition or biometric categorisation

Inform exposed natural persons where the statutory trigger applies and coordinate with prohibited-practice, high-risk, privacy, employment, and special-category-data assessments.

## 6. Core plain-language notice fields

- AI involvement and responsible organization;
- purpose and material decisions/actions influenced;
- information used and privacy link;
- material limitations and uncertainty;
- meaningful human review, challenge, correction, appeal, and contact routes;
- accessibility, language, and accommodation support;
- complaint and incident channels.

## 7. Quality and evidence

Test timing, visibility, clarity, accuracy, accessibility, comprehension, translation, persistence, machine readability where applicable, version linkage, and consistency with actual behavior.
