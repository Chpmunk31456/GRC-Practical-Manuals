# Chapter 127 — Regulatory Notification

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 127 draft language.

## Requirement

Organizations must identify and meet all applicable AI Act and related-law notification duties. Notification obligations differ by regulated actor, event, provision, authority, timing rule, content requirement, and legal regime; there is no single universal AI notification deadline.

## Plain-English explanation

A material AI issue may trigger notification to a market-surveillance authority, the AI Office, a competent authority, a data-protection authority, a cybersecurity authority, customers, workers, affected persons, or other bodies. The organization must determine which duty applies, who must notify, when the clock starts, what information is required, and how updates will be managed.

## Notification requirements

For each potential notification, determine:

1. the legal regime and exact trigger;
2. the regulated actor responsible;
3. the competent recipient authority or affected party;
4. the deadline and event that starts the clock;
5. required initial facts and permitted staged reporting;
6. serious-incident, safety, rights, privacy, security, conformity, or market-risk implications;
7. affected systems, models, versions, jurisdictions, suppliers, and persons;
8. evidence preservation and privilege controls;
9. consistency across parallel notifications;
10. approval authority and communication channel;
11. supplemental, corrective, and closure updates;
12. records demonstrating timeliness and completeness.

## GlobalWay example

GlobalWay receives evidence that a high-risk employee-management system may have contributed to a serious fundamental-rights impact. Legal determines the applicable AI Act actor and reporting route, assesses parallel GDPR and employment obligations, preserves logs and decision records, submits the required initial notification, and provides verified updates as the investigation develops.

## Control activity

Legal and Compliance must maintain a notification decision tree and authority register covering all jurisdictions in which material AI systems operate. Potentially reportable events must be escalated immediately, and uncertainty must not be used to delay preservation, triage, or deadline analysis.

## Evidence

- notification decision record;
- legal-trigger and actor analysis;
- authority and deadline register;
- submitted notices and acknowledgements;
- supporting evidence and chronology;
- approval and privilege records;
- supplemental updates;
- closure and lessons learned.

## Audit test

Select incidents and material compliance issues. Confirm that notification duties were assessed promptly, the correct actor and recipient were identified, deadlines were calculated accurately, submitted information was supportable, parallel notices were consistent, and updates were documented.

## Primary legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended, including applicable serious-incident, corrective-action, market-surveillance, provider, deployer, GPAI, and authority-cooperation provisions.
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 where applicable.
- Related notification duties under data-protection, cybersecurity, product-safety, employment, consumer-protection, and sector law must be assessed separately.
