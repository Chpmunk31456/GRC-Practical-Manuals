## Verified legal/source state

- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) remains in force and is the binding primary legal baseline for this manual.
- EUR-Lex identifies Regulation (EU) 2025/2518 as a related binding instrument establishing additional procedural rules relating to GDPR enforcement. Manual 11 must treat it as an enforcement-procedure development rather than rewriting substantive GDPR obligations that it does not amend.
- EDPB guidelines, recommendations, endorsed WP29 materials, templates, and other Board publications are official interpretive/implementation guidance. They are not themselves amendments to the GDPR and must remain clearly distinguished from binding legal text.
- In 2026 the EDPB continues active guidance work, including anonymisation, web scraping in the context of generative AI, scientific research, DPIA tooling, processor Binding Corporate Rules, pseudonymisation, blockchain, rights, transparency, consent, breach notification, automated decision-making/profiling, portability, and international transfer topics. Draft/public-consultation material must not be presented as final guidance.

## Release implications

- Maintain a strict distinction between binding GDPR requirements, binding related EU instruments, final EDPB guidance, draft consultation material, national supervisory-authority practice, standards, contracts, and organizational policy.
- Do not represent EDPB draft consultation documents as final guidance.
- Do not make organization-specific legal conclusions automatically. Lawful basis, legitimate-interest balancing, special-category conditions, international-transfer mechanisms, breach notification, DPIA sufficiency, DPO requirements, and automated-decision issues require context-specific competent human review.
- The manual may provide implementation workflows, evidence models, scenarios, training exercises, and controlled decision-support templates, but must not claim to provide legal advice or guarantee compliance.
- Reverify legal and guidance state at the exact final candidate head. Material legal or EDPB source-state changes reopen affected content, mappings, localization, graphics, QA, and approval.

## Assurance boundary

This record verifies source state only. It does not determine applicability to a particular organization, lawful basis, controller/processor status, breach reportability, transfer legality, DPIA adequacy, DPO necessity, legal compliance, or an enforcement outcome.
