# Manual 25 — ISO 22301 release-time source-state decision

Verified release baseline as of 2026-08-31:

- ISO 22301:2019 remains the current published second edition.
- ISO 22301:2019/Amd 1:2024 is published and applies to the 2019 edition.
- ISO/CD 22301, Edition 3, remains under development. ISO records the committee-draft lifecycle as having progressed through CD comment close and onward toward DIS registration; it is not a published replacement and remains change-watch only.
- ISO 22313:2020 remains the current published supporting guidance and was last reviewed/confirmed in 2025.
- ISO/AWI 22313, Edition 3, is under development and is not a published replacement for ISO 22313:2020.

Controlled boundaries:

- Do not reproduce copyrighted ISO normative text.
- Separate requirements, guidance, implementation examples, certification activity, internal policy, contractual/regulatory overlays, and project-authored controls.
- Localized editions are unofficial project localizations and must not be described as ISO-authorized translations.
- The 2024 climate-action amendment is part of the current published baseline and must be reflected in organizational-context and interested-party analysis without overstating its scope.
- ISO/CD 22301 Edition 3 and ISO/AWI 22313 Edition 3 are non-normative change-watch material until publication. If either becomes published before release, publication must fail closed pending impact analysis and material-change revalidation.
- Recheck ISO status again at the final publication reconciliation boundary.

This source decision supports controlled localization and publication-candidate generation. Publication state remains unchanged until all applicable localization, artifact, provenance, accessibility, workflow-security, exact-staging, predecessor-order, and final release-state gates are satisfied.
