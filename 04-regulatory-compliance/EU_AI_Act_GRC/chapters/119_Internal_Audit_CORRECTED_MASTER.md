# Chapter 119 — Internal Audit

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 119 draft language.

## Requirement

Internal audit must provide independent, risk-based assurance over AI governance, legal compliance, control design, operating effectiveness, evidence quality, issue management, and management reporting. Internal audit does not replace conformity assessment, notified-body review, competent-authority oversight, or management accountability.

## Plain-English explanation

Internal audit should test whether the AI control environment actually works, not merely whether policies exist. Scope and sampling must reflect legal role, system risk, jurisdiction, lifecycle stage, supplier dependency, incidents, complaints, and prior findings.

## Audit requirements

Define:

1. independent mandate and unrestricted evidence access;
2. competent multidisciplinary staffing;
3. risk-based universe and annual plan;
4. system, model, version, actor, and jurisdiction coverage;
5. design and operating-effectiveness methods;
6. technical validation and specialist support;
7. issue grading, escalation, and management response;
8. remediation tracking and independent closure validation;
9. reporting to appropriate governance bodies;
10. quality assurance and follow-up.

## GlobalWay example

GlobalWay Internal Audit samples high-risk recruitment, traveler-assistance, and fraud systems, testing classification, vendor controls, human oversight, logging, monitoring, incidents, and corrective-action closure.

## Control activity

The audit committee approves a risk-based AI audit plan and receives significant findings, overdue remediation, accepted residual risk, and recurring control-failure trends.

## Evidence

- audit charter and plan;
- competence and independence records;
- workpapers and sampling rationale;
- findings and reports;
- management responses;
- remediation and closure validation;
- committee reporting.

## Audit test

Review the internal-audit programme. Confirm independence, risk coverage, evidence sufficiency, appropriate technical competence, issue escalation, and objective validation before closure.

## Primary legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended: applicable governance, quality-management, documentation, monitoring, corrective-action, and authority-access provisions.
- Internal audit standards are non-binding unless incorporated through another binding requirement.
