## Cross-cutting findings

1. All six appendices have a single identified source and no competing corrected master was found.
2. Appendices U–Y can remain canonical after legal-status and modality headers are added.
3. Appendix Z requires a corrected master because its phase headings could be mistaken for statutory implementation deadlines without a prominent non-statutory-roadmap statement and current legal-date register.
4. Control and evidence registers must support article-, actor-, version-, jurisdiction-, retention-, test-, and source-level traceability.
5. Internal audit, board reporting, maturity, assurance, and programme management practices must not be presented as universal statutory duties.
6. Corrective-action records must distinguish internal issue closure from legally required corrective measures, withdrawal, recall, reporting, notification, and authority cooperation.
7. No deletion is authorized by this audit.
