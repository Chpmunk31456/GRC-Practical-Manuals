## 2. Dataset and lifecycle use

Record training, validation, testing, fine-tuning, retrieval, operational input, feedback, monitoring, and synthetic-data uses separately.

## 3. Article 10 and governance criteria

Assess, as applicable:

- relevant design choices;
- data collection and origin;
- preparation, annotation, labeling, cleaning, enrichment, and aggregation;
- assumptions about what the data measure and represent;
- availability, quantity, suitability, and required characteristics;
- bias that may affect health, safety, fundamental rights, or prohibited discrimination;
- measures to detect, prevent, and mitigate bias;
- gaps, shortcomings, and remediation;
- representativeness for the intended population and context;
- appropriate statistical properties;
- geographic, contextual, behavioral, and functional setting;
- versioning, lineage, integrity, and reproducibility.

## 4. Lawful acquisition, rights, and privacy

Document source, licence, permissions, intellectual-property restrictions, lawful processing basis, data-subject impacts, international transfers, minimisation, retention, access, deletion, and security.

## 5. Special-category data used for bias monitoring or correction

Where special-category personal data are processed for bias monitoring, detection, or correction, record the exact legal basis, strict necessity, access limits, safeguards, pseudonymisation, deletion, documentation, and qualified privacy/legal approval. Do not treat the AI Act as a general permission to process sensitive data.
