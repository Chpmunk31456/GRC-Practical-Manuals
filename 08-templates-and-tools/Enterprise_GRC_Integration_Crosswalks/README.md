# Manual 30 — Enterprise GRC Integration & Crosswalks Controlled Implementation

Manual 30 defines a controlled enterprise method for integrating obligations, controls, risks, evidence, testing, issues, and reporting across the published GRC manual series without asserting false equivalence between different source regimes.

- `controlled/en/MANUAL_30_CONTROLLED_EN.md`

Localized editions are unofficial project translations. They do not replace authoritative source text or jurisdiction-specific interpretation.

## Publication controls

Publication requires sequential predecessor satisfaction, trilingual structural parity, deterministic candidate generation, accessibility and rendered-page QA, immutable SHA-256 provenance, exact-byte staging, and final catalog/release-registry reconciliation.
