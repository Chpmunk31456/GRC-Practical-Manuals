Describe:

- business objective and expected benefit;
- affected persons and processes;
- system boundaries and dependencies;
- model, data, infrastructure, and vendor components;
- legal classification and organizational role;
- decision criticality and human involvement;
- assumptions and known limitations.

## 4. Scenario analysis

For material risks, document:

- initiating event;
- threat, error, or failure mechanism;
- exposed asset, person, or process;
- potential consequence;
- affected population and scale;
- detectability and reversibility;
- control dependencies;
- credible worst case;
- evidence supporting the rating.

## 5. Risk rating method

Use the organization’s approved methodology. At minimum, define:

- likelihood scale;
- impact scale;
- aggregation method;
- thresholds for escalation;
- criteria for risk acceptance;
- treatment deadlines by severity.

Do not use a numerical score as a substitute for professional judgment or documented evidence.

## 6. Risk treatment

Select one or more:

- avoid or prohibit the use;
- reduce through technical controls;
- reduce through process or human oversight;
- transfer through contract or insurance;
- limit scope, population, or jurisdiction;
- pilot with enhanced monitoring;
- accept residual risk through authorized approval;
- suspend or withdraw pending remediation.

## 8. Residual risk decision

Select one:

- [ ] Acceptable within approved tolerance
- [ ] Acceptable with conditions and monitoring
- [ ] Executive risk acceptance required
- [ ] Board escalation required
- [ ] Not acceptable; remediation required
- [ ] Not acceptable; deployment prohibited or suspended

### Decision rationale

Document benefits, alternatives, control effectiveness, uncertainty, affected persons, and reasons the residual risk is or is not acceptable.

## 9. Reassessment triggers

Reassess after:

- model, data, prompt, or architecture change;
- intended-purpose or population change;
- new jurisdiction;
- material incident or complaint;
- performance or bias drift;
- vendor or contract change;
- security vulnerability;
- legal or regulatory development;
- audit or validation finding;
- substantial modification.
