# Chapter 72 — AI Contracting and Compliance Clauses

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 72 draft language.

## Requirement

Contracts for AI systems and general-purpose AI models must allocate responsibilities clearly enough to support the parties’ actual legal roles, required information flows, operational controls, evidence access, incident response, change management, and exit obligations. Contract language must not misstate or contractually erase statutory duties.

## Plain-English explanation

A contract cannot convert a deployer into a provider merely by label, nor can it remove obligations imposed by law. The agreement should reflect how the system is actually developed, branded, supplied, configured, modified, monitored, and used.

## Required clause areas

Address, as applicable:

1. intended purpose and prohibited or unsupported uses;
2. each party’s AI Act role and responsibility boundaries;
3. technical documentation, instructions, conformity, and registration support;
4. data provenance, privacy, intellectual-property, and confidentiality controls;
5. accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, accessibility, and human oversight;
6. logging, retention, monitoring, complaint, and incident cooperation;
7. regulatory inquiries, inspections, audit rights, and evidence production;
8. subcontractors and material supply-chain dependencies;
9. advance notice of model, data, functionality, hosting, or policy changes;
10. substantial-modification and provider-role reassessment;
11. corrective action, suspension, withdrawal, recall, and vulnerability handling;
12. business continuity, termination assistance, data return, deletion, and migration;
13. indemnity, liability, insurance, and remediation consistent with applicable law.

## GlobalWay example

GlobalWay’s contract for an AI recruitment platform requires the supplier to provide current instructions, classification support, material-change notices, subgroup testing evidence, incident cooperation, regulator-facing documentation, and transition assistance. GlobalWay retains the right to suspend use immediately where legal or safety thresholds are exceeded.

## Control activity

Legal and Procurement must use an approved AI contracting standard proportionate to the system’s risk and the parties’ roles. Material deviations require documented approval and compensating controls. Contracts must be reassessed after role changes, substantial modification, regulatory change, incident, or renewal.

## Evidence

- approved AI contract template;
- role and responsibility matrix;
- negotiated deviations;
- supplier evidence commitments;
- change-notification clauses;
- incident and regulatory-cooperation clauses;
- audit and assurance rights;
- exit and continuity provisions;
- approval and reassessment records.

## Audit test

Select a sample of material AI contracts. Confirm that clauses align with actual roles, support all required evidence and operational processes, include change and incident cooperation, and were reassessed after relevant triggers.
