## Modality control

| English modality | Spanish | Portuguese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| must | debe | deve | Binding duty or mandatory internal control only when source supports it. |
| should | debería | deve-se / recomenda-se | Recommended practice. |
| may | puede | pode | Permission, option, or possibility. |
| recommended | recomendado | recomendado | Non-binding practice. |
| optional enhancement | mejora opcional | melhoria opcional | Explicitly non-mandatory. |
