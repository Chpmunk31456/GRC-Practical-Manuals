# Manual 47 — EU AI Act Practical Scenarios

**Controlled language:** English

## Scenario 1 — Employment screening AI

Determine territorial scope, value-chain roles, high-risk classification, data-governance requirements, human oversight, logging, validation, deployer controls, fundamental-rights assessment obligations where applicable, monitoring and evidence.

## Scenario 2 — Customer-service chatbot

Determine whether Article 50 transparency duties apply, what disclosure is required, how production evidence demonstrates the disclosure operates, and which privacy/security controls remain outside the AI Act but are still necessary.

## Scenario 3 — Third-party high-risk AI procurement

Separate provider and deployer obligations. Identify documentation, instructions for use, logging, monitoring, change notification, incident notification, contractual evidence, oversight and exit requirements.

## Scenario 4 — GPAI-enabled internal application

Determine the organization's role, downstream reliance on GPAI provider information, model limitations, copyright/transparency considerations, system-level risk assessment, security and change management.

## Scenario 5 — Deepfake/public-interest content

Determine whether Article 50 labeling/disclosure duties apply, how the disclosure is implemented and tested, and what evidence must be retained.

## Scenario 6 — Material provider/model change

Assess whether role, classification, intended purpose, performance, cybersecurity, transparency or conformity-related conclusions are affected. Require regression testing, documented reassessment, rollback planning and renewed approval where material.

## Scenario 7 — AI incident after deployment

Contain the event, preserve evidence, assess harm/seriousness, determine notification/escalation obligations, coordinate provider/deployer responsibilities, remediate and revalidate before resuming use.

## Scenario 8 — Agentic AI with consequential tool access

Apply the EU AI Act classification and transparency analysis at system level while also governing identity, authorization, human approval checkpoints, action logs, tool limits, runtime monitoring and emergency disablement.

## Scenario-response template

For every scenario document:

1. AI use and intended purpose.
2. Territorial/material scope.
3. Value-chain role(s).
4. Prohibited-practice screen.
5. Risk/GPAI/transparency classification.
6. Applicable obligations.
7. Control objectives and control activities.
8. Required evidence.
9. Approval/exception authority.
10. Monitoring, change and incident triggers.
11. Residual-risk decision.
