# Audit Readiness and Remediation Management Practical Manual

A practical manual and toolkit for preparing organizations for internal and external audits, identifying control gaps, managing findings, assigning corrective actions, validating remediation, and sustaining readiness between assessment cycles.

## Controlled scope

This manual covers readiness governance, control ownership, scope definition, pre-assessment reviews, evidence coordination, gap analysis, finding classification, root-cause analysis, corrective-action planning, risk acceptance, validation, closure, reporting, escalation, and continuous readiness.
