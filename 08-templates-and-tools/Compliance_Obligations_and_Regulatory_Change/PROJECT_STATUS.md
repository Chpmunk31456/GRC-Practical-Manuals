## Release safeguards

- No legal conclusion will be represented as automated or definitive.
- Every obligation record will retain source, version, jurisdiction, applicability rationale, owner, implementation status, evidence, and review date.
- Regulatory changes will retain publication, adoption, effective, transition, and enforcement dates separately.
- Superseded, withdrawn, stayed, delayed, amended, and draft instruments will not be treated as currently effective without verification.
- A register entry will not be represented as proof of compliance or control effectiveness.
- Native-language, legal, regulatory, accessibility, and full page-by-page human review limitations will remain explicit.
