Reuse original repository implementation material while rebuilding Manual 16 against the current ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd 1:2024 and ISO/IEC 27002:2022 source-state boundaries. ## Controlled interpretation boundaries

- ISO/IEC 27001 contains ISMS requirements; ISO/IEC 27002 provides control implementation guidance.
- The manual must not reproduce copyrighted ISO/IEC clauses, Annex A control text, tables or proprietary explanatory material beyond legally permissible identifiers and brief quotations.
- Certification and conformity-assessment judgments remain with qualified accredited parties; implementation guidance must not claim certification or ISO endorsement.
- Statement of Applicability decisions must remain risk- and context-based; no crosswalk may imply that all Annex A controls are automatically mandatory in every environment.
- The 2024 climate-action amendment must be considered in organizational-context and interested-party analysis without copying protected amendment text.

## Migration acceptance criteria

Each controlled chapter must identify, where applicable: purpose; context/applicability; risk or management-system objective; accountable owner; implementation procedure; operating frequency or trigger; evidence object; evidence source/location; review/test approach; exception/remediation path; and reassessment trigger.

The migration must preserve useful original implementation material but revalidate every authoritative claim. Any legacy text that appears to quote or closely paraphrase protected ISO/IEC wording requires copyright/editorial review before reuse.

## Localization and artifact rule

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions must derive from the exact final English controlled source, not from legacy localized artifacts. Human semantic review remains mandatory where repository policy explicitly requires it. Candidate DOCX/PDF files must be generated from exact controlled sources, pass rendered accessibility/visual/content QA, be SHA-256 bound, and be durably staged without regeneration after hash binding.
