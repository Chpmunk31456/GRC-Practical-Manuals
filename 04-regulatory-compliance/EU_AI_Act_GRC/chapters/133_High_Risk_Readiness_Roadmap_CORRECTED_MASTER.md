# Chapter 133 — High-Risk Readiness Roadmap

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This chapter is a recommended readiness roadmap. It does not replace the legally required conformity-assessment route, notified-body involvement, registration, declaration, marking, or authority oversight. It controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 133 language.

## Requirement

Organizations acting as providers or deployers of high-risk AI systems must identify the applicable legal duties and establish a risk-based readiness plan tied to the system’s role, category, intended purpose, release date, jurisdiction, and applicable effective date.

## Plain-English explanation

High-risk readiness is not a single checklist. Provider and deployer duties differ, conformity routes vary, and some obligations apply only to particular systems or actors. Internal readiness reviews are useful, but they cannot substitute for a required legal assessment or authority decision.

## Readiness workstreams

1. confirm legal role, intended purpose, high-risk classification, and applicable date;
2. identify provider, deployer, importer, distributor, authorised-representative, and product-law interfaces;
3. establish risk-management, quality-management, and data-governance processes;
4. complete technical documentation and version-controlled evidence;
5. validate logging, transparency, instructions for use, and human oversight;
6. test accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, safety, and foreseeable misuse;
7. determine the applicable conformity-assessment route and notified-body trigger;
8. prepare registration, EU declaration of conformity, and CE-marking evidence where applicable;
9. establish post-market monitoring, serious-incident reporting, corrective action, and authority-response processes;
10. confirm deployer duties, including monitoring, log retention, worker information, and fundamental-rights assessment where applicable.

## GlobalWay example

GlobalWay determines that an employee-allocation system may be high-risk and that placing it into service under GlobalWay’s own name could create provider obligations. It freezes release until role analysis, Annex IV documentation, testing, quality-management evidence, conformity routing, registration, and post-market controls are complete.

## Control activity

No system classified as high-risk may be placed on the market or put into service until accountable Legal, Compliance, Risk, and Technical owners confirm that all applicable pre-release duties are satisfied and that any required external conformity process has been completed.

## Evidence

- role and high-risk classification assessment;
- effective-date analysis;
- QMS and risk-management records;
- data-governance and technical-documentation files;
- validation and test reports;
- conformity-route decision;
- notified-body records where applicable;
- registration, declaration, and marking evidence;
- post-market and incident plans;
- deployer-control evidence.

## Audit test

Select systems classified or potentially classified as high-risk. Confirm that the organization used the current legal text, identified the correct actor duties and effective dates, completed required evidence, selected the correct conformity route, and did not rely on an internal readiness review as a substitute for a statutory process.
