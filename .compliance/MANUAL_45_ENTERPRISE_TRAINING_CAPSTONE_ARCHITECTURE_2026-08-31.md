# Manual 45 — Enterprise Compliance Training Capstone — Controlled Architecture Gate

Status: active downstream prebuild. Publication remains sequential behind Manual 44.

## Controlled source boundary

Manual 45 is an original capstone that synthesizes the published manual series. It is not a new law, regulation, standard, certification, or substitute for role-specific professional training. Source exercises must preserve the exact authority and version boundaries of the underlying manual/source and must not turn illustrative scenarios into universal legal conclusions.

## Evidence and assessment model

Each exercise will identify learning objective, source/manual version, role, scenario facts, decision points, expected evidence, scoring rubric, acceptable alternate reasoning, escalation triggers, and remediation/retest criteria. Correct answers must preserve jurisdictional and source limitations rather than reward rote universalization.
