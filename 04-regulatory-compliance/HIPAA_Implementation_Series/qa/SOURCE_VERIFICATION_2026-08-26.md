# Manual 06 — HIPAA Source Verification Record

**Manual:** HIPAA Implementation and Audit  
**Verification date:** 2026-08-26  
**Controlled branch:** `build/hipaa-implementation-manual-06-2026`

## Authoritative verification

Verified against current U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), Office for Civil Rights (OCR) materials:

- The HIPAA Security Rule currently in effect remains the governing Security Rule baseline.
- HHS states that the Security Rule establishes administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for electronic protected health information (ePHI).
- The December 2024 / January 2025 HIPAA Security Rule cybersecurity rulemaking remains a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM), not current law.
- HHS expressly states that while the Department is undertaking that rulemaking, the current Security Rule remains in effect.
- The Manual 06 controlled baseline must therefore continue to separate current-law requirements from NPRM readiness planning.
- Current enforcement activity in 2026 continues to apply the existing HIPAA Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification Rules, including risk-analysis obligations.
- HHS states that the June 18, 2025 federal court order vacated most of the 2024 HIPAA Privacy Rule to Support Reproductive Health Care Privacy. The remaining Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP) modifications were not vacated and had a compliance date of February 16, 2026. Manual 06 must not represent the vacated provisions as current federal HIPAA requirements.
- HHS's revised February 2026 model Notices of Privacy Practices reflect the surviving HIPAA/42 CFR Part 2 notice changes and are a current operational reference where applicable.

## Release implications

- No basis was found to promote the Security Rule NPRM into the current-law baseline as of this verification date.
- NPRM-derived controls may be presented only as readiness/future-state practices and must be visibly labeled proposed.
- Current-law implementation guidance must remain traceable to existing HIPAA rules and authoritative HHS/OCR guidance.
- Privacy Rule implementation must account for the June 18, 2025 partial vacatur; vacated 2024 reproductive-health provisions must not be presented as current federal requirements.
- The surviving NPP changes and current HHS model notices should be considered where the organization's applicability analysis requires them.
- Any final HHS Security Rule, further judicial/regulatory change to the Privacy Rule, or material official update requires impact analysis before changing the controlled baseline, localized terminology, graphics, control mappings, or release artifacts.

## Assurance boundary

This source-verification record confirms source state only. It does not determine whether a particular organization is a covered entity or business associate, does not provide legal advice, does not determine whether a breach is reportable, and does not establish HIPAA compliance or an audit opinion.
