# Manual 34 — ISO/IEC 27701 authoritative source-state decision

## Controlled baseline

Manual 34 uses ISO/IEC 27701:2025, Edition 2, as the current controlled standards baseline. ISO lists the standard as published on 2025-10-14 and identifies ISO/IEC 27701:2019 as withdrawn. The 2025 edition is therefore the edition that must govern all source-state, architecture, terminology, provenance, and release checks for this manual.

## Scope and relationship boundary

The manual will treat ISO/IEC 27701:2025 as a privacy information management system standard covering requirements and guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving a PIMS. The current edition may be used as an independent management-system standard while remaining aligned with ISO/IEC 27001-based management-system implementations.

The manual must not silently inherit clause structures, control numbering, or dependency assumptions from the withdrawn 2019 edition. Any legacy 2019 material in the repository must be explicitly reconciled against the 2025 edition before reuse.

## Copyright and source-control boundary

ISO standards text is copyrighted. The repository may record public bibliographic facts, implementation architecture, original explanatory guidance, evidence models, crosswalk logic, and organization-authored implementation procedures, but must not reproduce protected ISO normative text beyond what is permitted by repository policy and applicable law.

No project localization may be represented as an ISO-authorized translation. Spanish es-419 and Brazilian Portuguese pt-BR editions remain project translations subject to semantic review and protected-term controls.

## Controlled architecture targets

The 32-chapter architecture will cover PIMS context and scope, leadership, privacy governance, roles and accountability, risk and opportunity management, privacy objectives, operational planning, PII controller and processor responsibilities, data-subject rights support, privacy-by-design integration, supplier/processor governance, data lifecycle controls, records and evidence, incident and breach support, monitoring and measurement, internal audit, management review, nonconformity and corrective action, continual improvement, integration with ISO/IEC 27001/27002, regulatory mapping, certification-readiness boundaries, and audit evidence.

## Release boundary

Immediately before publication, reverify ISO's current edition/status metadata and any amendments or corrigenda. Preserve certification and accreditation boundaries: this manual may support implementation and readiness but must not claim ISO certification, accredited conformity assessment, or legal compliance.

Localization, terminology review, editorial review, rendered accessibility/visual review, provenance/checksums, workflow security, reproducible exact artifacts, any required genuine-human substantive review, predecessor publication, and final catalog/release-registry reconciliation remain fail-closed.

Publication remains sequential behind Manuals 18–33.
