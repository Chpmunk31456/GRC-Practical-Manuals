## 2. Audit universe

Include:

- enterprise AI governance;
- high-risk and material AI systems;
- GPAI use and integration;
- business-unit and jurisdiction deployments;
- critical vendors and model providers;
- data and technical platforms;
- human-oversight processes;
- incident, complaint, and regulatory-response processes.

## 5. Core audit objectives

- assess governance and accountability;
- test inventory completeness and classification;
- evaluate prohibited-practice controls;
- test risk, rights, privacy, and data governance;
- assess technical documentation and version traceability;
- test accuracy, bias, robustness, security, and oversight controls;
- review vendor and contract controls;
- test monitoring, incidents, complaints, and corrective action;
- assess conformity and regulatory readiness;
- validate management reporting and board oversight.

## 6. Testing methods

Use as appropriate:

- inquiry and observation;
- document inspection;
- reperformance;
- data analytics;
- configuration review;
- sampling;
- technical validation;
- walkthroughs and simulations;
- third-party evidence review.

## 7. Independence and competence

Document auditor independence, conflicts, technical specialists, legal consultation, and quality review.
