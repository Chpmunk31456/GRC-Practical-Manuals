# AI Governance & Audit Toolkit — Usability, Accessibility & Provenance QA Gate

Status: PRE-STAGED / FAIL-CLOSED

Before publication of generated templates:
- every schema has a generated human-usable template;
- required fields remain visibly distinguishable from optional fields;
- tab order, headings, labels, instructions and reading order are accessible;
- spreadsheet/document validation does not silently alter controlled values;
- sample data are clearly marked as examples and contain no sensitive information;
- cross-links to evidence and source/criteria fields are usable;
- generated artifacts have version, source commit, checksum and release-manifest provenance;
- trilingual labels/instructions have approved semantic review;
- security review confirms formulas, macros, links or embedded content do not introduce unsafe behavior;
- usability review confirms a practitioner can complete, review, remediate and close a record without losing audit trail.

Material schema, generator, localization or template changes invalidate affected QA. Final Human Release Approval remains mandatory.
