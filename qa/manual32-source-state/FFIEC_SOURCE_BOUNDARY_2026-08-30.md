# Manual 32 — FFIEC source-state and boundary decision

## Controlled baseline

Manual 32 uses current Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council (FFIEC) Information Technology Examination Handbook materials and the member agencies' applicable cybersecurity, information-security, architecture/infrastructure, operations, development/acquisition/maintenance, business-continuity, authentication/access, outsourcing/technology-service-provider, and examination guidance as its controlled supervisory baseline.

## Source-layer boundary

The manual must distinguish FFIEC interagency examination guidance from binding statutes, regulations, agency-specific rules and orders, and institution-specific supervisory findings. FFIEC handbook material is not to be represented as a standalone federal statute or as a substitute for OCC, Federal Reserve, FDIC, NCUA, CFPB, state, or other applicable requirements.

The release process must reverify the active handbook booklets, statements, architecture/infrastructure guidance, cybersecurity resources, and member-agency overlays immediately before publication because FFIEC materials are revised over time and applicability depends on institution type, charter, regulator, services, technology, and risk profile.

## Controlled architecture targets

The 32-chapter implementation architecture will cover governance and board oversight, risk management, architecture and infrastructure, operations, development/acquisition/maintenance, cybersecurity, identity and access, authentication, data protection, logging/monitoring, vulnerability and patch management, incident response, business continuity, third-party and technology-service-provider oversight, cloud and emerging technology, change/configuration management, resilience testing, audit/examination evidence, issue remediation, metrics, regulatory mapping, and examination readiness.

## Release boundary

This record does not establish regulatory applicability, legal compliance, examination conclusions, safety-and-soundness conclusions, or an audit opinion. Localization, legal/regulatory interpretation, editorial review, rendered accessibility/visual review, provenance, workflow security, exact artifacts, and any documented genuine-human substantive review remain fail-closed before publication.

Publication remains sequential behind Manuals 18–31.
