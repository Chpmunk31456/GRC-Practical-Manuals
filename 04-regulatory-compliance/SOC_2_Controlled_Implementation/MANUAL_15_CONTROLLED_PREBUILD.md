## Authority boundaries

SOC 2 is an attestation/reporting framework, not a certification, law, regulation, or generic cybersecurity framework. Keep Trust Services Criteria, Description Criteria, attestation standards, management responsibilities, practitioner responsibilities, SOC 1, SOC 3, SOC for Cybersecurity and SOC for Supply Chain distinct.
