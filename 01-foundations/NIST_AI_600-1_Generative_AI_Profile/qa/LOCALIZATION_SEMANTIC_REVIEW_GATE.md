# Manual 04 — Localization & Semantic Review Gate

Status: PRE-STAGED / FAIL-CLOSED

Languages: English controlled source, Spanish `es-419`, Brazilian Portuguese `pt-BR`.

## Required before localized publication

- English controlled master frozen to an exact commit.
- Full structural parity across localized source blocks.
- NIST terms, risk-family names, actor/task concepts, applicability boundaries, uncertainty, stop/rollback language, and assurance disclaimers reviewed for semantic equivalence.
- Graphics labels, accessible explanations, captions and alt text localized consistently.
- No localized text may imply that NIST AI 600-1 is mandatory law, certification criteria, or universally applicable.
- Reviewer records must include reviewer, date, decision, evidence, findings and remediation.
- Any material English change reopens affected localization review.

## Decision

Allowed values: APPROVED / APPROVED WITH REMEDIATION / REJECTED / NOT REVIEWED.

Automated parity or grammar QA does not constitute semantic approval.
