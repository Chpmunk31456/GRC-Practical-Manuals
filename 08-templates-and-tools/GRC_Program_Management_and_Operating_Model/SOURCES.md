# Authoritative Source Register

**Validated:** 1 August 2026  
**Status:** core baseline verified against official publisher pages

## Approved primary sources

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- Title: *The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0*
- Publication date: 26 February 2024
- Intended use: governance outcomes, roles, policy, oversight, risk strategy, supply-chain risk, and organizational context
- Official location: NIST publication pages
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

### ISO 37301:2021 and Amendment 1:2024

- Publisher: International Organization for Standardization
- Title: *Compliance management systems - Requirements with guidance for use*
- Status: ISO 37301:2021 remains published; Amendment 1:2024 is published
- Intended use: compliance-management governance, responsibilities, planning, operation, evaluation, and improvement
- Official location: ISO publication pages
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

### Global Internal Audit Standards

- Publisher: The Institute of Internal Auditors
- Issued: 9 January 2024
- Effective: 9 January 2025
- Intended use: governance, independence, strategy, resource management, performance, communication, and quality concepts; not copied as proprietary text
- Official location: The IIA standards pages
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

### ISO 31000:2018

- Publisher: International Organization for Standardization
- Title: *Risk management - Guidelines*
- Status: published current edition; a later committee draft is not treated as current
- Intended use: integration of risk management into governance, planning, decisions, monitoring, and improvement
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

## Source-control requirements

Official sources must be revalidated before material revision. Licensed or copyrighted standards are referenced conservatively; this toolkit does not reproduce their protected requirements. Organization-specific legal, regulatory, contractual, professional, and board requirements require qualified review.
