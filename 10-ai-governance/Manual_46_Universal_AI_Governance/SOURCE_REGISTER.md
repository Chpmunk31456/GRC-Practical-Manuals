## Source controls

1. Primary law and official government/regulator sources take precedence for legal obligations.
2. Standards-body material is used within copyright and licensing constraints.
3. Do not reproduce protected ISO standard text without authorization.
4. Distinguish law, standards, voluntary frameworks, codes and guidance.
5. Refresh time-sensitive legal and framework status immediately before release.
6. Framework alignment does not establish legal compliance, certification or regulator approval.
