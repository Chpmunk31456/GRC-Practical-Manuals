# Appendix O — AI Vendor Questionnaire

## Purpose

Use this questionnaire to assess an AI vendor’s governance, legal role, model and data practices, security, transparency, monitoring, incident response, and ability to provide reliable evidence.

## 2. Legal and regulatory role

- What role does the vendor consider itself to perform?
- Does the vendor place the system on the market under its name or trademark?
- Does it provide an authorized representative where required?
- What jurisdictions and sector requirements are supported?
- Has the system been classified under applicable AI regulation?
- What conformity, registration, declaration, or certification evidence is available?

## 3. Intended purpose and restrictions

- Describe intended purpose and supported use cases.
- Identify prohibited or unsupported uses.
- Identify affected populations and known limitations.
- Describe controls preventing unauthorized repurposing.

## 4. Model and technical information

- Model type, provider, and version.
- Architecture and material dependencies.
- Configuration and customization options.
- Performance metrics and limitations.
- Known failure modes.
- Release, deprecation, and change-notification process.
- Ability to preserve or reproduce prior versions.

## 5. Data governance

- Sources and provenance of training, tuning, and evaluation data.
- Lawful acquisition and licensing controls.
- Quality and representativeness methods.
- Bias and subgroup testing.
- Customer-data use for training or service improvement.
- Retention, deletion, and data-location controls.
- Synthetic-data use and labeling practices.

## 6. Security and resilience

- Secure-development practices.
- Threat modeling and testing.
- Access control and encryption.
- Vulnerability management.
- Logging and monitoring.
- Incident response and notification.
- Business continuity, disaster recovery, and exit support.
- Independent security assurance.

## 7. Human oversight and transparency

- User instructions and limitations.
- Explainability or interpretability support.
- Confidence and uncertainty information.
- Override and escalation capabilities.
- Accessibility and language support.
- Complaint and appeal support.

## 8. Monitoring and incidents

- Post-deployment monitoring approach.
- Drift and performance thresholds.
- Serious-incident criteria and notification process.
- Complaint and corrective-action process.
- Regulatory inquiry support.

## 9. Evidence and audit rights

Confirm availability of:

- technical documentation;
- validation reports;
- data-governance evidence;
- security test results;
- audit reports or attestations;
- incident records;
- change history;
- regulator-facing information;
- customer audit or inspection support.
