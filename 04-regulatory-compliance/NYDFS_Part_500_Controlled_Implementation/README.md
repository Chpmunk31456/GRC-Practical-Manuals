# Manual 31 — NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500 Controlled Implementation

Manual 31 provides a controlled implementation method for New York Department of Financial Services Cybersecurity Regulation, 23 NYCRR Part 500, using the November 1, 2023 Second Amendment as the release-time regulatory baseline.

## Controlled editions

- `controlled/en/MANUAL_31_CONTROLLED_EN.md`
- `controlled/es-419/MANUAL_31_CONTROLLED_ES-419.md`
- `controlled/pt-BR/MANUAL_31_CONTROLLED_PT-BR.md`

Localized editions are unofficial project translations. They do not replace the English regulatory text, current DFS materials, or qualified legal analysis.

## Controlled boundary

The manual distinguishes binding Part 500 requirements from DFS explanatory resources and advisories, organization-specific implementation practices, and legal determinations concerning applicability, exemptions, Class A status, notices, filings, or violations.

## Publication controls

Publication requires release-time source verification, 32-chapter trilingual structural parity, deterministic candidate generation, accessibility and rendered-page QA, immutable SHA-256 provenance, exact-byte staging, predecessor Manual 30 publication, and final catalog/release-registry reconciliation.
