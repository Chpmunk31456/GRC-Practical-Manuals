1. Read the [implementation paths for organizations of every size](./MANUAL_02_IMPLEMENTATION_PATHS.md).
2. Use the [English practical AIMS manual](./English/ISO_IEC_42001_2023_Practical_AIMS_Manual_English_v1.0.md) for detailed implementation, audit, evidence, and template guidance.
3. Use the localized implementation entry in [Latin American Spanish](./translations/es-419/source/MANUAL_02_RUTAS_DE_IMPLEMENTACION.md) or [Brazilian Portuguese](./translations/pt-BR/source/MANUAL_02_CAMINHOS_DE_IMPLEMENTACAO.md).
4. Obtain authorized copies of applicable ISO publications and confirm the current requirements with qualified professionals.

## What this manual covers

- establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system (AIMS);
- proportional Essential, Structured, and Enhanced implementation paths;
- organizational context, scope, leadership, policy, roles, and objectives;
- AI risk and impact assessment, treatment, and Statement of Applicability evidence;
- life-cycle, data, supplier, transparency, and responsible-use controls;
- monitoring, internal audit, management review, corrective action, and continual improvement;
- audit and certification readiness without promising certification; and
- practical manager and junior-analyst work products.
