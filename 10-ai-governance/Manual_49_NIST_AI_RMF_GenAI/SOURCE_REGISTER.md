## Controlled framework state — 1 September 2026

- AI RMF 1.0 remains the currently published AI RMF baseline for GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE.
- NIST explicitly states that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised; a revised final AI RMF had not replaced 1.0 in the official sources verified for this controlled drafting stage.
- NIST AI 600-1 remains the published cross-sectoral Generative AI Profile and companion resource to AI RMF 1.0.
- The AI RMF Playbook remains available for suggested actions/documentation practices; NIST states it will be updated after the AI RMF 1.0 revision.
- Draft, proposed, zero-draft, concept-note or revision-in-progress material must not be represented as a final superseding framework.

## Operating mapping

Manual 49 operationalises:

**GOVERN → MAP → MEASURE → MANAGE**

as a continuous enterprise AI-risk operating cycle, with GenAI-specific risk treatment layered through NIST AI 600-1. The training architecture includes TEVV, content/information integrity, security, privacy, human-AI configuration, third-party dependencies, RAG/source governance, monitoring, incident handling and change/revalidation.

## Release-source rules

- Use NIST primary publications and current NIST/AIRC pages as the authority for framework/profile state.
- Distinguish voluntary framework guidance from legal obligations and certification standards.
- Reverify AI RMF revision/publication status immediately before candidate freeze and again immediately before publication.
- If a revised AI RMF is published before release, stop candidate freeze and reconcile the changed scope before publication.
- Do not claim that alignment with NIST AI RMF or NIST AI 600-1 establishes legal compliance or certification.
