# Manual 38 — FERPA Controlled Implementation

Controlled implementation manual for the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), anchored to 20 U.S.C. § 1232g and 34 CFR Part 99.

## Source hierarchy
1. FERPA statute and 34 CFR Part 99.
2. Binding orders or other applicable legal authority.
3. U.S. Department of Education Student Privacy Policy Office guidance and FAQs, identified as guidance rather than regulation.
4. Applicable state student-privacy laws, contracts, grants, and sector overlays, each assessed separately.
5. Organization-specific controls and implementation choices.

## Publication boundary
The manual does not treat Department guidance as regulatory text, does not assume every institution or record is FERPA-covered, and does not make universal claims about consent exceptions, directory information, school-official arrangements, redisclosure, studies/audit exceptions, or vendor access without documented applicability analysis.

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions are project translations and are not official U.S. Department of Education translations. Publication remains sequential after Manual 37 and requires the standard exact-candidate, provenance, accessibility/render, staging, and registry-reconciliation gates.
