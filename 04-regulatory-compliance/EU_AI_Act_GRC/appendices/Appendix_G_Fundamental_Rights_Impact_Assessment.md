## 4. Impact pathways

Evaluate risks arising from:

- inaccurate or unreliable outputs;
- biased data or performance disparities;
- proxy discrimination;
- automation bias or inadequate human oversight;
- opaque reasoning or insufficient notice;
- inability to challenge or correct decisions;
- surveillance or excessive data collection;
- security compromise or misuse;
- function creep or repurposing;
- vendor or supply-chain limitations;
- language, cultural, or accessibility failures;
- cumulative effects across systems.
