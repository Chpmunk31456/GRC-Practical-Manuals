# Chapter 75 — AI Supplier Exit and Continuity

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 75 draft language.

## Requirement

Organizations must plan for suspension, replacement, termination, insolvency, regulatory restriction, or loss of support for material AI suppliers. Exit arrangements must preserve safety, legal compliance, business continuity, records, affected-person protections, and required evidence.

## Plain-English explanation

AI dependency can become a concentration and continuity risk. A supplier may discontinue a model, change terms, lose regulatory standing, suffer a severe incident, or become unable to support the system. Exit planning must exist before the organization becomes operationally trapped.

## Exit-plan elements

Address:

1. critical services, decisions, integrations, and affected populations;
2. minimum continuity period and fallback process;
3. safe suspension and manual alternatives;
4. data, logs, configurations, prompts, documentation, and evidence export;
5. model or system replacement and validation;
6. regulatory, employee, customer, and affected-person communications;
7. retention and deletion responsibilities;
8. subcontractor and hosting disengagement;
9. intellectual-property and licensing constraints;
10. migration testing, rollback, and post-transition monitoring;
11. ownership, funding, and exercise frequency.

## GlobalWay example

GlobalWay maintains a manual recruitment-review fallback, export procedures for decision and audit records, a tested replacement pathway, and contractually defined transition assistance. The system can be suspended without losing legally required evidence.

## Control activity

Business Continuity, Technology, Legal, Compliance, Procurement, and the business owner must approve an exit plan before a material supplier enters production. High-impact plans must be tested periodically and after major architecture or supplier changes.

## Evidence

- supplier exit plan;
- business-impact analysis;
- fallback procedures;
- data and evidence export specification;
- replacement validation plan;
- transition-assistance clauses;
- exercise results and remediation;
- termination and deletion confirmations.

## Audit test

Select a sample of critical AI suppliers. Confirm that exit plans are current, executable, tested, and capable of preserving operations and required evidence without continuing an unsafe or noncompliant system.
