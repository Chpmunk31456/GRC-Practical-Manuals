# Manual 55 — AI Third-Party, Vendor & Supply-Chain Governance

**Controlled development lane**  
**Current stage:** Stage 1 — source qualification, lifecycle control mapping, evidence architecture, and scenario design  
**Date:** 2 September 2026

## Scope
Manual 55 covers governance of AI providers, vendors, hosted models, foundation-model services, agents, plugins, connectors, MCP servers, tool providers, data suppliers, subprocessors, fourth parties, and other AI supply-chain dependencies.

## Current controlled architecture
The manual now defines TP-01 through TP-18 covering provider inventory/materiality, due diligence, security, privacy/data-use restrictions, model/version transparency, subprocessors/fourth parties, hosting/residency, identity/delegated action, supply-chain integrity, contract/evidence rights, provider-claim challenge, change/revalidation, incident coordination, concentration risk, continuity/exit, monitoring, exceptions, and post-exit assurance.

The Stage-1 evidence catalogue and ten-scenario pack are recorded in `SOURCE_CONTROL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Source-status discipline
Legal requirements, contractual obligations, supervisory expectations, voluntary frameworks, and community guidance must remain explicitly distinguished. Supplier claims are not treated as independently verified evidence unless substantiated.

## Relationship to prior manuals
Manual 51 provides agent identity, authorization and action-boundary controls. Manual 52 provides adversarial security and supply-chain threat controls. Manual 53 provides privacy/data-governance requirements. Manual 54 provides independent model/system validation. Manual 55 consolidates those dependencies into the third-party lifecycle and contract/evidence model rather than duplicating them.
