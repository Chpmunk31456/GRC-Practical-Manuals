## Current-law verification

The following points were checked against current official HHS materials:

- The December 2024 HIPAA Security Rule rulemaking remains a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking; the current Security Rule remains in effect.
- The proposal was published in the Federal Register on January 6, 2025. Proposed requirements must not be described as final law.
- On June 18, 2025, a federal district court vacated most of the 2024 HIPAA reproductive-health privacy amendments.
- The court left undisturbed the remaining Notice of Privacy Practices modifications, with compliance required by February 16, 2026.
- The 2024 final rule aligning 42 CFR Part 2 more closely with HIPAA became effective April 16, 2024, with compliance required by February 16, 2026.
- OCR now administers and enforces Part 2, including complaint and breach-reporting processes applicable from February 16, 2026.
- HHS online-tracking guidance remains limited by the June 20, 2024 court order concerning an IP address combined with a visit to an unauthenticated public webpage addressing health conditions or providers.
