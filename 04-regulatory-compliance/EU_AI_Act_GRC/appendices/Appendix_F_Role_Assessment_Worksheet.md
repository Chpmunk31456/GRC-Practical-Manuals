## 4. Contractual and operational reality

Document:

- who controls intended purpose;
- who controls model selection and configuration;
- who supplies training, tuning, or retrieval data;
- who controls user access;
- who monitors performance and incidents;
- who can suspend, recall, or withdraw the system;
- who communicates with authorities and affected persons;
- who retains required evidence.

Do not rely solely on contractual labels when actual conduct indicates a different role.

## 7. Review triggers

Reassess after:

- rebranding or white-labeling;
- substantial modification;
- intended-purpose change;
- new legal entity or jurisdiction;
- acquisition or restructuring;
- vendor or contract change;
- new integration into a regulated product;
- change in operational control.
