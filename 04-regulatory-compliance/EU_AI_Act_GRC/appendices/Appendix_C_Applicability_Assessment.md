# Appendix C — Applicability Assessment

## Purpose

Use this worksheet to determine whether the EU AI Act applies to an organization, system, model, activity, or deployment. Record legal analysis separately where interpretation is uncertain.

## 2. Territorial scope screening

Answer Yes, No, or Uncertain.

- Is the organization established or located in the EU?
- Is the AI system or GPAI model placed on the EU market?
- Is the system put into service or used in the EU?
- Is an importer, distributor, authorized representative, or product manufacturer located in the EU?
- Is the output produced outside the EU used in the EU?
- Are people in the EU affected by the system or its output?

## 3. Subject-matter screening

- Does the capability meet the applicable definition of an AI system?
- Is a general-purpose AI model involved?
- Is the activity research, development, testing, military, national security, personal non-professional use, open-source distribution, or another potentially excluded or specially treated activity?
- Is the system part of a regulated product or safety component?
- Is the activity covered by sector-specific EU law in addition to the AI Act?

## 4. Role assessment

Identify all potentially applicable roles:

- Provider
- Deployer
- Importer
- Distributor
- Product manufacturer
- Authorized representative
- GPAI model provider
- Downstream provider
- Other contractual or sector role

For each role, document:

- entity name;
- factual basis;
- contractual allocation;
- obligations triggered;
- evidence source;
- unresolved interpretation.

## 5. Risk and obligation screening

Determine whether the system may involve:

- a prohibited practice;
- a high-risk system under product-safety or standalone categories;
- transparency obligations;
- emotion recognition or biometric categorization;
- synthetic or manipulated content;
- GPAI obligations;
- GPAI systemic-risk obligations;
- registration, conformity, post-market, incident, or documentation duties.

## 6. Applicability outcome

Select one:

- In scope
- Partially in scope
- Out of scope
- Exempt or specially treated
- Uncertain — legal review required

## 7. Required actions

- Complete prohibited-practice screening.
- Complete role and high-risk classification.
- Identify effective dates and transition periods.
- Map applicable articles to controls and evidence.
- Identify competent authorities and notification routes.
- Reassess after material change, new jurisdiction, repurposing, or supplier change.
