# Manual 26 — Incident Response & Cyber Crisis Management Controlled Implementation

This directory contains the controlled-build source and eventual publication package for Manual 26.

Primary implementation baseline: NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, final April 2025, which supersedes Rev. 2 and aligns incident response with NIST CSF 2.0. NIST guidance, CISA guidance, jurisdiction-specific legal and regulatory notification duties, contractual obligations, insurer requirements, sector overlays, law-enforcement coordination, and internal policy remain distinct source layers and are not generalized into a single universal reporting rule.

Operational incident severity is kept distinct from legal or regulatory reportability. Evidence preservation, chain-of-custody, privacy/legal coordination, crisis governance, communications, recovery, third-party response, and OT/ICS safety constraints are handled according to the facts and controlling requirements.

Controlled publication languages are English, Spanish (es-419), and Portuguese (pt-BR). Project translations are unofficial unless separately documented otherwise. Publication is governed by the repository canonical release authorization rule and fail-closed source, applicability, localization, rendering, accessibility, integrity, provenance, workflow-security, substantive-review, and predecessor-order controls.
