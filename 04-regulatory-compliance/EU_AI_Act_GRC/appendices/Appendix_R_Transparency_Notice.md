## 2. Core notice

### AI involvement

You are interacting with, or a decision affecting you is supported by, an artificial-intelligence system.

### Purpose

Explain in plain language:

- what the system does;
- why it is being used;
- which decisions or recommendations it supports;
- whether a human makes the final decision.

### Information used

Describe the main categories of information used and identify where additional privacy information is available.

### Limitations

Explain material limitations, including possible errors, uncertainty, language or accessibility limitations, and situations in which the system should not be relied upon.

### Human review

State:

- whether human review is available or required;
- how to request review;
- how to challenge or correct an outcome;
- how to contact a person rather than an automated service.

### Complaints and appeals

Provide clear channels for questions, complaints, appeals, and accessibility support.

## 4. Notice-quality checklist

- [ ] Provided before or at the time it is needed.
- [ ] Clear, concise, and understandable.
- [ ] Accessible to persons with disabilities.
- [ ] Available in relevant languages.
- [ ] Consistent with actual system behavior.
- [ ] Does not overstate accuracy or capability.
- [ ] Explains meaningful human involvement accurately.
- [ ] Provides functioning complaint and appeal channels.
- [ ] Version controlled and linked to the deployed system.
