# GRC Policy Template Toolkit

A practical toolkit for creating, approving, publishing, maintaining, and evidencing organizational security, privacy, resilience, technology, and third-party risk policies.

## Controlled scope

The toolkit covers policy governance, document hierarchy, ownership, approval, exceptions, review cycles, legal and contractual adaptation, implementation evidence, and ten editable starter policies:

1. Information Security
2. Access Control
3. Acceptable Use
4. Incident Response
5. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery
6. Third-Party and Vendor Risk
7. Data Protection and Privacy
8. Change Management
9. Vulnerability and Patch Management
10. Logging and Monitoring

## Planned publication package

- English controlled implementation manual
- Ten editable English policy templates
- Policy register and exception tracker
- Microsoft Word and searchable PDF editions
- Latin American Spanish (`es-419`) publication candidate
- Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) publication candidate
- Automated QA reports and SHA-256 checksums

## Important notice

These templates are educational starting points. They must be reviewed and adapted for the organization’s laws, regulations, contracts, workforce rules, collective agreements, systems, data, risk appetite, business model, and operating context. They do not constitute legal, regulatory, privacy, labor, accounting, certification, or formal audit advice.
