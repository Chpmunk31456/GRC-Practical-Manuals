# Manual 37 — Colombia Data Protection / Habeas Data Release-Time Source State

**Verified:** 2026-08-31  
**Publication state:** preparation only; publication remains sequentially behind Manual 36.

## Controlled legal/regulatory baseline

Manual 37 uses Ley Estatutaria 1581 de 2012 as the general personal-data protection statutory baseline, together with applicable provisions consolidated in Decreto 1074 de 2015 and current binding instructions issued by the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC). Constitutional habeas-data/privacy rights, statutory law, decrees, binding SIC instructions/circulars, SIC guidance/enforcement material, special sectoral regimes, contractual duties, and organization-authored implementation practice remain distinct source layers.

Release-time verification preserves the following operational boundaries:

- RNBD registration obligations are applicability-dependent; they are not universal for every controller or database.
- For entities required to register databases in the RNBD, current SIC instructions include reporting security incidents through the registry within the applicable period after detection; this manual must preserve the exact current SIC timing and scope at release.
- International **transfer** and **transmission** are legally distinct concepts and require different applicability/contract/control analysis.
- Current SIC procedures include a 2026 process for handling declarations of conformity related to international personal-data transfers. The existence of a conformity procedure must not be presented as a blanket authorization for every transfer.
- Binding corporate rules and other recognized transfer mechanisms/conditions must be treated according to their actual legal status and current implementing provisions.
- Legislative or regulatory reform proposals remain change-watch material unless enacted and effective.

## Controlled implementation boundary

The manual must not turn SIC FAQs, enforcement examples, concepts, guidance, or proposed reforms into enacted law. It must avoid blanket conclusions concerning authorization/consent, RNBD applicability, breach reporting, international transfers, sector-specific data regimes, or response obligations without a documented applicability assessment.

Immediately before publication, reverify Ley 1581/2012, Decreto 1074/2015, current SIC Circular Única Title V instructions, RNBD obligations, incident-reporting requirements, transfer/transmission rules, active conformity procedures, binding corporate rules, sanctions/enforcement procedures, and any enacted reform or material sector overlay.
