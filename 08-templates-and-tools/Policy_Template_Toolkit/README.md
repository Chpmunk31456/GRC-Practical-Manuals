# GRC Policy Template Toolkit

A practical toolkit for creating, approving, publishing, maintaining, and evidencing organizational security, privacy, resilience, technology, and third-party risk policies.

## Published package

The toolkit covers policy governance, document hierarchy, ownership, approval, exceptions, review cycles, legal and contractual adaptation, implementation evidence, and ten editable starter policies:

1. Information Security
2. Access Control
3. Acceptable Use
4. Incident Response
5. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery
6. Third-Party and Vendor Risk
7. Data Protection and Privacy
8. Change Management
9. Vulnerability and Patch Management
10. Logging and Monitoring

The completed publication package includes:

- Four controlled English implementation chapters
- Ten controlled English Markdown policy templates
- Ten editable English policy-template DOCX files
- Combined English Markdown, Microsoft Word, and searchable PDF editions
- A 25-field policy register
- A 25-field policy exception tracker
- A 33-field policy adoption and review checklist
- Latin American Spanish (`es-419`) machine-assisted publication candidate in Markdown, DOCX, and searchable PDF
- Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) machine-assisted publication candidate in Markdown, DOCX, and searchable PDF
- English assembly records, automated QA reports, and SHA-256 checksums
- Localized structural QA records and SHA-256 checksums

## Validation status

Automated English package validation passed for controlled assembly, combined DOCX ZIP integrity, searchable PDF text, ten editable DOCX templates, template placeholders and drafting safeguards, and all three CSV schemas.

Both localized editions passed level-one and level-two heading parity, protected-token restoration, DOCX ZIP integrity, searchable PDF validation, page-count and extracted-word checks, and checksum generation. Each localized PDF contains 29 pages. The Spanish PDF contains 7,108 extracted words; the Brazilian Portuguese PDF contains 6,842 extracted words.

## Human-review limitations

The localized editions are machine-assisted publication candidates. Native-language editorial approval is not represented as completed.

Organization-specific legal, regulatory, privacy, labor, records, contractual, standards, accessibility, and technical review remains necessary. Automated checks do not replace assistive-technology testing or full page-by-page human visual approval.

These templates are educational starting points. They must be reviewed and adapted for the organization’s laws, regulations, contracts, workforce rules, collective agreements, systems, data, risk appetite, business model, and operating context. They do not constitute legal, regulatory, privacy, labor, accounting, certification, or formal audit advice.
