## Cross-cutting findings

1. Appendices A, B, E, G, H, I, and J should contain a standard header recording legal entity, actor role, classification, provision, application date, system/model version, jurisdiction, evidence location, reviewer, approval, and reassessment trigger.
2. Mandatory-law statements must be tied to a specific actor and provision. Internal governance requirements should use `should`, `organization requires`, or equivalent language unless a binding duty applies.
3. `Limited/minimal risk` is a practical governance label and must not be represented as a formal statutory risk category equivalent to prohibited, high-risk, transparency, or GPAI obligations.
4. Every appendix should state that current consolidated EUR-Lex text controls over older summaries and that effective dates must be verified at the time of assessment.
5. Appendices C and F are the controlling corrected masters. Their earlier drafts remain preserved pending archive-manifest creation and link validation.
6. No deletion is authorized by this audit.
