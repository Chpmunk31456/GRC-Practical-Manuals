# Manual 31 — NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500 source-state decision

## Controlled baseline

Manual 31 uses New York Department of Financial Services Cybersecurity Regulation, 23 NYCRR Part 500, as amended effective November 1, 2023, as the current regulatory baseline.

Authoritative-source layer:
- NYDFS Cybersecurity Resource Center, which identifies the November 1, 2023 second amendment as the current amended regulation and provides implementation timelines, filing guidance, FAQs, and training resources.
- NYDFS final adopted Second Amendment text to 23 NYCRR Part 500.

## Implementation boundary

The manual must distinguish binding Part 500 requirements from DFS explanatory guidance, training material, advisories, and other non-binding resources. Covered-entity applicability, exemptions, Class A status, filing obligations, incident-notification duties, and compliance dates must be determined from the regulation and current DFS materials rather than generalized cybersecurity practice.

Key controlled topics for the 32-chapter architecture include cybersecurity governance/CISO authority, cybersecurity program and policy, risk assessment, access privileges, vulnerability management, MFA, asset inventory, encryption, incident response, BCDR, third-party service-provider security, monitoring, training, notices to the superintendent, annual compliance filings, exemptions, Class A obligations, evidence, testing, remediation, and board/senior-governing-body accountability.

The regulation's phased compliance dates must be treated as dated regulatory facts. The source-state control must be rechecked before release because DFS maintains an actively updated Cybersecurity Resource Center and publishes additional cybersecurity guidance and advisories.

## Release boundary

This source-state record does not assert legal advice, covered-entity status, exemption eligibility, compliance, certification, or audit opinion. It does not satisfy localization-semantic, legal/regulatory, editorial, rendered-accessibility, provenance, artifact, workflow-security, or final publication gates.

Publication remains sequential behind Manuals 18–30.
