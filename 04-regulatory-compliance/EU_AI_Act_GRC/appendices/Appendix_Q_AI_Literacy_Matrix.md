## 2. Role matrix

| Role | Required knowledge | Required practical skills | Risk level | Training frequency | Competence evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Board and executives | AI risk, accountability, regulatory exposure, strategy | Challenge management, approve risk, oversee remediation | High | Annual and event-driven | Attendance, scenario exercise, board records |
| Business owners | Intended purpose, classification, human impact, control ownership | Approve use, manage risk, monitor outcomes | High | Annual and before ownership | Assessment and attestation |
| Developers and engineers | Data, model limitations, testing, security, documentation | Build, test, document, monitor, remediate | High | Initial and recurring | Practical test and project evidence |
| Data teams | Provenance, quality, bias, privacy, lineage | Assess and control datasets | High | Initial and recurring | Data-assessment exercise |
| Human reviewers | Limitations, automation bias, override and escalation | Challenge outputs, document decisions, protect affected persons | High | Before assignment and recurring | Scenario-based competence test |
| Procurement and vendor management | Vendor risk, roles, evidence, contract controls | Due diligence, negotiate, monitor suppliers | Medium to high | Annual | Completed vendor assessment |
| Legal and compliance | Applicability, roles, obligations, notifications, evidence | Interpret, advise, escalate, maintain traceability | High | Continuous updates | Legal review and training record |
| Privacy and security | Data protection, threat models, incidents, resilience | Assess controls, investigate, respond | High | Recurring | Exercise or test results |
| Internal audit | Control design, evidence, technical assurance concepts | Plan, sample, test, report, validate closure | High | Annual and assignment-specific | Audit workpapers and assessment |
| General users | Approved use, limitations, confidentiality, reporting | Use safely, recognize errors, report incidents | Medium | Onboarding and annual | Quiz or attestation |

## 3. Curriculum topics

- AI concepts and limitations;
- organizational policy and approved tools;
- legal and ethical responsibilities;
- risk classification and prohibited uses;
- data protection and confidentiality;
- bias, fairness, accessibility, and fundamental rights;
- human oversight and automation bias;
- cybersecurity, misuse, and prompt safety;
- incident, complaint, and escalation procedures;
- evidence and documentation duties.

## 6. Effectiveness measures

Track:

- completion and overdue rates;
- assessment scores;
- unsafe-use incidents;
- policy violations;
- reviewer override quality;
- repeated audit findings;
- employee confidence and escalation behavior;
- performance after targeted remediation.

## 7. Exception management

Document temporary exceptions, interim supervision, remediation deadlines, and approval authority.
