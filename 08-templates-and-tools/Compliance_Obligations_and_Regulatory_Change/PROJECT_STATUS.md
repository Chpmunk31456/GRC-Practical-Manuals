## Release safeguards

- No legal conclusion is represented as automated or definitive.
- Every obligation record retains source, version, jurisdiction, applicability rationale, owner, implementation status, evidence, and review date fields.
- Regulatory changes retain publication, adoption, effective, transition, and enforcement dates separately.
- Superseded, withdrawn, stayed, delayed, amended, and draft instruments are not treated as currently effective without verification.
- A register entry is not represented as proof of compliance or control effectiveness.

## Human-review limitations

Native-language editorial approval, legal or regulatory interpretation, privacy, labor, tax, sector and jurisdictional review, standards certification, formal audit assurance, assistive-technology testing, and full page-by-page human visual review are not represented as completed.
