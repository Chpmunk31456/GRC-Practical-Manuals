# Manual 06 — Document, Accessibility & Publication QA Gate

Status: PRE-STAGED / FAIL-CLOSED

Required before publication:
- accessible DOCX and PDF candidates for every approved language;
- page-level visual QA for legal notices, safeguards, tables, workflows, incident/breach examples, findings and page breaks;
- alt text/accessibility verification for instructional graphics;
- metadata, language tags, reading order, links and bookmarks verified;
- release manifest and checksums tied to the exact reviewed commit;
- current-law/proposed-rule labels preserved in every artifact;
- no artifact may imply legal advice, regulator approval or compliance certification.

Any substantive source, translation or legal-baseline change invalidates affected artifacts and requires regeneration/re-review. Final Human Release Approval remains mandatory.
