# Translation Control

The localized editions use the English controlled master as the source of truth.

## Locales

- Latin American Spanish: `es-419`
- Brazilian Portuguese: `pt-BR`

## Required controls

1. Preserve the meaning, scope, examples, warnings, roles, formulas, field names, and source attributions.
2. Do not translate identifiers, file names, formulas, framework names, publication numbers, or URLs.
3. Use professional GRC terminology appropriate to the locale rather than literal word-for-word translation.
4. Preserve heading hierarchy, lists, tables, examples, and educational-use limitations.
5. Treat `risk appetite` and `risk tolerance` as related but distinct governance concepts.
6. Distinguish control design effectiveness from operating effectiveness.
7. Preserve inherent, residual, and target risk as separate states.
8. Record automated QA separately from native-language human review.
9. Do not claim legal equivalence across jurisdictions.
10. Rebuild DOCX/PDF from the localized controlled Markdown; do not edit generated binaries as the master.

## Status labels

- `machine-assisted draft` — structurally complete but not represented as native-language approved.
- `terminology reviewed` — glossary and key terms checked.
- `publication candidate` — package and automated QA complete, with remaining human limitations stated.
