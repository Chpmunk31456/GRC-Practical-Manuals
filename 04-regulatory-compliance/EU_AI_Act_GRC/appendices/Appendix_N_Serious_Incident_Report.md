## 6. Evidence preserved

- prompts, inputs, outputs, and logs;
- human-review and override records;
- relevant data and lineage;
- code, release, and change records;
- vendor communications;
- complaints and witness information;
- system and security telemetry.

## 7. Investigation

Document:

- timeline;
- technical cause;
- data cause;
- process and governance cause;
- human factors;
- vendor contribution;
- control failures;
- whether similar systems share the weakness.
