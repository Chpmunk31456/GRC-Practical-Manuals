# Foundation Timeline and Source Register Correction — 30 July 2026

## Status

This correction controls over conflicting timeline or source-register statements in the foundation, chapters, appendices, figures, tables, translations, and publication artifacts until the corrections are incorporated directly into the canonical English master.

## Controlling legal instruments

1. Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Artificial Intelligence Act.
2. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, amending Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
3. The current consolidated EUR-Lex text.
4. Official European Commission and EU AI Office guidance, clearly identified as non-binding unless incorporated into a binding legal instrument.

## Corrected implementation timeline

| Date | Binding application milestone |
|---|---|
| 1 August 2024 | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 entered into force. |
| 2 February 2025 | Chapters I and II began applying, including Article 4 AI-literacy duties and the original Article 5 prohibited practices, subject to later amendments. |
| 2 August 2025 | Governance provisions and obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models began applying under the applicable transitional rules. |
| 27 July 2026 | Articles 102–110 began applying under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. |
| 2 August 2026 | The general application date for provisions not subject to a specific exception or later transitional date. |
| 2 December 2026 | New Article 5 intimate-content and child-sexual-abuse-material prohibitions apply. Providers and deployers of synthetic-content systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 must also comply with Article 50(2) by this date. |
| 2 August 2027 | Existing GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must comply, subject to the applicable transitional provisions. |
| 2 December 2027 | Chapter III, Sections 1–3 apply to high-risk AI systems classified under Article 6(2) and Annex III, except Article 6(5). |
| 2 August 2028 | Chapter III, Sections 1–3 apply to high-risk AI systems classified under Article 6(1) and Annex I, except Article 6(5). |
| 2 August 2030 | Providers and deployers of high-risk AI systems intended for use by public authorities must complete the specific transitional compliance steps stated in the amended regulation. |

## Required drafting rules

- Do not describe all high-risk obligations as applying from 2 August 2026.
- Distinguish Annex III high-risk systems from Annex I product-safety systems.
- Identify the 2 December 2026 Article 5 and Article 50(2) transitional rules explicitly where relevant.
- Do not describe official guidance, codes of practice, standards, or assurance methods as binding law unless the legal basis makes them binding.
- Use the phrase “Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744” where amendment-sensitive provisions are discussed.
- Reverify the current consolidated EUR-Lex text immediately before publication.
