# Manual 12 — CCPA / CPRA California Privacy Controlled Implementation

**Status:** Controlled development / training-grade California privacy manual.

**Controlled source language:** English (`en`)

**Planned publication languages:** English, neutral Latin American Spanish (`es-419`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`)

## Purpose

Manual 12 provides a practical, training-grade implementation route for the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act and implemented through current California Privacy Protection Agency regulations.

It is designed for privacy, legal, security, data governance, product, engineering, marketing, procurement, audit, risk, and business teams that must translate California privacy obligations into controlled operating processes, evidence, and review.

## Current 2026 controlled baseline

- CCPA / CPRA-amended statutory baseline;
- CPPA regulations effective January 1, 2026;
- current risk-assessment requirements;
- cybersecurity-audit requirements with phased submission/certification dates;
- ADMT requirements with compliance beginning January 1, 2027 where applicable;
- related Delete Act / DROP requirements for data brokers, kept distinct from general business obligations;
- official CPPA implementation information and FAQs classified separately from binding statutory/regulatory text.

See [source verification](./qa/SOURCE_VERIFICATION_2026-08-27.md).

## Training-grade implementation scope

Manual 12 covers:

- applicability, thresholds, exemptions, and role analysis;
- personal information and sensitive personal information;
- collection/use purpose limitations and data minimization;
- privacy policy and notice at collection;
- rights to know/access, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing, limit sensitive-PI use/disclosure, and other applicable rights;
- request verification and authorized-agent processes;
- opt-out preference signals and Global Privacy Control implementation;
- minors and opt-in requirements;
- financial incentives and price/service differences;
- service-provider, contractor, third-party, and subprocessor-like relationship governance;
- contracts and downstream restrictions;
- retention/deletion and data-quality operations;
- risk assessments effective in 2026;
- cybersecurity-audit readiness and phased deadlines;
- ADMT inventory, pre-use notice, access, opt-out, significant-decision analysis, and 2027 compliance timing;
- reasonable security and incident considerations;
- private-right-of-action awareness for qualifying security incidents;
- data-broker/Delete Act/DROP distinctions;
- recordkeeping, evidence, enforcement readiness, corrective action, and training scenarios.

## Legal and timing boundaries

Manual 12 must distinguish:

- statute from regulation;
- current effective regulation from phased future compliance dates;
- historical 2023 regulations from the updated 2026 regulatory baseline;
- general CCPA business obligations from data-broker/Delete Act/DROP obligations;
- official guidance/information from binding legal text.

It must not automatically determine applicability, exemptions, business/service-provider/contractor/third-party status, ADMT classification, risk-assessment sufficiency, cybersecurity-audit scope, consumer-request outcome, or legal compliance.
