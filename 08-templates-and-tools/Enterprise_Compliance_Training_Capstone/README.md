# Manual 45 — Enterprise Compliance Training Capstone

Manual 45 is the integration capstone for the controlled manual series. It is intentionally dependency-aware: final freeze and publication require Manuals 01–44 to be published and their release states reverified. The controlled package integrates governance, regulatory applicability, control design, evidence, assurance, incident response, supplier risk, privacy, AI governance, cloud/security engineering, and secure software development into practical enterprise training and assessment scenarios.

Publication remains sequential after Manual 44.
