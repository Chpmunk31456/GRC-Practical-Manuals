# Manual 49 — Global AI Governance Crosswalk

**Working scope:** EU AI Act × ISO/IEC 42001 × NIST AI RMF × Singapore  
**Status:** PRE-STAGE — +3 RESEARCH/INTAKE LANE

## Objective

Create an implementation-oriented crosswalk that lets an enterprise establish a common control framework once, then map regulatory and framework-specific obligations to it without falsely asserting equivalence.

## Crosswalk domains

1. Governance and accountability.
2. AI inventory and scope.
3. Roles and value-chain responsibilities.
4. AI classification and risk tiering.
5. Risk and impact assessment.
6. Human/fundamental-rights considerations.
7. Data governance and provenance.
8. Privacy.
9. Security and resilience.
10. Transparency and explainability.
11. Human oversight.
12. Testing, evaluation, verification and validation.
13. Documentation and recordkeeping.
14. Approval and conformity/assurance concepts.
15. Supplier and third-party governance.
16. Monitoring and post-deployment controls.
17. Incident and escalation management.
18. Change management and revalidation.
19. Audit, management review and assurance.
20. AI literacy, competence and training.
21. Continuous improvement.
22. Agentic-AI autonomy and action controls.

## Mapping rule

Each row will distinguish:

**Common control objective → EU legal obligation/status → ISO management-system relationship → NIST AI RMF outcome/action relationship → Singapore governance/assurance relationship → Evidence examples → Gap/qualification note.**

No row may imply that voluntary-framework alignment automatically proves legal compliance or ISO certification.
