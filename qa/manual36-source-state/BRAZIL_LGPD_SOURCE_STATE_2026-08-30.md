## Controlled baseline

The controlled statutory baseline is **Lei nº 13.709, de 14 de agosto de 2018 — Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados Pessoais (LGPD)**, as currently in force. The manual must not silently substitute guidance, FAQ text, organization practice, or adjacent digital-law obligations for the LGPD itself.

The current supervisory authority is the **Agência Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD)**. Official ANPD institutional materials reflect its 2026 status as a federal regulatory agency following Lei nº 15.352, de 25 de fevereiro de 2026, with organizational structure under Decreto nº 12.881, de 18 de março de 2026. This institutional change does not rewrite the LGPD control baseline; it is a regulator/governance fact that must be separately source-layered and reverified at release.

## Source-layer boundaries

Manual 36 must distinguish, at minimum:

1. LGPD statutory requirements and definitions;
2. binding regulations/resolutions issued by the ANPD within its competence;
3. official ANPD guidance, FAQs, regulatory-agenda material, and explanatory publications;
4. other Brazilian laws and decrees that may create adjacent or sector-specific obligations;
5. sector-regulator requirements and contractual duties;
6. organization-specific implementation choices, risk decisions, procedures, and evidence.

No guidance document, FAQ, template, or project control may be presented as if it were statutory text.

## Applicability and implementation boundaries

The controlled manual must preserve jurisdiction/applicability analysis rather than make blanket claims. It must separately address controller/operator roles, lawful treatment bases, data-subject rights, security and incident governance, records/evidence, international-transfer considerations, processor/vendor governance, retention/deletion, privacy governance, and ANPD-facing obligations only where supported by the current authoritative layer.

Brazilian privacy obligations can overlap with sector rules and newer digital-law requirements. Such adjacent requirements must be explicitly identified rather than silently folded into LGPD controls.

## 2026 change watch

ANPD materials in 2026 reflect material institutional and adjacent-regulatory developments, including its transition to regulatory-agency status and additional competencies under newer digital-law instruments. Before candidate freeze and again before release, reverify:
