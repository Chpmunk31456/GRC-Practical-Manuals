# Chapter 73 — Ongoing Supplier Oversight

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 73 draft language.

## Requirement

Organizations must maintain ongoing oversight of material AI suppliers throughout the contract and system lifecycle. Oversight must verify continued compliance, performance, security, documentation quality, incident responsiveness, and change transparency rather than relying only on pre-contract due diligence.

## Plain-English explanation

An acceptable supplier can become unacceptable after a model update, ownership change, security incident, regulatory action, degraded performance, or undocumented subcontractor change. Oversight must therefore be risk-based, evidence-driven, and tied to escalation and exit rights.

## Oversight activities

The oversight program should include:

1. periodic review of role, classification, intended purpose, and supported uses;
2. monitoring of material product, model, data, hosting, and subcontractor changes;
3. review of assurance reports, certifications, testing, and regulatory communications;
4. tracking of incidents, complaints, vulnerabilities, drift, bias, and service degradation;
5. confirmation that instructions, technical information, and contact points remain current;
6. review of corrective actions and remediation commitments;
7. reassessment after merger, acquisition, financial distress, or key-person loss;
8. risk-rating updates and management escalation;
9. suspension, replacement, or exit where risk exceeds tolerance.

## GlobalWay example

GlobalWay reviews its recruitment-platform supplier quarterly. The review covers model releases, testing changes, incidents, new subprocessors, support quality, and unresolved compliance gaps. A material model update cannot be enabled until GlobalWay completes change and role reassessment.

## Control activity

Vendor Management must maintain a risk-tiered oversight calendar, evidence requirements, trigger events, escalation thresholds, and accountable owners. High-risk suppliers must receive enhanced monitoring and documented management review.

## Evidence

- supplier risk rating;
- oversight schedule;
- periodic review records;
- change notices;
- assurance and test reports;
- incident and remediation records;
- escalation decisions;
- updated risk acceptance or exit plan.

## Audit test

Select a sample of material AI suppliers and verify that oversight occurred at the required frequency, addressed actual changes and incidents, updated the risk rating, and led to documented action where thresholds were exceeded.
