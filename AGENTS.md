# Agent Instructions — GRC Practical Manuals

When creating or editing documentation in this repository:

1. Read and follow [DOCUMENT_WRITING_STANDARD.md](./DOCUMENT_WRITING_STANDARD.md).
2. Preserve the controlled-source model, authoritative citations, legal and technical meaning, publication status, control IDs, standard names, dates, versions, links, hashes, and evidence boundaries.
3. Do not perform uncontrolled repository-wide prose rewrites.
4. Improve grammar and human readability without inventing facts, requirements, metrics, certifications, legal conclusions, control effectiveness, or source support.
5. Treat English, neutral Latin American Spanish (`es-419`), and Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) as distinct controlled locales. Do not apply English-only style rules to localized editions.
6. When English is the controlled source, make substantive source edits there first and propagate approved changes through the repository's established localization workflow.
7. Do not accept automated grammar or humanization suggestions when they would alter a defined legal, regulatory, audit, cybersecurity, privacy, AI, OT/ICS, cloud, or standards term.
8. Edit source material rather than generated PDF/DOCX derivatives whenever the repository identifies a controlled source.
9. Run applicable writing, structural, source, security, accessibility, localization, document, and publication QA before representing an artifact as release-ready.
10. Keep editorial changes traceable and separate from unrelated substantive changes whenever practical.
