# Manual 39 — COPPA Controlled Implementation

Controlled implementation manual for the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506, and 16 CFR Part 312, using the FTC's current amended rule baseline.

## Source hierarchy
1. COPPA statute and current 16 CFR Part 312.
2. Binding FTC rule amendments and applicable orders.
3. FTC guidance, policy statements, FAQs, enforcement materials, and approved safe-harbor materials, identified according to their legal status.
4. Applicable state child/privacy laws, education-sector obligations, contracts, and platform terms, assessed separately.
5. Organization-specific controls and implementation choices.

## Publication boundary
The manual does not treat FTC guidance or policy statements as regulatory text, does not assume every service involving minors is child-directed, and does not treat school or EdTech use as a universal COPPA exception. Applicability, actual knowledge, parental notice/consent, disclosure, advertising, retention, security, safe-harbor reliance, and third-party obligations require documented fact-specific analysis.

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions are project translations and are not official FTC translations. Publication remains sequential after Manual 38 and requires exact-candidate, provenance, accessibility/render, staging, predecessor, workflow-security, and final registry-reconciliation gates.
