# Manual 33 — SEC Cybersecurity Governance and Disclosure source-state decision

## Controlled baseline

Manual 33 uses the SEC final rule **Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure**, Release No. 33-11216, effective September 5, 2023, together with current forms, Commission/staff guidance, and release-time official SEC materials.

The current binding disclosure architecture includes, as applicable, Form 8-K Item 1.05 for material cybersecurity incidents; Regulation S-K Item 106 for annual cybersecurity risk-management, strategy, and governance disclosure; comparable Form 6-K and Form 20-F treatment for foreign private issuers; and Inline XBRL tagging requirements.

## Release-time verification — 2026-08-31

Official SEC rulemaking records continue to list Release 33-11216 as a **Final Rule**. Current 2026 issuer filings continue to use the cybersecurity risk-management/strategy/governance disclosure taxonomy. SEC guidance also continues to distinguish mandatory Item 1.05 disclosure of incidents determined material from voluntary disclosure of incidents not yet determined material or determined not material.

## Controlled boundary

The manual must distinguish: (a) binding SEC rules and forms, (b) Commission or staff guidance, interpretations, and compliance material, (c) proposals, petitions, litigation positions, or other non-effective material, (d) enforcement/case-specific facts, and (e) issuer-specific implementation and legal judgment.

Materiality is an issuer-specific securities-law judgment. This project does not determine materiality, provide legal advice, authorize a delayed disclosure, prepare a filing on behalf of an issuer, or certify compliance.

## Key implementation facts

- A domestic registrant generally files Form 8-K Item 1.05 within four business days after determining a cybersecurity incident is material, subject to the rule's specific delayed-disclosure process for substantial national-security or public-safety risk.
- Annual disclosure under Regulation S-K Item 106 addresses cybersecurity risk-management processes, material effects or reasonably likely material effects, board oversight, and management roles.
- Foreign private issuer treatment must remain distinct under Forms 6-K and 20-F.
- Disclosure controls, evidence preservation, legal/IR/security coordination, and Inline XBRL validation are implementation controls; they do not replace securities-law judgment.
