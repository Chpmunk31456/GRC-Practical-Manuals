# Manual 36 — Brazil LGPD Release-Time Source State

**Verified:** 2026-08-31  
**Publication state:** preparation only; publication remains sequentially behind Manual 35.

## Controlled legal/regulatory baseline

Manual 36 uses Lei nº 13.709/2018 (LGPD), as currently in force, as the primary statutory baseline. Binding ANPD regulations are separate from ANPD guidance, FAQs, enforcement material, sector overlays, contractual duties, and organization-authored implementation practice.

Current ANPD regulatory materials relevant to this manual include, among others:

- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 1/2021 — inspection and administrative sanctioning process.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 2/2022 — differentiated treatment for small processing agents.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 4/2023 — administrative-sanctions dosage/application regulation.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 15/2024 — security-incident communication regulation.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 18/2024 — regulation of the encarregado/DPO function.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 19/2024 — international-transfer regulation and standard contractual clauses, currently subject to the published 18-Aug-2025 rectification.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 23/2024, as amended by Resolução CD/ANPD nº 31/2025 — ANPD 2025–2026 regulatory agenda.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 30/2025 — priority-topic map for 2026–2027.
- Resolução CD/ANPD nº 32/2026 — recognition of the European Union as providing an adequate level of personal-data protection for international-transfer purposes.

The controlled manual must not treat the existence of an adequacy decision as eliminating LGPD applicability or all other transfer, purpose, transparency, security, contractual, or sector-specific requirements.

## Release boundaries

The manual will separately control: statutory LGPD duties; binding ANPD regulations; ANPD advisory/enforcement materials; adjacent Brazilian digital, consumer, employment, financial, health or other sector law; contractual requirements; and organization-specific controls. Applicability, lawful basis, legitimate-interest balancing, children/adolescent processing, automated-decision rights, incident notification, international transfers, sanctions, and sector overlays require fact-specific analysis and must not be expressed as universal legal conclusions.

Release-time verification must recheck the consolidated LGPD text, the ANPD regulations page, amendments/rectifications, adequacy decisions, incident rules, transfer mechanisms, DPO requirements, small-agent rules, and material regulatory changes after this record.
