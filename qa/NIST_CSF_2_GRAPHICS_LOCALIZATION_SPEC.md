# NIST CSF 2.0 Graphics Localization Specification

**Status:** Controlled localization specification for Latin American Spanish (`es-419`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`).

This document defines the required translated labels, captions, alt text, filenames, and review checks for every localized figure in the NIST CSF 2.0 manual. English-language text must not remain embedded in the Spanish or Portuguese publication graphics unless it is a proper name, product name, framework identifier, or approved acronym.

## Global production requirements

- Preserve the original meaning, information hierarchy, sequence, and visual relationships.
- Preserve NIST identifiers and approved acronyms exactly: NIST, CSF, GRC, ERM, MFA, RACI.
- Use sentence case unless the source graphic intentionally uses all capitals for the six CSF Functions.
- Ensure sufficient contrast and readable type at normal DOCX/PDF viewing size.
- Do not communicate meaning through color alone.
- Export each localized figure as PNG at publication resolution and retain an editable source format.
- Update the corresponding Markdown image reference, caption, and alt text.
- Visually inspect every rendered DOCX and PDF page containing a localized figure.

## Localization QA checklist

- [ ] No unintended English text remains embedded in the localized figures.
- [ ] All labels use the approved terminology standard.
- [ ] Accents, punctuation, capitalization, and line breaks are correct.
- [ ] Text is not clipped, crowded, stretched, or overlapping.
- [ ] Arrows and sequence relationships retain the source meaning.
- [ ] Captions match the localized image content.
- [ ] Alt text describes purpose and relationships, not decorative details.
- [ ] Figures remain understandable in grayscale.
- [ ] Figures remain legible at normal DOCX/PDF zoom.
- [ ] Every page containing a figure is visually inspected after regeneration.

## Publication rule

The Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese NIST CSF 2.0 editions must not be marked publication-ready until all localized figures are inserted, the DOCX/PDF packages are regenerated, and the visual/accessibility QA checklist is documented as complete.
