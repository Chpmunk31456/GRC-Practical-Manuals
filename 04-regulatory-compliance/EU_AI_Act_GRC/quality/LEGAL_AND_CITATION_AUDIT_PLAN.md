# EU AI Act Manual — Legal and Citation Audit Plan

## Purpose

This plan governs the final legal, regulatory, and citation review of the English master before translation or publication.

## Review principles

- Use the official consolidated text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as the primary legal source.
- Prefer European Commission, EUR-Lex, AI Office, EDPB, EDPS, ENISA, and competent-authority materials where relevant.
- Distinguish binding legal requirements from guidance, standards, industry practice, and recommended controls.
- Avoid presenting unresolved interpretation as settled law.
- Verify every article number, annex reference, deadline, threshold, retention period, penalty statement, and legal-role attribution.
- Record the source, access date, reviewer conclusion, and corrective action for every material issue.

## Audit sequence

1. Verify scope, definitions, territorial reach, exclusions, and legal roles.
2. Verify prohibited practices and exceptions.
3. Verify high-risk classification routes and Annex III references.
4. Verify provider, deployer, importer, distributor, authorized-representative, and product-manufacturer duties.
5. Verify GPAI and systemic-risk obligations.
6. Verify transparency obligations.
7. Verify conformity assessment, registration, CE marking, declarations, and quality-management requirements.
8. Verify post-market monitoring, serious incidents, corrective actions, and authority cooperation.
9. Verify governance, enforcement, fines, transitional measures, and effective dates.
10. Verify all appendices, checklists, templates, graphics, captions, and alt text against the reviewed conclusions.

## High-risk review items

- Article 5 prohibited-practice wording and exceptions.
- Article 6 classification logic and Annex I/Annex III interaction.
- Article 10 data and data-governance obligations.
- Articles 11–15 technical documentation, logging, transparency to deployers, human oversight, accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity.
- Articles 16–27 role-specific obligations.
- Articles 43–49 conformity assessment, certificates, registration, declarations, and CE marking.
- Articles 50–56 transparency and GPAI duties.
- Articles 72–73 post-market monitoring and serious-incident reporting.
- Articles 99–101 penalties and fines.
- Article 113 entry into force and phased applicability.

## Citation quality gates

A statement passes only when:

- the cited source supports the precise proposition;
- the citation points to the correct article, annex, recital, or official section;
- dates and deadlines are current and contextually qualified;
- exceptions and conditions are not omitted;
- the prose does not overstate guidance as law;
- paraphrasing preserves legal meaning;
- translations will not introduce a broader or narrower obligation.

## Known graphics issues requiring correction

- Chapter 83 data-governance graphic must reference Article 10, not Article 17.
- Duplicated human-oversight graphics must be consolidated.
- Any graphic asserting a universal ten-year retention period must be corrected or qualified.
- Mixed-language footer text and typographic defects must be removed.
- All graphic article references must be checked against the final legal audit.

## Completion criteria

The legal audit is complete only when every chapter and appendix has a recorded disposition, every material issue is corrected or explicitly flagged, and the English master is frozen for translation.
