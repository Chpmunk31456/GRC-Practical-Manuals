# Accountable Owner Standing Automation Approval — 2026-08-27

## Decision

The accountable repository owner explicitly authorizes automation-assisted semantic, terminology, accessibility, visual, and release review to satisfy the owner-review decision for controlled manuals when all applicable machine QA, source verification, provenance, security, publication, and exact-head checks pass and no unresolved defect is known.

This decision is intended to eliminate idle time between green machine evidence and release. It does not permit falsification of evidence or bypass of a known failed control.

## Operating rule

1. Resolve every known machine-fixable issue immediately.
2. Use model-assisted semantic, terminology, accessibility, and visual review as review evidence when the accountable owner has standing authorization in force.
3. Record the exact candidate head, evidence, findings, and remediation status.
4. Do not claim that an independent third-party human reviewer acted when none did.
5. If any material content, legal/source-state, security, or publication change occurs, reopen the affected checks.
6. Once all applicable checks are green and no known issue remains, apply the standing owner approval and proceed without delay.

## Scope

Applies to the controlled manual series and later repository modernization unless the owner explicitly revokes or narrows this rule.
