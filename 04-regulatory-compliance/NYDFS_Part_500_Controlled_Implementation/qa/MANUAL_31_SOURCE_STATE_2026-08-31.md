# Manual 31 — NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500 release-time source state

**Verified:** 2026-08-31

## Controlled baseline

Manual 31 uses the New York Department of Financial Services Cybersecurity Regulation, 23 NYCRR Part 500, as amended by the Second Amendment effective November 1, 2023, as the operative regulatory baseline.

Authoritative release-time sources:
- New York State Department of Financial Services Cybersecurity Resource Center: https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry_guidance/cybersecurity
- DFS final adoption materials for the Second Amendment to 23 NYCRR Part 500.

The DFS Resource Center continues to identify the November 1, 2023 Second Amendment as the current amended Cybersecurity Regulation. It states that new requirements took effect in phases beginning December 1, 2023 and April 29, 2024, with additional one-year, 18-month, and two-year implementation periods. As of this release verification date, those phased implementation dates have elapsed; the manual therefore treats the amended requirements as current obligations where applicable rather than as future transition items.

## Controlled regulatory boundary

The manual separates:
1. binding requirements in 23 NYCRR Part 500;
2. DFS FAQs, training, checklists, implementation timelines, resource-center explanations, industry letters, and advisories;
3. organization-specific implementation practice; and
4. legal conclusions that require qualified analysis of the actual covered entity and facts.

Covered-entity status, exemptions, limited exemptions, Class A status, notice duties, extortion-payment reporting, annual filings, material-compliance determinations, and violation analysis are not inferred generically. They must be evaluated against the current regulation and the entity's facts.

## Release-time observations

- DFS states that Part 500 was amended effective November 1, 2023.
- DFS continues to maintain implementation, filing, exemption, incident-notification, MFA, governance, incident-response/BCDR, and other compliance resources.
- DFS's October 2024 AI cybersecurity industry letter connects AI-related risk management to existing Part 500 duties and notes the November 1, 2025 asset-inventory milestone; this letter is guidance, not a substitute for the regulation.
- DFS's June 23, 2025 industry letter on global conflict encourages regulated entities to review Part 500 compliance and emphasizes MFA, privileged access, vulnerability management, third-party risk, and incident/BCDR readiness; it remains guidance/advisory material.
