# Manual 50 — Global AI Governance Crosswalk Source Map

**Verification date:** 1 September 2026  
**Canonical stage:** 1 — source map and mapping method

## Source families

### A. Universal operating model

**Manual 46 — Universal AI Governance Foundation**  
Use as the common-control spine. It is an internal operational model, not a legal or standards authority.

### B. European Union

**Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — EU AI Act**  
**2026 amendment / consolidated legal state:** use the current primary-law baseline registered and reconciled in Manual 47.  
Status: binding law within its scope.  
Crosswalk rule: legal obligations, actors, thresholds and application dates must remain explicit and may not be reduced to generic control themes.

### C. ISO/IEC 42001

**ISO/IEC 42001 — Artificial intelligence management system standard**  
Status: voluntary management-system standard unless incorporated by contract/law; certification is separate from mere alignment.  
Copyright rule: do not reproduce copyrighted standard text or clause content beyond what repository permissions allow. Map using high-level management-system/control relationships and independently developed descriptions.

### D. NIST

**NIST AI RMF 1.0 / NIST AI 100-1**  
**NIST AI 600-1 GenAI Profile**  
Current state verified through Manual 49: AI RMF 1.0 remains published; NIST states it is being revised.  
Status: voluntary framework/profile.  
Crosswalk rule: GOVERN/MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE relationships must not be represented as law or certification.

### E. Singapore

**Model AI Governance Framework**  
**Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI**  
**AI Verify Testing Framework**  
**Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI — May 2026 update / v1.5 identified by current IMDA ecosystem page**  
Current state verified through Manual 48.  
Status: governance guidance and assurance/testing tools, not automatic legal compliance or certification.

### F. OECD

**OECD AI Principles / Recommendation on AI [OECD/LEGAL/0449]**  
Current OECD source verified 1 September 2026 states the Principles were adopted in 2019 and updated in 2024.  
https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html

The updated principles include five values-based principles and five recommendations to governments. For enterprise crosswalk purposes, the primary value-based relationships are:

- inclusive growth, sustainable development and well-being;
- human rights and democratic values, including fairness and privacy;
- transparency and explainability;
- robustness, security and safety;
- accountability.

Status: intergovernmental policy standard/recommendation, not a private certification scheme or direct substitute for jurisdiction-specific law.

## Source hierarchy and conflict rule

1. Binding primary law controls legal mapping.
2. Official standards/framework publications control their own framework mapping.
3. Official implementation guidance supports interpretation but does not silently change the status of primary law or standards.
4. Internal Manual 46 controls are the harmonisation spine only; they do not override external requirements.
5. A blank or qualified mapping is preferred over unsupported equivalence.
