# Authoritative Source Register

This register records the primary standards and guidance used to develop the Audit Readiness and Remediation Management Practical Manual. Requirements must be verified against the edition, jurisdiction, contract, audit criteria, and organizational context that actually apply.

## Primary sources

1. **NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5 — Assessing Security and Privacy Controls in Information Systems and Organizations**
   - Assessment procedures for examining, interviewing, and testing controls.
   - Release 5.2.0 assessment-procedure updates dated August 2025 are the current baseline used by this manual.

2. **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations**
   - Control objectives, implementation expectations, documentation, and continuous monitoring context.
   - Release 5.2.0 updates dated August 2025 are included in the baseline.

3. **NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0**
   - Governance, risk management, improvement, and outcome-based program context.

4. **ISO 19011:2026 — Guidelines for auditing management systems**
   - Audit principles, audit-program management, conducting audits, competence, evidence, findings, and reporting.

5. **U.S. Government Accountability Office, Standards for Internal Control in the Federal Government (2025 Green Book)**
   - Internal-control design, implementation, operating effectiveness, documentation, monitoring, deficiency evaluation, and corrective actions.
   - Effective beginning fiscal year 2026.

6. **The Institute of Internal Auditors, Global Internal Audit Standards**
   - Effective 9 January 2025.
   - Supports audit planning, engagement performance, communication, findings, recommendations, action plans, and follow-up.

## Source-use controls

- Do not copy protected standards text beyond permitted quotation and citation limits.
- Distinguish mandatory criteria from guidance and organizational practice.
- Record the exact framework, standard, regulation, contract, policy, and version used for each assessment.
- Do not represent automated checking as an audit opinion, certification decision, legal conclusion, or human professional judgment.
- Revalidate this register before each publication revision.
