**State:** controlled architecture only; not a publication candidate.  
**Privacy baseline:** Singapore PDPA and current PDPC regulations/guidance, subject to release-time reverification.  
**AI baseline:** current IMDA/PDPC/AI Verify governance frameworks for Traditional, Generative, and Agentic AI, subject to release-time reverification.  
**Boundary:** binding privacy law/regulation, regulator guidance/enforcement material, voluntary AI governance frameworks/testing resources, sector overlays, and organisation practice must remain distinct.

## Chapter design requirements

Every chapter must explicitly distinguish: (a) binding PDPA/statutory or regulatory requirements, (b) PDPC advisory/enforcement guidance, (c) voluntary IMDA/PDPC/AI Verify AI governance or testing guidance, (d) sector-specific or contractual overlays, and (e) organisation-specific implementation choices. Voluntary AI governance guidance must not be described as a standalone legal mandate.

The controlled master must preserve the current status of data portability and must not state that the obligation is operative unless release-time verification confirms that the necessary regulations have taken effect. Breach-notification timing and thresholds must be treated as dated legal/regulatory facts and reverified immediately before release.

The Agentic AI layer must preserve human accountability, bounded agent authority, meaningful approval checkpoints, technical lifecycle controls, third-party-agent risk, multi-agent considerations, transparency, training, and automation-bias safeguards without converting non-binding guidance into legal requirements.

## Supersession boundary

The earlier HITRUST CSF Manual 35 source-state and controlled architecture are superseded preparation artifacts. They remain historical evidence but are not the current series-order-35 publication target.

## Localization and review boundary

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR project translations must preserve Singapore legal and regulatory terms, PDPA obligation distinctions, controller/intermediary concepts, voluntary-versus-binding AI framework boundaries, breach-notification conditions, cross-border transfer concepts, data-portability status, and Agentic AI governance terminology. They are not PDPC-, IMDA-, or Singapore-government-authorised translations.
