# Chapter 128 — Evidence Preservation and Legal Hold

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 128 draft language.

## Requirement

Organizations must preserve records required by the EU AI Act and other applicable law and should implement legal holds when litigation, investigation, enforcement, incident, complaint, or dispute is reasonably anticipated. A legal hold is a legal-process control, not a standalone EU AI Act obligation, but it supports compliance with documentation, logging, recordkeeping, authority-access, incident, and corrective-action duties.

## Plain-English explanation

Routine deletion must stop when relevant evidence may be needed. Preservation must cover not only documents, but also logs, model and system versions, prompts, outputs, configuration, training and validation records, supplier communications, decisions, notices, and access history.

## Preservation requirements

Define at minimum:

1. statutory and contractual retention obligations;
2. legal-hold triggers and approval authority;
3. custodians, systems, models, versions, repositories, and suppliers in scope;
4. relevant logs, prompts, outputs, datasets, code, configuration, and documentation;
5. preservation of metadata, authenticity, integrity, and chain of custody;
6. suspension of deletion, rotation, overwriting, and model retirement;
7. third-party preservation notices and verification;
8. privacy, minimisation, confidentiality, privilege, and access controls;
9. collection, export, indexing, and reproducibility requirements;
10. periodic hold review, modification, and release;
11. documentation of gaps, unavailable evidence, and remediation;
12. secure disposition after lawful release.

## GlobalWay example

After receiving a complaint concerning an automated recruitment decision, GlobalWay places a hold on the relevant model version, applicant records, prompts, scoring outputs, recruiter actions, override logs, supplier notices, testing records, and policy versions. It verifies that normal log rotation and deletion are suspended for the preserved material.

## Control activity

Legal must issue documented holds when required, and Technology, Records Management, Security, Privacy, HR, and suppliers must confirm implementation. The organization must test whether technically volatile AI evidence can actually be preserved and reproduced.

## Evidence

- retention schedule and legal-hold procedure;
- hold notice and scope;
- custodian and system acknowledgements;
- preservation and collection logs;
- integrity and chain-of-custody records;
- supplier confirmations;
- periodic review records;
- release and disposition approval.

## Audit test

Select legal holds involving AI systems. Confirm that triggers were identified promptly, scope included relevant technical and human evidence, deletion was suspended, suppliers complied, integrity was protected, and release was authorized and documented.

## Primary legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended: applicable technical-documentation, logging, recordkeeping, quality-management, monitoring, incident, corrective-action, and authority-access provisions.
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and other applicable privacy and procedural law.
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 where applicable.
- Legal-hold duties depend on applicable procedural and substantive law and must not be presented as a universal AI Act requirement.
