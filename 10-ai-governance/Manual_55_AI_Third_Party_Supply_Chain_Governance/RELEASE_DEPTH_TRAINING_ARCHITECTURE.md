# Manual 55 — Release-Depth Training Architecture

**Controlled stage:** Stage 2 — release-depth control/evidence/scenario architecture  
**Date:** 2 September 2026

## Training objective
Prepare practitioners to govern material AI third parties across selection, contracting, onboarding, operation, change, incident response, continuity, and exit using evidence-based decisions rather than supplier assertions.

## Core learning modules
1. Third-party AI taxonomy and materiality.
2. Due diligence and evidence sufficiency.
3. Security, privacy, data-use, and residency controls.
4. Model/version transparency and material change.
5. Agentic delegation, plugins, connectors, MCP, and tool authorization.
6. Supply-chain integrity and provenance.
7. Contractual control clauses and evidence rights.
8. Provider-claim challenge and independent verification.
9. Incident coordination and evidence preservation.
10. Concentration, continuity, portability, and exit.
11. Monitoring, exceptions, residual risk, and revalidation.
12. Post-exit assurance.

## Required exercises
- classify provider scenarios by materiality and control depth;
- distinguish E0/E1/E2/E3 evidence and decide whether each is sufficient;
- review a provider contract for missing AI-specific control clauses;
- evaluate a silent model/version change and determine revalidation scope;
- analyze a plugin permission expansion as an agentic third-party risk event;
- respond to a provider security incident requiring coordinated containment;
- assess concentration risk across shared cloud/foundation-model dependencies;
- execute an exit checklist and identify missing deletion/credential evidence.

## Controlled decision model
Each provider decision must produce a traceable record containing provider/service identity, owners, materiality tier, applicable TP controls, evidence and evidence level, unresolved gaps, compensating controls, residual-risk owner, decision state, expiry, and change triggers.

## Release-depth evidence requirements
A release candidate must demonstrate that TP-01 through TP-18 are represented in training content, each control has evidence expectations, the scenario pack exercises material lifecycle risks, and supplier claims are clearly distinguished from independently verified evidence.

## Localization architecture
Controlled publication sources will be maintained in English, es-419, and pt-BR with stable TP control identifiers. Localization may adapt prose for language and regional clarity but must preserve control meaning, source-status qualifiers, decision states, evidence levels, and scenario intent.

## Candidate preparation gate
Manual 55 can enter deterministic candidate build only after the source-control architecture, evidence workbook, release-depth training architecture, trilingual controlled sources, deterministic DOCX/PDF build, visible-text checks, and retained publication gates are all in place.
