# Manual 35 — Singapore AI & Privacy Governance Controlled Implementation

This controlled implementation manual operationalises Singapore privacy and AI-governance requirements and guidance while preserving their legal status and source boundaries.

## Controlled source hierarchy

1. Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and applicable regulations.
2. Current Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) regulatory, advisory, breach-notification, enforcement, and compliance materials.
3. Current IMDA/PDPC/AI Verify governance resources for Traditional AI, Generative AI, and Agentic AI.
4. Sector-specific regulatory or contractual overlays where applicable.
5. Organisation-specific implementation procedures and evidence.

Voluntary AI governance frameworks and testing resources are not represented as standalone statutory obligations. Data Portability is treated as change-watch until the necessary regulations take effect. Project translations in es-419 and pt-BR are unofficial and are not Singapore-government-authorised translations.

## Publication controls

The manual follows the repository controlled-build process: 32-chapter EN master, controlled es-419 and pt-BR localisations, structure/parity QA, reproducible six-binary DOCX/PDF candidate, rendered/accessibility review, immutable SHA-256 provenance, exact-byte staging, predecessor publication, and final catalog/release-registry reconciliation.
