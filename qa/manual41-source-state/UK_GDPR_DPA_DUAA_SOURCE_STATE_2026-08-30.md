# Manual 41 — UK GDPR / Data Protection Act Authoritative Source State

**Manual:** 41 — UK GDPR / Data Protection Act Controlled Implementation  
**State:** downstream authoritative-source gate only; publication state unchanged  
**Verified:** 2026-08-30/31 UTC against current ICO materials

## Controlled baseline

The current UK general data-protection baseline is the **UK GDPR**, read together with the **Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018)**, as amended by the **Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA)**. ICO guidance updated June 19, 2026 confirms that all DUAA provisions affecting data-protection law are now in force.

The DUAA amends but does not replace the UK GDPR, DPA 2018 or Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Manual 41 must therefore treat the post-DUAA text and current ICO guidance as the active baseline and must not preserve pre-DUAA assumptions where the law has changed.

## Source-layer boundaries

Manual 41 must separately identify:

1. UK GDPR requirements;
2. DPA 2018 statutory provisions and sector-specific Parts;
3. DUAA amendments now in force;
4. PECR requirements where relevant;
5. binding regulations and commencement provisions;
6. ICO statutory codes, guidance and regulatory materials;
7. court/tribunal decisions where they materially affect interpretation;
8. organization-specific policies, procedures, contracts, risk decisions and evidence.

ICO guidance must not be presented as if it were the statutory text, and pre-commencement guidance must be reconciled against the now-effective DUAA changes before use.

## Current DUAA boundary

By June 19, 2026, the data-protection provisions of the DUAA had come into force. The controlled manual must therefore assess current law rather than treat the DUAA as future-change material. Relevant changes include, where applicable, recognized legitimate interests, changes affecting legitimate-interests analysis, data-subject-access handling, complaints procedures, automated decision-making, cookies/PECR, international transfers, research/scientific processing, and ICO powers.

The exact scope of each change must be verified against the enacted text and current ICO guidance before control language is frozen.

## Core implementation boundaries

The controlled manual must correctly scope at least: lawfulness, fairness and transparency; purpose limitation; data minimization; accuracy; storage limitation; integrity/confidentiality; accountability; lawful bases and special-category/criminal-offence processing; transparency notices; data-subject rights; DPIAs; privacy by design/default; processor/controller and joint-controller governance; contracts; international transfers; breach handling and ICO/data-subject notification where applicable; automated decision-making; direct marketing/PECR interactions; records of processing; and evidence of accountability.

No blanket claim may be made that every UK-related processing activity falls under identical UK GDPR/DPA rules, that all processing requires consent, that DUAA removed core UK GDPR protections, or that ICO guidance has the same status as legislation.
