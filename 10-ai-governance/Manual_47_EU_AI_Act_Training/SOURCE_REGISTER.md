## Controlled legal interpretation rules

- Primary legislation controls where explanatory materials conflict.
- Verify consolidated/amending legislation before publication.
- Distinguish rules currently applicable from rules with future application dates.
- Distinguish binding obligations from Commission guidelines, FAQs and voluntary codes.
- Do not infer provider/deployer status solely from procurement labels; analyze the statutory role and actual activity.
- Do not state that framework alignment proves compliance.
- Reverify every application date immediately before publication.
