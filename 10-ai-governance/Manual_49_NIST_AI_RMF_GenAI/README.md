# Manual 49 — NIST AI RMF 1.0 + NIST AI 600-1 GenAI Operational Guide

**Status:** PRE-STAGE / CONTROLLED DEVELOPMENT  
**Version:** 0.1.0-draft  
**Source verification date:** 31 August 2026

## Purpose

Turn NIST AI RMF 1.0 and the NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile into a practical enterprise risk-management and assurance program that maps cleanly to Manual 46's universal governance spine.

## Currency note

NIST states that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised. Manual 49 therefore treats 1.0 as the currently published framework while requiring a source refresh before release.

## Architecture

1. AI RMF purpose, audience and voluntary-use model.
2. Trustworthiness characteristics and risk framing.
3. GOVERN as the cross-cutting governance function.
4. MAP — context, purpose, actors, impacts and dependencies.
5. MEASURE — testing, evaluation, verification and validation.
6. MANAGE — prioritization, treatment, monitoring and response.
7. AI RMF Playbook and evidence practices.
8. NIST AI Resource Center and operational resources.
9. NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile.
10. GenAI risk taxonomy and risk treatment.
11. Confabulation and content-integrity risk.
12. Information-security risk.
13. Privacy risk.
14. Harmful bias and representational risk where relevant.
15. Human-AI configuration and overreliance risk.
16. Intellectual-property and data-provenance considerations.
17. RAG and knowledge-source governance.
18. Model/provider and third-party risk.
19. Red teaming, TEVV and independent challenge.
20. AI inventory and risk tiering.
21. Monitoring, KRIs, incident handling and change management.
22. Mapping AI RMF outcomes to Manual 46 controls.
23. Crosswalk to EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and Singapore governance.
24. Scenario-based exercises and evidence catalogue.
25. Audit/assurance checklist.

## Practical operating model

Use the four NIST functions as a continuous system rather than a sequential checklist:

**GOVERN** establishes accountability, policy, culture and risk tolerance.  
**MAP** establishes context, intended use, affected parties, dependencies and impact.  
**MEASURE** evaluates performance, risk, uncertainty and control effectiveness.  
**MANAGE** prioritizes and treats risk, monitors outcomes and responds to change.

Governance should inform every other function throughout the lifecycle.

## Evidence model

For each relevant AI RMF outcome, capture:

**Outcome → organizational interpretation → control objective → control activity → owner → evidence → test procedure → finding/exception → remediation → residual risk**
