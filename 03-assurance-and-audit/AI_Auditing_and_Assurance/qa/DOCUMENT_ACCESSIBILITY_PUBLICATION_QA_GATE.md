# Manual 05 — Document, Accessibility & Publication QA Gate

Status: PRE-STAGED / FAIL-CLOSED

Required before publication:
- accessible DOCX and PDF candidates for every approved language;
- page-level visual review of headings, tables, lists, figures, workpaper examples, findings and page breaks;
- alt text/accessibility verification for all instructional graphics;
- metadata, language tags, reading order, links and bookmarks checked;
- release manifest and checksums tied to the reviewed commit;
- audit/assurance disclaimers preserved so publication does not imply certification or a formal audit opinion.

Any substantive source, localization or assurance-boundary change invalidates affected artifacts and requires regeneration/re-review. Final Human Release Approval remains mandatory.
