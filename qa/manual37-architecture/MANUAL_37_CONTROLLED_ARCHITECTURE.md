# Manual 37 — Colombia Data Protection / Habeas Data Controlled Architecture

**State:** controlled architecture only; not a publication candidate.  
**Baseline:** Ley Estatutaria 1581 de 2012 plus applicable regulatory provisions consolidated in Decreto 1074 de 2015, with current SIC circulars/instructions and release-time reverification of enacted reform, RNBD applicability, transfer/transmission rules, and explicitly claimed sector overlays.  
**Boundary:** constitutional habeas-data/privacy rights, statutory law, binding decrees, binding SIC instructions, SIC guidance/enforcement materials, special data regimes, and organization-specific implementation practices must remain distinct.

## Chapter design requirements

Every chapter must identify, as applicable: purpose; applicability; controlling source layer; accountable role; implementable procedure; operating trigger/frequency; required evidence object; evidence location; review/test method; exception/remediation path; reassessment trigger; and any decision requiring qualified legal, privacy, localization, accessibility, or specialist judgment.

The controlled English master must not present SIC guidance, FAQs, enforcement examples, or reform proposals as enacted statutory text. It must not make blanket claims regarding RNBD registration, consent, breach reporting, international transfers, sector applicability, or universal obligations without current source support and applicability analysis. Legislative proposals remain change-watch material unless enacted and effective.

## Evidence-object model

Reusable evidence objects should include: applicability assessment; constitutional/statutory source map; data inventory; role/responsibility matrix; authorization record; consent-exception rationale; privacy-notice inventory; processing-purpose register; sensitive-data assessment; child/adolescent-data assessment; consultation/claim log; identity-validation record; response-timing record; security-control mapping; incident assessment record; privacy-risk assessment; RNBD applicability assessment; RNBD operational record; international-transfer assessment; transmission agreement checklist; vendor/encargado register; subprocessor register; shared-responsibility matrix; cloud-processing inventory; marketing/profiling assessment; workforce privacy record; retention/deletion schedule; SIC obligation register; sector-overlay matrix; evidence index; exception/remediation log; management dashboard; regulatory-change record; provenance manifest; exact-hash substantive-review record.

## Localization and review boundary

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions must preserve Colombian legal terminology and meaning, especially habeas data, responsable/encargado distinctions, authorization and exceptions, consultations/claims, RNBD applicability, transfer versus transmission distinctions, SIC regulatory/guidance boundaries, sector-overlay conditions, and organization-specific implementation choices. Project translations must not be represented as official SIC translations. Semantic review must be tied to exact frozen source identities whenever a genuine non-deterministic language/legal judgment is required.

## Release gates

Before publication: authoritative-source and current-law reverification; enacted-law vs decree vs binding SIC instruction vs guidance/reform boundary verification; controlled-English completion; exact English freeze; controlled es-419/pt-BR localization; deterministic structure/parity/link/metadata/accessibility QA; reproducible six-binary DOCX/PDF candidate; workflow-security and release-package QA; SHA-256 provenance; durable staging; exact-hash substantive human review only where a specific documented non-deterministic competency genuinely requires it; predecessor publication through Manual 36; and final catalog plus work-product release-registry reconciliation.

No routine or generic final human approval is added beyond the standing clean-candidate release authorization. A clean candidate with no unresolved error or issue and all applicable objective gates green advances automatically once predecessor order is satisfied, except for any specifically documented non-delegable human judgment that remains genuinely required.
