## Verified technical points

- CSF 2.0 is voluntary, flexible cybersecurity risk-management guidance rather than a prescriptive control catalogue or certification scheme.
- The Core is organized around six concurrent and continuous Functions: GOVERN, IDENTIFY, PROTECT, DETECT, RESPOND, and RECOVER.
- GOVERN was added in CSF 2.0 and addresses organizational context, risk-management strategy, roles and authorities, policy, oversight, and cybersecurity supply-chain risk management.
- Organizational Profiles describe current and/or target cybersecurity posture using CSF outcomes.
- CSF Tiers characterize the rigor of cybersecurity risk governance and management; they are not maturity scores or universal compliance grades.
- The manual correctly separates outcome definition, implementation, evidence, testing, ownership, gaps, corrective action, and reporting.
- Framework mappings and tool output are correctly described as supporting evidence rather than proof of compliance or operating effectiveness.
