## Assurance boundaries

Metrics, scores, trends, thresholds, benchmarks, dashboards, and management reports are decision aids. They do not independently prove compliance, security, control effectiveness, audit assurance, or business performance.

## Human-review limitations

Native-language editorial approval, organization-specific legal or regulatory review, statistical validation, accessibility testing, formal audit assurance, and full page-by-page human visual review are not represented as completed.
