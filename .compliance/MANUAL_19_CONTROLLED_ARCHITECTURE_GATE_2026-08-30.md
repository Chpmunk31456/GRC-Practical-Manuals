# Manual 19 — FedRAMP / FISMA Controlled Architecture Gate

**Series order:** 19  
**State:** controlled architecture / QA preparation; not controlled-English master and not publication authorization

## Authoritative scope boundary

Build from the current official FedRAMP transition state, current NIST RMF/SP 800-53 sources, FISMA statutory requirements, and current OMB/CISA policy sources applicable to scope. Keep these layers distinct. Do not present legacy Rev5 procedures as timeless requirements, proposed/RFC material as adopted requirements, or FedRAMP certification mechanics as identical to agency authorization obligations.

## Acceptance model

Each chapter must later identify purpose, applicability, governing source layer, accountable owner, implementable procedure, operating trigger/frequency, evidence object, evidence location, test/review method, exception/remediation path, and reassessment trigger.

## Fail-closed preparation controls

Before controlled-English promotion require explicit separation of FISMA, FedRAMP, NIST, OMB/CISA, and agency-specific layers; current-source verification for FedRAMP transition dates and adopted rule sets; no unsupported certification/authorization equivalence claims; original explanatory text rather than copied protected material; complete 32-chapter structure; and exact-source freeze before localization.

Manual 19 may continue in parallel but cannot publish before Manual 18. Publication later requires controlled trilingual sources, exact-head QA, applicable substantive review evidence, rendered/accessibility QA, six durable DOCX/PDF artifacts, SHA-256 provenance, workflow security, and catalog/release-registry reconciliation.
