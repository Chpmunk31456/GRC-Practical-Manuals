# AI Governance Training Series

This section contains the controlled AI-governance training series beginning with Manual 46.

1. **Manual 46 — Universal AI Governance Foundation**

## Learning architecture

Manual 46 establishes the universal, framework-neutral governance operating model. Manual 47 is the first specialist legal training and operationalizes the EU AI Act. Later manuals layer Singapore, NIST, cross-framework interoperability, agentic AI, cybersecurity, privacy/data governance, model risk and other specialist domains onto the same universal governance spine.

Interview-specific questions, case drills and 30/60/90-day material are reserved for the final capstone rather than embedded in the universal foundation.

## Staggered publication pipeline

The series follows the repository's canonical one-stage-behind rule. The front manual is driven continuously toward publication while each downstream manual remains exactly one substantive stage behind its immediate predecessor whenever safe work is available.

Publication order remains sequential even when research, architecture, controlled drafting, localization preparation and QA work proceed in parallel.

## Release authorization

The repository canonical release equation applies throughout this series:

**NO ERRORS + NO ISSUES + ALL APPLICABLE OBJECTIVE GATES GREEN + PREDECESSOR PUBLISHED = HUMAN APPROVAL SATISFIED UNDER STANDING AUTHORIZATION = PUBLISH NOW.**

No separate generic final approval, owner confirmation or routine release-signoff gate is added after that equation is satisfied. A narrowly scoped specialist review remains fail-closed only when a specific documented non-deterministic judgment genuinely cannot be established by objective evidence.

## Source and framework discipline

- Distinguish binding law, standards, voluntary frameworks, codes of practice and explanatory guidance.
- Use current authoritative/primary sources and refresh time-sensitive regulatory facts before release.
- Do not treat framework alignment as proof of legal compliance or ISO certification.
- Respect copyrighted standards; summarize and operationalize concepts without reproducing protected text.
- Preserve traceability from requirement/risk through controls, evidence, testing, exceptions, remediation and residual-risk decisions.
