# Appendix A — AI Inventory Template

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master. This is an organizational governance register. Specific fields become legally required only where a binding provision applies to the relevant actor, system, model, or use.

## Inventory record

| Field | Required information |
|---|---|
| Inventory ID | Unique identifier |
| Legal entity | Entity developing, providing, importing, distributing, integrating, or deploying the system/model |
| Actor role(s) | Provider, GPAI provider, deployer, importer, distributor, authorised representative, product manufacturer, downstream provider, other |
| System/model name | Official business and technical name |
| Business and technical owners | Accountable owners and alternates |
| Lifecycle stage | Idea, pilot, development, validation, production, suspended, retired |
| Intended purpose | Approved purpose, decision context, users, and affected persons |
| Version/configuration | Production model, system, prompts, tools, data, and release |
| Jurisdictions | Development, market placement, deployment, output use, hosting, and support locations |
| Provider/vendor and dependencies | Model, API, cloud, data, software, subprocessors, and critical dependencies |
| AI Act applicability | In scope, partially in scope, out of scope, excluded/specially treated, uncertain |
| Legal classification | Article 5 concern, Article 6/Annex I or III high-risk, Article 50 transparency, GPAI/systemic-risk, other |
| Governance risk tier | Organization-defined tier, clearly distinguished from statutory classification |
| Data | Training, tuning, retrieval, operational, monitoring, personal, special-category, confidential, and retention information |
| Human oversight | Reviewer, authority, override, stop, appeal, and escalation |
| Required assessments | Applicability, role, classification, FRIA, DPIA, security, vendor, conformity, transparency, other |
| Monitoring and incidents | Metrics, thresholds, complaints, incidents, and notification obligations |
| Evidence location | System of record and version-linked repository |
| Application dates | Provision-specific dates and transitional rules |
| Approval/status | Approved, conditional, restricted, suspended, rejected, retired |
| Review history | Last review, next review, triggers, open findings, and exceptions |

## Control requirements

- Reconcile the inventory with procurement, finance, cloud, software, data, security, HR, product, and vendor records.
- Record pilots and externally supplied AI where material risk or legal obligations may arise.
- Preserve historical versions, role/classification decisions, suspension, and retirement evidence.
- Reassess after intended-purpose, actor-role, version, model, data, supplier, population, jurisdiction, incident, or legal change.
- Current consolidated EUR-Lex text controls over older summaries.
