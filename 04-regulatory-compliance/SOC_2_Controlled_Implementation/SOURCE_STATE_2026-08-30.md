# Manual 15 — SOC 2 authoritative source state

**Verification date:** 2026-08-30
**Lane:** downstream preflight / not publication
**Authority:** AICPA & CIMA official SOC 2 resource surfaces

## Verified current baseline

The official AICPA & CIMA SOC 2 topic page currently identifies the following controlled materials for SOC 2 work:

- **2017 Trust Services Criteria (With Revised Points of Focus — 2022)**. The AICPA resource page identifies this as the Trust Services Criteria used for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.
- **2018 SOC 2 Description Criteria (With Revised Implementation Guidance — 2022)**. The AICPA SOC 2 topic page continues to expose this as the description-criteria resource for management's service-organization system description.
- The AICPA SOC 2 reporting guide currently describes SOC 2 as an assertion-based examination of a service organization's description of its system and controls relevant to security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, or privacy and states that the 2022 guide applies the 2017 TSC with revised 2022 points of focus and the 2018 description criteria with revised 2022 implementation guidance.
- AICPA published a refreshed **SOC for Service Organizations Engagements — Overview** on 2026-04-23; this is contextual program guidance and does not by itself replace the TSC or description-criteria baselines above.

## Controlled interpretation boundary

Manual 15 must remain an original implementation/readiness manual. It may use permissible identifiers, framework structure, and short quotations where legally appropriate, but must not reproduce AICPA copyrighted criteria or paid guide content. It must not describe SOC 2 as a certification or imply AICPA endorsement.

The implementation manual must keep separate:

1. Trust Services Criteria from Description Criteria;
2. management's system description/assertion from practitioner examination responsibilities;
3. Type 1 readiness from Type 2 operating-effectiveness readiness;
4. SOC 2 from SOC 1, SOC 3, SOC for Cybersecurity, and SOC for Supply Chain;
5. internal readiness evidence from the independent CPA's professional judgment and report.

## Release-time revalidation triggers

Before Manual 15 publication, re-check the official AICPA & CIMA SOC 2 resource surface for:

- replacement or revision of the Trust Services Criteria;
- replacement or revision of the SOC 2 Description Criteria;
- changes to relevant attestation standards or official SOC 2 reporting guidance;
- changes affecting Type 1/Type 2 terminology, system-description expectations, subservice organizations, complementary user-entity controls, or reporting boundaries.

Any successor authoritative material invalidates stale source assumptions and requires impact analysis before publication.
