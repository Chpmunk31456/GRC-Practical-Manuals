# Manual 18 — GLBA / FTC Safeguards Controlled Architecture Gate

**Series order:** 18  
**State:** controlled architecture / QA preparation; not controlled-English master and not publication authorization  
**Predecessor:** Manual 17 — controlled-English master under exact-head QA

## Authoritative scope

Use the merged Manual 18 authoritative-source verification as the controlling source decision. Preserve separate layers for statutory GLBA context, 16 CFR Part 314 Safeguards Rule requirements, FTC amendments/effective dates, official FTC compliance guidance, and organization-specific implementation practices. FTC jurisdiction must not be generalized to institutions governed by other GLBA regulators.

The current security-event notification boundary must remain scoped to the Rule's definitions and conditions, including the current 500-consumer threshold and no-later-than-30-days reporting requirement verified from official FTC sources. Reverify the current text and effective-date state before candidate freeze.

## Chapter acceptance model

Each chapter must later identify purpose, applicability, regulatory/source layer, accountable owner, implementable procedure, operating trigger/frequency, evidence object, evidence location, review/test method, exception/remediation path, and reassessment trigger.

## QA preparation

Before the controlled-English master is promoted, require checks for:
- explicit source-layer provenance for substantive regulatory statements;
- no representation of FTC guidance as regulatory text;
- no jurisdictional overgeneralization;
- exact preservation of the current notification-event scope and timing boundary;
- original explanatory language rather than copied regulatory text;
- complete 32-chapter structure;
- evidence/test/remediation/reassessment concepts in every chapter;
- localization blocked until exact English freeze;
- artifact-first publication sequence preserved.

Manual 18 must remain one substantive stage behind Manual 17 and may not enter localization or publication-candidate work before Manual 17 advances through its corresponding predecessor gate.
