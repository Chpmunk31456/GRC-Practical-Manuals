## Verified source state

- **NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2** remains the controlled RMF lifecycle reference for PREPARE, CATEGORIZE, SELECT, IMPLEMENT, ASSESS, AUTHORIZE, and MONITOR.
- **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5** remains final. NIST issued **Release 5.2.0** on August 27, 2025 with new and revised controls/control enhancements plus discussion and related-control updates.
- **NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5** remains final. Release 5.2.0 includes corresponding new assessment procedures for the 5.2.0 control changes.
- **NIST SP 800-53B** remains final. NIST states Release 5.2.0 contains **no changes to the baselines**; the release number was updated for consistency with SP 800-53 and SP 800-53A.
- **NIST SP 800-18 Rev. 2** is final, published June 30, 2026. It covers system security, privacy, and cybersecurity supply chain risk management plans and correlates plan elements with RMF steps/tasks.
- NIST's Cybersecurity and Privacy Reference Tool identifies SP 800-53, SP 800-53A, and SP 800-53B Release 5.2.0 datasets as final and provides structured human- and machine-consumable reference data.

## Release implications

- Manual 10 may treat SP 800-53, SP 800-53A, SP 800-53B Release 5.2.0, and SP 800-18 Rev. 2 as current final NIST sources.
- Do not imply that SP 800-53B Release 5.2.0 changed control-baseline content; NIST explicitly states that it did not.
- Preserve SP 800-53 controls and baselines as risk-based and tailorable rather than universal checklist requirements outside their applicable governance context.
- Preserve the distinction between implementation evidence, assessment evidence, risk acceptance, POA&M, and the accountable human authorization decision.
- OSCAL and machine-readable evidence improve traceability and automation but do not create authorization, certification, or assurance by themselves.
- Reverify all controlled sources at the exact final candidate head. Material source changes reopen affected mappings, technical review, localization, graphics, QA, compliance assessment, and release approval.

## Assurance boundary

This record verifies public NIST source state only. It does not authorize a system, establish compliance, certify control effectiveness, determine acceptable residual risk, or substitute for an authorizing official or competent assessor.
