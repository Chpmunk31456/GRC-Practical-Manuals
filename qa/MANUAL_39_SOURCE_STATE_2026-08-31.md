# Manual 39 — COPPA release-time source state

**State:** downstream prepublication source refresh.  
**Verified baseline:** Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506, and **16 CFR Part 312**. FTC final rule amendments were published April 22, 2025; the amended rule is the current controlled regulatory baseline. FTC policy statements and enforcement materials remain separate from binding rule text.

## Controlled boundary
- Statute, 16 CFR Part 312, FTC rule amendments, FTC policy statements/guidance, safe-harbor materials, state child/privacy laws, and organization-specific practices remain distinct.
- Do not present policy statements as regulation or treat school/EdTech use as creating a universal exception.
- Age determination, actual knowledge, parental notice/consent, data minimization, retention, security, third-party disclosure, advertising, and safe-harbor applicability require fact-specific analysis.

## Release requirement
Reverify the FTC COPPA rule page, April 22, 2025 final amendments, and any later binding amendments at candidate freeze. Project translations are unofficial.
