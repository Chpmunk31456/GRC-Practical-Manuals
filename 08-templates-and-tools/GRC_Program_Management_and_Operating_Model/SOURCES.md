# Authoritative Source Register

**Validated:** 1 August 2026  
**Status:** official publisher pages reviewed for publication status and intended use

## NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- Identifier: NIST CSWP 29
- Publication date: 26 February 2024
- Status: Final
- Intended use: governance outcomes, roles and responsibilities, risk strategy, policy, oversight, communication, and continuous improvement
- Official location: https://www.nist.gov/publications/nist-cybersecurity-framework-csf-20
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

## NIST IR 8286D Update 1

- Publisher: National Institute of Standards and Technology
- Title: *Using Business Impact Analysis to Inform Risk Prioritization and Response*
- Publication date: February 2025
- Status: Final; supersedes the November 2022 edition
- Intended use: business-impact context, risk prioritization, response, communication, and enterprise-risk integration
- Official location: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8286/d/upd1/final
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

## ISO 31000:2018

- Publisher: International Organization for Standardization
- Title: *Risk management - Guidelines*
- Identifier: ISO 31000:2018, Edition 2
- Status: published baseline; organization-specific licensed access and current-edition verification remain required at use
- Intended use: principles for integrating risk management into governance, planning, decision-making, monitoring, and improvement
- Official location: https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

## 2024 Global Internal Audit Standards

- Publisher: The Institute of Internal Auditors
- Status: released 9 January 2024; effective 9 January 2025
- Intended use: distinction between management activity and independent internal-audit assurance; governance and quality context
- Official location: https://www.theiia.org/en/standards
- Accessed: 1 August 2026

## Source-control boundary

These sources inform general governance and risk-management practices. They do not establish organization-specific applicability, decision authority, legal sufficiency, compliance, control effectiveness, certification, or audit assurance. Copyrighted standards are referenced, not reproduced.
