# Manual 12 — CCPA / CPRA Authoritative Source Verification

**Manual:** CCPA / CPRA California Privacy Controlled Implementation  
**Verification date:** 2026-08-27  
**Controlled branch:** `build/ccpa-cpra-manual-12-2026`

## Verified source state

- The California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act remains the binding statutory baseline.
- CPPA regulations adopted in 2025 were approved by the Office of Administrative Law and became effective **2026-01-01**. They include updates to existing CCPA regulations plus requirements relating to risk assessments, annual cybersecurity audits, automated decisionmaking technology (ADMT), and insurance-company applicability.
- Risk-assessment compliance began **2026-01-01**. Submission/attestation obligations have later phased deadlines.
- ADMT requirements for businesses using ADMT to make significant decisions begin **2027-01-01**. Manual 12 must distinguish current effective regulatory text from future phased compliance dates.
- Cybersecurity-audit certification/submission deadlines are phased beginning in 2028 and vary by revenue tier.
- The CPPA's DROP regulations under the Delete Act are final and effective **2026-01-01** for the accessible deletion mechanism. These related data-broker obligations must be distinguished from the general CCPA business obligations.
- The 2023 CCPA regulations remain historically important but have been updated. Manual 12 must not teach the 2023 text as if it were the complete current 2026 regulatory baseline.

## Release implications

- Every requirement must carry an effective-date status where timing matters: current/effective, phased future compliance, historical/superseded-in-part, or guidance/information.
- Do not represent the 2027 ADMT compliance start as already due in 2026.
- Do not represent later cybersecurity-audit submission deadlines as already due in 2026, while still explaining preparatory obligations and applicability analysis.
- Risk-assessment requirements effective in 2026 should be treated as current where applicable.
- Distinguish general CCPA obligations from data-broker/Delete Act/DROP obligations.
- Applicability thresholds, exemptions, role classifications, consumer-right determinations, ADMT classification, and enforcement consequences require organization-specific competent human review.
- Reverify CPPA rulemaking and statutory status at the exact final candidate head.

## Assurance boundary

This record verifies source state only. It does not determine whether a particular organization is a business, service provider, contractor, third party, data broker, or covered ADMT user; it does not determine whether an exemption applies; and it does not establish legal compliance or enforcement outcomes.
