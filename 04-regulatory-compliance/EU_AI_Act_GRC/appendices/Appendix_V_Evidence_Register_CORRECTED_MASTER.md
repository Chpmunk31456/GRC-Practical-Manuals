## Quality criteria

Evidence must be relevant, complete, accurate, attributable, authentic, timely, version-linked, internally consistent, protected, understandable, retrievable, and sufficient for the stated legal, control, conformity, audit, or management purpose.

A policy title, certificate, screenshot, vendor statement, or live webpage is not sufficient by itself unless scope, version, period, source, and supporting detail are established.

## Evidence categories

Inventory/role/classification; risk/FRIA/DPIA; data governance; technical documentation; validation/testing; conformity; transparency/oversight; logs/monitoring; vendor/contracts; incidents/complaints; corrective action; literacy/competence; authority correspondence; audit/assurance; legal holds.
