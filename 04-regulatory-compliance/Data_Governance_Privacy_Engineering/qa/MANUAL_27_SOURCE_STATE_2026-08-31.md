# Manual 27 — Release-Time Source State — 2026-08-31

## Current controlled baseline

- NIST Privacy Framework Version 1.0 remains the published NIST Privacy Framework baseline.
- NIST Privacy Framework 1.1 remains an Initial Public Draft / forthcoming update; NIST's current project page still labels the final version as coming soon. It is change-watch material only for this release.
- NISTIR 8062, *An Introduction to Privacy Engineering and Risk Management in Federal Systems*, remains a published NIST reference. Its privacy-engineering objectives include predictability, manageability, and disassociability.

## Source-layer rule

Manual 27 keeps the following layers distinct:

1. applicable laws and regulations by jurisdiction;
2. regulator or competent-authority guidance;
3. NIST Privacy Framework and NIST privacy-engineering guidance;
4. contracts and customer requirements;
5. organizational policy, standards, procedures, and architecture decisions.

No NIST guidance is represented as binding law. No jurisdiction-specific privacy right, consent rule, transfer mechanism, retention period, breach-notification threshold, or data-category rule is generalized globally.

## Release decision

Proceed with the existing 32-chapter controlled English master. Freeze localization from the exact merged English source on current main. Preserve PF 1.1 as change-watch only and require source revalidation again if the candidate changes materially or NIST publishes PF 1.1 before final release.
