# AI Governance and Audit Toolkit

**Status:** Controlled companion-toolkit intake, stacked behind Manual 08.

**Controlled source language:** English (`en`)

**Planned publication languages:** English, neutral Latin American Spanish (`es-419`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`)

**Author and accountable human creator:** Alberto “Al” Leiva

## Purpose

This toolkit will provide reusable, evidence-oriented records for the controlled compliance-manual series. It is designed to reduce reinvention across AI governance, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, Generative AI, AI audit, AI security, HIPAA-related AI environments, and third-party risk work.

The toolkit will remain framework-aware without claiming that one record proves compliance across different laws or standards.

## Planned controlled tools

1. **AI System and Use Case Register** — inventory, owner, purpose, lifecycle, data/model/tool dependencies, risk tier, approvals and status.
2. **AI Risk and Impact Register** — risk/impact statement, affected parties, likelihood/impact, controls, treatment, residual risk and decision.
3. **AI Control and Evidence Matrix** — criteria/control objective, owner, implementation, evidence, test, result, gaps and reviewer.
4. **AI Audit Workpaper Index** — engagement, scope, criteria, test procedure, sample, evidence references, preparer/reviewer and conclusion.
5. **AI Findings and Remediation Log** — criteria, condition, cause, risk, severity, owner, response, remediation, validation and closure.
6. **AI Vendor and Component Register** — provider/component, criticality, data/access, subprocessors/fourth parties, evidence, contract controls and monitoring.
7. **AI Change and Reassessment Log** — model/data/tool/vendor/legal/source changes, impact, approvals, tests and reopened review gates.
8. **Human Review and Approval Record** — reviewer, date, decision, evidence, findings, remediation, exceptions and invalidation after material change.
9. **Release Evidence Manifest** — exact source commit, languages, artifacts, checksums, source review, QA gates, human approvals, GitHub release and DOI metadata.

## ISACA AAIA alignment

The toolkit will support Manual 05's controlled ISACA AAIA professional-practice alignment across **AI Governance and Risk**, **AI Operations**, and **AI Auditing Tools and Techniques**. It will use original record structures and public practice-domain names only; proprietary ISACA review-manual and exam content will not be reproduced.

## Design principles

- one authoritative record ID per item;
- explicit owner and status;
- source/criteria and version traceability;
- evidence links rather than unsupported assertions;
- decisions separated from observations;
- reviewer/date/decision records for human gates;
- findings and remediation retained rather than overwritten;
- residual risk and exceptions visible;
- material change triggers reassessment;
- multilingual terminology controlled without representing localized material as an authorized standards translation;
- CSV structures remain machine-readable and spreadsheet-friendly.

## Assurance boundary

Filling in a template does not establish compliance, certification, conformity, security, trustworthy-AI achievement, or an audit opinion. The records are evidence-management instruments that support accountable human decisions.
