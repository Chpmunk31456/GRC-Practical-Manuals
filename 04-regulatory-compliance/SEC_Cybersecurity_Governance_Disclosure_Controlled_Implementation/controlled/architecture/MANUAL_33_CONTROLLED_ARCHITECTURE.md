## Chapter design requirements

Every chapter must separate: (a) binding SEC rule/form requirement, (b) dated staff or Commission guidance, (c) petition or proposed change that is not effective law, (d) enforcement or case-specific material, and (e) issuer-specific implementation practice. The manual must not make issuer-specific materiality determinations or provide legal advice.

Materiality, filing timing, foreign-private-issuer treatment, delayed-disclosure eligibility, and amendment obligations are legal/disclosure decision points requiring documented judgment and appropriate counsel involvement.

## Evidence-object model

Reusable evidence objects should include: applicability assessment; governance charter; board oversight record; management accountability matrix; cyber-risk register; materiality analysis; incident escalation record; Item 1.05 decision memo; filing-timeline record; delayed-disclosure decision record; disclosure draft/review evidence; Item 106 annual-disclosure workpaper; FPI applicability record; Inline XBRL validation evidence; disclosure-controls testing; legal/IR/security coordination record; third-party incident analysis; privilege/confidentiality record; remediation log; assurance record; release-source verification record.

## Localization and review boundary

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions must preserve U.S. securities-law terminology, materiality concepts, filing-form distinctions, timing language, and non-legal-advice boundaries. Legal/regulatory meaning, localization semantics, editorial quality, and exact-PDF rendered accessibility/visual review must be planned before final candidate reconciliation. Automated checks may support but must not replace documented genuine-human judgment where required.
