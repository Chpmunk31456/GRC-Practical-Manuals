# Manual 47 — EU AI Act Training & Operationalization

**Status:** PRE-STAGE — FIRST SPECIALIST TRAINING AFTER MANUAL 46  
**Source baseline:** 31 August 2026  
**Dependency:** Manual 01 — EU AI Act GRC Compliance Manual

## Purpose

Manual 47 is the first specialist training manual after the Universal AI Governance Foundation. It teaches the EU AI Act as an operational governance program rather than duplicating the repository's detailed Manual 01 compliance manual.

The sequence is intentional:

**Manual 46 Universal Foundation → Manual 47 EU AI Act Training → later framework and specialist manuals.**

## Training objectives

Learners should be able to:

1. Explain the EU AI Act risk-based regulatory model.
2. Distinguish prohibited, high-risk, transparency-regulated, GPAI and lower-risk uses.
3. Identify provider, deployer, importer, distributor and value-chain responsibilities.
4. Determine when AI literacy, transparency, high-risk and GPAI obligations apply.
5. Translate legal requirements into governance controls, ownership and evidence.
6. Integrate EU AI Act obligations into an enterprise AI lifecycle.
7. Explain current application and enforcement dates accurately.
8. Build an operational compliance roadmap without treating regulation as a checklist-only exercise.

## Current timing control

The manual will preserve a dated application timeline and refresh it immediately before release. It will distinguish statutory text, later amending legislation, Commission guidance, codes, standards and implementation material.

## Relationship to Manual 01

Manual 01 remains the detailed EU AI Act GRC compliance reference. Manual 47 is the structured training and operationalization layer built on top of it. This avoids duplication while making the EU AI Act the first focused learning module after the universal foundation.
