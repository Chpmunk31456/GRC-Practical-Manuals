# Manual 08 — Vendor and Third-Party Risk Lifecycle

**Status:** Controlled implementation intake, stacked behind Manual 07.

**Controlled source language:** English (`en`)

**Planned publication languages:** English, neutral Latin American Spanish (`es-419`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`)

**Author and accountable human creator:** Alberto “Al” Leiva

## Purpose

Manual 08 will provide a practical, risk-based third-party lifecycle for vendors, cloud/SaaS providers, outsourced services, AI/model/API suppliers, subprocessors, component providers, and other external dependencies. It will connect intake, due diligence, contracting, monitoring, issue management, reassessment, and offboarding to traceable evidence and accountable decisions.

## Controlled source baseline

The intake is anchored to controlled source IDs including:

- `nist-sp-800-161-r1-upd1` — cybersecurity supply-chain risk management;
- `nist-sp-1305-c-scrm` — CSF 2.0 C-SCRM quick-start guidance;
- `nist-csf-2` — enterprise cybersecurity governance/risk context;
- `nist-ai-rmf-1-0` and `nist-ai-600-1` — AI and Generative AI supplier/ecosystem risk;
- `iso-iec-27001-2022` — management-system control context.

## Lifecycle model

Manual 08 will cover:

1. intake and vendor/service classification;
2. criticality and inherent-risk assessment;
3. evidence-based due diligence;
4. risk decision and approval;
5. contractual/security/privacy/resilience requirements;
6. onboarding and access/data-flow controls;
7. continuous monitoring and evidence refresh;
8. issue, incident, exception, and change management;
9. renewal/reassessment;
10. termination, access revocation, data return/deletion, and evidence closure.

The manual will explicitly address **AI suppliers, model/API providers, cloud dependencies, subprocessors, and fourth parties** rather than limiting TPRM to questionnaire-based security review.

See [Manual 08 implementation paths](./MANUAL_08_IMPLEMENTATION_PATHS.md).

## Assurance boundary

A completed questionnaire, certification, or contract clause is evidence input—not proof that supplier risk is eliminated. Conclusions must reflect current evidence, scope, criticality, unresolved findings, fourth-party visibility, and residual risk.
