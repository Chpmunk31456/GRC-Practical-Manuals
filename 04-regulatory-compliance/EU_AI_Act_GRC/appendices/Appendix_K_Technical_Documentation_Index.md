## 4. Evidence quality check

For each indexed item, confirm:

- current and approved;
- attributable to an accountable owner;
- complete for the deployed version;
- internally consistent;
- supported by source evidence;
- protected from unauthorized alteration;
- retrievable within required timeframes;
- retained under the applicable schedule.
