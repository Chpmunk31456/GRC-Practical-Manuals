# Chapter 76 — Supplier Change Notification and Reassessment

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 76 draft language.

## Requirement

Material supplier changes must be disclosed early enough for the customer to assess legal, operational, security, privacy, performance, classification, and role consequences before the changed AI system or model is used in production.

## Plain-English explanation

AI services can change without a traditional software release. Model substitutions, silent retraining, new data sources, altered safeguards, revised usage policies, new subprocessors, and changed hosting locations may affect compliance even where the product name remains the same.

## Change-notification scope

Require advance notice, where feasible, for:

1. model or system replacement;
2. material retraining, fine-tuning, or architecture changes;
3. intended-purpose, supported-use, or prohibited-use changes;
4. data-source, provenance, retention, or processing changes;
5. performance, limitation, safety, bias, or security changes;
6. logging, human-oversight, transparency, or explainability changes;
7. hosting, jurisdiction, ownership, or subcontractor changes;
8. conformity, registration, regulatory, or classification changes;
9. serious incidents, significant vulnerabilities, or corrective actions;
10. discontinuation, end-of-support, or material commercial restrictions.

## GlobalWay example

A supplier proposes replacing the underlying GPAI model used in GlobalWay’s traveler-assistance service. GlobalWay blocks automatic activation until it reviews updated instructions, testing, data processing, transparency, security, role allocation, and downstream impact.

## Control activity

Contracts and technical integration controls must require material-change notification and provide a review window. Production activation must be gated until required reassessment, testing, approval, documentation, and user communication are complete.

## Evidence

- contractual notification clause;
- supplier change notice;
- impact and role assessment;
- classification review;
- regression, safety, bias, and security testing;
- updated instructions and documentation;
- approval or rejection decision;
- post-change monitoring results.

## Audit test

Select a sample of supplier-driven changes. Confirm that notice was timely, the change was assessed before activation, required evidence and testing were obtained, approvals matched risk, and post-change monitoring verified expected outcomes.
