# Manual 42 — Canada Privacy Controlled Implementation

Controlled implementation guidance for Canadian private-sector privacy governance. PIPEDA, applicable provincial/private-sector laws, sector rules, and regulator guidance are maintained as distinct source layers; applicability must be decided from the organization, province, activity, sector, employment context, and interprovincial/international facts.

Controlled editions are under `controlled/en`, `controlled/es-419`, and `controlled/pt-BR`. Exact frozen DOCX/PDF publication artifacts will be retained under `publication/<locale>` after provenance verification. Publication requires source reverification, trilingual QA, candidate provenance, document/PDF inspection, exact-byte staging, workflow-security validation, predecessor publication, and registry reconciliation.
