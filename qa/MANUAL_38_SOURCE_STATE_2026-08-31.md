# Manual 38 — FERPA release-time source state

**State:** downstream prepublication source refresh.  
**Verified baseline:** Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g, implemented by **34 CFR Part 99**. The U.S. Department of Education Student Privacy Policy Office continues to identify 34 CFR Part 99 as the current FERPA regulations.

## Controlled boundary
- Statute, 34 CFR Part 99, Department of Education guidance/FAQs, contractual obligations, state student-privacy laws, and organization-specific practices remain separate source layers.
- Do not present Department guidance as regulation or silently import state-law duties into FERPA.
- Applicability, school-official exceptions, consent exceptions, directory information, record-access rights, redisclosure, studies/audit exceptions, and vendor controls require fact-specific analysis.

## Release requirement
Reverify the Department of Education FERPA page and current e-CFR Part 99 at candidate freeze. Controlled translations are project translations, not official Department translations.
