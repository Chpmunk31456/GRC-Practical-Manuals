# Visual Learning Standard / Estándar de aprendizaje visual / Padrão de aprendizagem visual

**Project rule:** Purposeful, accessible graphics are part of the learning design of every practical manual. They help readers recognize key concepts, remember relationships, make decisions, and verify implementation. Graphics do not replace the controlled text, authoritative sources, or professional judgment.

**Regla del proyecto:** Los gráficos útiles y accesibles forman parte del diseño de aprendizaje de cada manual práctico. Ayudan a reconocer conceptos clave, recordar relaciones, tomar decisiones y verificar la implementación. Los gráficos no sustituyen el texto controlado, las fuentes oficiales ni el juicio profesional.

**Regra do projeto:** Gráficos úteis e acessíveis fazem parte do desenho de aprendizagem de cada manual prático. Eles ajudam a reconhecer conceitos-chave, lembrar relações, tomar decisões e verificar a implementação. Os gráficos não substituem o texto controlado, as fontes oficiais nem o julgamento profissional.

## 1. When a graphic is required / Cuándo se requiere / Quando é necessário

Use a graphic when it materially improves understanding of at least one of these relationships:

- decision or classification routing;
- implementation workflow or lifecycle;
- control, obligation, or framework mapping;
- timeline, sequence, hierarchy, or data flow; or
- evidence-and-assurance chain.

Use prose, a list, or a table when it communicates the idea more accurately. Do not add decorative graphics that create visual noise without teaching a concept.

Utilice un gráfico cuando mejore de manera importante la comprensión de una ruta de decisión o clasificación, un flujo o ciclo de implementación, un mapeo de controles u obligaciones, una cronología, secuencia, jerarquía, flujo de datos o una cadena de evidencia y aseguramiento. Use prosa, listas o tablas cuando sean más precisas. No agregue gráficos decorativos que no enseñen un concepto.

Use um gráfico quando ele melhorar de forma relevante a compreensão de uma rota de decisão ou classificação, um fluxo ou ciclo de implementação, um mapeamento de controles ou obrigações, uma linha do tempo, sequência, hierarquia, fluxo de dados ou uma cadeia de evidências e asseguração. Use prosa, listas ou tabelas quando forem mais precisas. Não inclua gráficos decorativos que não ensinem um conceito.

## 2. Placement and learning purpose / Ubicación y propósito / Posicionamento e propósito

Place each graphic immediately after the text that introduces the concept or before the action it supports. Give it a short descriptive heading. One graphic should teach one primary relationship; split dense diagrams into smaller views.

Each manual should normally include visual memory aids at its highest-value learning points, such as:

1. **Route:** What applies and which path should the reader follow?
2. **Act:** What sequence turns the requirement into operating practice?
3. **Verify:** How do controls produce evidence, testing, findings, and remediation?

The number of graphics is determined by the material, not by a decorative quota. A manual-specific quality baseline may set a minimum where the learning design requires it.

## 3. Accuracy and assurance boundaries / Precisión y límites / Precisão e limites

- A graphic must not oversimplify a legal or technical obligation into a misleading rule.
- Distinguish binding requirements from guidance, examples, and recommended practice.
- Show uncertainty, exceptions, reassessment, and escalation where they affect the decision.
- Keep the detailed explanation, citations, applicability limits, and disclaimer in text.
- Treat every diagram as controlled content subject to source review and change management.

## 4. Accessibility and localization / Accesibilidad y localización / Acessibilidade e localização

Every informative graphic must:

- have an accessible text explanation directly beneath it;
- use meaningful, concise labels and a logical reading order;
- communicate meaning without relying on color alone;
- maintain readable contrast and text size in derived formats;
- be understandable when printed in grayscale;
- avoid flashing, unnecessary animation, and visual clutter; and
- be fully localized in each published language, including labels and explanation.

The accessible explanation must describe the relationship or decision shown—not merely say that a diagram exists. If a format cannot render the graphic, the explanation must still preserve its essential meaning.

Cada gráfico informativo debe incluir una explicación textual accesible, etiquetas localizadas, orden lógico de lectura y significado que no dependa solo del color. La explicación debe describir la relación o decisión representada.

Cada gráfico informativo deve incluir uma explicação textual acessível, rótulos localizados, ordem lógica de leitura e significado que não dependa apenas de cores. A explicação deve descrever a relação ou decisão representada.

## 5. Source control and quality review / Control de fuentes y revisión / Controle de fontes e revisão

Visuals must be stored with the manual source and reviewed in the pull request that changes the related text. Quality review must confirm:

- factual and legal alignment with the controlled text;
- valid syntax and successful rendering;
- consistency between the graphic and its accessible explanation;
- semantic parity across English, Latin American Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese editions;
- working headings, links, and reading order; and
- regeneration and visual inspection of affected PDF, Word, or other release formats.

Automated checks may validate presence, syntax, localization, and parity, but human review remains responsible for accuracy, clarity, accessibility, and publication approval.

## 6. Preferred formats / Formatos preferidos / Formatos preferidos

- Use a **table** for exact comparisons, responsibility mappings, or control crosswalks.
- Use a **flowchart** for decisions, feedback loops, and implementation workflows.
- Use a **timeline** for staged obligations or change over time.
- Use a **hierarchy or architecture view** for ownership, system boundaries, and dependencies.
- Use charts only when quantitative data supports a meaningful comparison, trend, or composition.

Prefer source-controlled, text-based diagrams such as Mermaid when they can represent the concept accurately and accessibly. Use images only when a diagram, table, or chart cannot communicate the idea effectively; retain editable source files and provide equivalent accessible text.
