# Authoritative Source Register

**Workstream:** GRC Policy Template Toolkit  
**Source-control date:** 1 August 2026

## Primary official sources

1. **NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0** — governance, policy, roles, oversight, supply-chain risk, protection, detection, response, and recovery outcomes.
2. **NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, Release 5.2.0** — control requirements and enhancement concepts used to structure policy topics and accountability.
3. **NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3** — incident-response recommendations aligned to CSF 2.0.
4. **NIST Privacy Framework 1.0** — privacy-risk governance and data-processing outcomes.
5. **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 with Amendment 1:2024** — information-security management system requirements and climate-change amendment.
6. **ISO/IEC 27002:2022** — information-security control guidance.
7. **ISO 22301:2019 with Amendment 1:2024** — business-continuity management requirements and climate-change amendment.
8. **CIS Critical Security Controls v8.1** — prioritized implementation guidance relevant to access, vulnerability, logging, incident, asset, and supplier policies.
9. **Applicable official laws, regulations, regulator guidance, contracts, and records-retention requirements** — organization-specific and jurisdiction-specific; not reproduced or presumed by these templates.

## Source-use controls

- These templates summarize control and governance concepts; they do not reproduce copyrighted standards text.
- A policy owner must validate legal, labor, privacy, regulatory, contractual, and sector-specific requirements before adoption.
- Framework mappings are informative and do not prove compliance or certification.
- Source status and edition must be rechecked before a formal organizational release.
- The web search service was unavailable at workstream initialization. No claim is made that a new web revalidation occurred at that moment; the toolkit uses the repository’s dated official-source baseline already verified on 1 August 2026.
