## Screening

For each Article 5 point, record Yes/No/Uncertain, exact legal element, evidence, exception, reviewer, and decision.

1. Subliminal, purposefully manipulative, or deceptive techniques causing or likely to cause significant harm.
2. Exploitation of vulnerabilities due to age, disability, or specific social/economic situation.
3. Social scoring and detrimental or disproportionate treatment.
4. Individual criminal-offence risk prediction based solely on profiling or personality traits, subject to the statutory framework.
5. Untargeted scraping of facial images to create or expand facial-recognition databases.
6. Emotion recognition in workplaces or educational institutions, subject only to narrow statutory exceptions.
7. Biometric categorisation inferring protected or sensitive characteristics, subject only to narrow statutory treatment.
8. Real-time remote biometric identification in publicly accessible spaces for law-enforcement purposes, subject to the narrow statutory conditions.
9. Non-consensual sexually explicit/intimate content and child sexual abuse material prohibitions introduced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, using the applicable 2 December 2026 date.
10. Proxy, repurposing, workflow, agent, prompt, plugin, or configuration routes that indirectly produce a prohibited outcome.
