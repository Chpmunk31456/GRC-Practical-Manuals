## Limitations

This is a Codex visual inspection, not human publication approval, native-language editorial review, accessibility or assistive-technology testing, print-production proofing, legal review, standards certification, or audit assurance. DOCX files passed structural ZIP validation; LibreOffice DOCX rendering was unavailable in this execution environment and is not represented as completed.
