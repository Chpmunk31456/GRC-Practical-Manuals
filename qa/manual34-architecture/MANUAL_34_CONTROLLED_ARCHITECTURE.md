## Chapter design requirements

Every chapter must distinguish: (a) project-authored implementation guidance, (b) ISO/IEC 27701:2025 requirements or concepts referenced without reproducing protected normative text, (c) ISO/IEC 27001/27002 integration points, (d) jurisdiction-specific legal or regulatory requirements, and (e) organization-specific implementation choices. The manual must not imply that a control or process alone establishes legal compliance.

The architecture must not silently reuse withdrawn ISO/IEC 27701:2019 clause structures, control numbering, dependency assumptions, or certification statements. Any legacy material must be explicitly reconciled to Edition 2 before inclusion.

## Evidence-object model

Reusable evidence objects should include: PIMS scope statement; interested-party register; privacy requirements inventory; policy approval record; role/accountability matrix; privacy risk register; objectives and metrics register; processing and data-flow records; controller/processor responsibility matrix; data-subject request record; privacy-by-design review; retention/disposal schedule; transparency/notice record; supplier/subprocessor assessment; transfer-impact record where applicable; security-integration mapping; incident/breach record; monitoring results; audit plan and findings; management-review minutes; corrective-action record; continual-improvement backlog; legal/regulatory mapping; certification-readiness evidence index; release-source verification record.

## Localization and review boundary

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR editions must preserve privacy-management terminology, controller/processor distinctions, evidence expectations, management-system concepts, certification-readiness boundaries, and the fact that project translations are not ISO-authorized translations. Terminology and semantic review must be bound to exact frozen source identities before publication.

## Release gates

Before publication: authoritative-source reverification; legacy-2019 reconciliation; controlled-English completion; exact English freeze; controlled es-419/pt-BR localization; structure/parity QA; reproducible six-binary DOCX/PDF candidate; workflow security; release-package QA; SHA-256 provenance; durable staging; exact-hash substantive review where required; predecessor publication through Manual 33; final catalog and work-product release-registry reconciliation.
