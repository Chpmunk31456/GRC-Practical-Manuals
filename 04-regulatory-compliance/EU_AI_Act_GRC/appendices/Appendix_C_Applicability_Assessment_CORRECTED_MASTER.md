## 5. Risk and obligation screening

Determine whether the matter involves:

- a prohibited practice;
- Article 6(1)/Annex I high-risk treatment;
- Article 6(2)/Annex III high-risk treatment;
- Article 6(3) exception analysis and the profiling caveat;
- transparency obligations;
- GPAI-provider or systemic-risk obligations;
- conformity, registration, documentation, post-market monitoring, incident, or corrective-action duties;
- independently applicable privacy, employment, consumer, security, product-safety, or sector obligations.

## 7. Outcome

Select one and explain:

- In scope;
- Partially in scope;
- Out of scope;
- Excluded or specially treated, with documented conditions;
- Uncertain — legal review required.

## 8. Review triggers

Reassess after changes in legal entity, jurisdiction, market placement, deployment, output use, branding, intended purpose, model, provider, integration, functionality, data, affected population, or applicable law.
