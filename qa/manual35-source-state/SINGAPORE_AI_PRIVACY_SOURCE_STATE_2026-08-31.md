# Manual 35 — Singapore AI & Privacy Governance source-state decision

## Controlled baseline

Manual 35 is repurposed from the earlier HITRUST preparation lane to a Singapore AI and privacy governance implementation manual.

The binding privacy baseline is Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and current Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) regulations, advisory guidelines, enforcement materials, and breach-notification guidance. The manual must distinguish statutory/regulatory obligations from non-binding guidance and organisation-specific implementation practice.

The AI-governance layer uses current Singapore IMDA/PDPC and AI Verify Foundation materials, including the Model AI Governance Framework for Traditional AI, the Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI, AI Verify / implementation guidance, and the Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI. These AI governance frameworks are guidance and assurance resources, not standalone statutes.

## 2026 release-time facts

- PDPA obligations include accountability, notification, consent, purpose limitation, accuracy, protection, retention limitation, transfer limitation, access/correction, and mandatory data-breach notification.
- Data portability must not be represented as currently operative unless release-time verification confirms that the necessary regulations have taken effect.
- Notifiable data breaches must be assessed under current PDPA/PDPC criteria. Current PDPC guidance states that where notification to the PDPC is required, it must be made as soon as practicable and no later than three calendar days.
- Singapore's Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI was launched in January 2026 and updated in May 2026 with additional case studies and practices, including multi-agent systems, third-party agents, and automation-bias controls.

## Controlled boundary

The manual must keep separate: (a) PDPA statutory/regulatory obligations, (b) PDPC advisory and enforcement guidance, (c) IMDA/PDPC/AI Verify voluntary AI governance frameworks and testing resources, (d) sector-specific or contractual overlays, and (e) organisation-specific implementation practice. It must not imply that following a voluntary AI framework by itself establishes PDPA compliance, regulatory approval, certification, or legal sufficiency.

## Supersession of prior Manual 35 lane

Earlier HITRUST CSF Manual 35 source-state and architecture records remain in repository history as superseded preparation evidence. They are not the current Manual 35 roadmap and must not be promoted to candidate or publication under series order 35 unless the roadmap is explicitly changed again.
