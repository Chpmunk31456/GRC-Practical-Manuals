# Manual 48 — NIST AI RMF 1.0 + NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Operational Guide

**Status:** PRE-STAGE — +2 SOURCE/ARCHITECTURE LANE  
**Source baseline:** 31 August 2026

## Objective

Turn NIST AI RMF 1.0 and the NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile into an enterprise governance operating guide, control/evidence model and interview training resource.

## Controlled architecture

1. AI RMF purpose, audience and voluntary nature.
2. Trustworthiness characteristics and lifecycle framing.
3. GOVERN — governance culture, policy, accountability and risk tolerance.
4. MAP — context, use, actors, impacts and dependencies.
5. MEASURE — testing, evaluation, verification, validation and uncertainty.
6. MANAGE — prioritization, response, monitoring and communication.
7. AI RMF Playbook operationalization.
8. TEVV and assurance evidence.
9. NIST AI 600-1 GenAI risk landscape.
10. GenAI risk treatment mapped to GOVERN/MAP/MEASURE/MANAGE.
11. RAG, model/provider, data and deployment considerations.
12. Security, privacy, harmful bias, content integrity and human-AI risks.
13. AI inventory and risk-tier integration.
14. Third-party and acquisition governance.
15. Monitoring, incident and change/revalidation triggers.
16. Executive metrics and KRIs.
17. Crosswalk to ISO/IEC 42001.
18. Crosswalk to EU AI Act.
19. Crosswalk to Singapore AI Verify/Agentic AI.
20. Interview scenarios and concise executive answers.
21. Control/evidence catalogue.
22. Audit-ready implementation checklist.

## Currency control

NIST's official AI RMF program page states that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised as of August 2026. This manual therefore treats AI RMF 1.0 as the current published framework while requiring a fresh source check immediately before release and clearly distinguishing published material from drafts/concept notes.
