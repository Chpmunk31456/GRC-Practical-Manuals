# Manual 45 — Enterprise Compliance Training Capstone release-time source state

**State:** downstream prepublication source refresh.  
**Controlled baseline:** Manual 45 is an integration/capstone manual, not an independent regulatory standard. Its authoritative inputs are the then-published Manuals 01–44 plus explicitly identified organization policies, role requirements, legal/regulatory overlays, and training evidence requirements.

## Controlled boundary
- The capstone must not convert every obligation from every preceding manual into a universal enterprise requirement.
- Training content must be role-, jurisdiction-, system-, and applicability-aware; legal requirements, voluntary frameworks, standards, contracts, internal policy, and recommended practices remain distinct.
- Completion records, competency checks, acknowledgements, remediation, exceptions, refresh triggers, metrics, and evidence retention must be designed as auditable evidence objects.

## Release requirement
Manual 45 cannot freeze until Manuals 01–44 publication states and final titles are reconciled. Candidate generation must use an exact dependency snapshot and record the predecessor commit identities used to build the capstone.
