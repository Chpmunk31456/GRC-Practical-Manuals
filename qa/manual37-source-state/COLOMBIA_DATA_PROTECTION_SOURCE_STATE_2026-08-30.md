# Manual 37 — Colombia Data Protection / Habeas Data Authoritative Source State

**Manual:** 37 — Colombia Data Protection / Habeas Data Controlled Implementation  
**State:** downstream authoritative-source gate only; publication state unchanged  
**Verified:** 2026-08-30/31 UTC against current SIC and Colombian government materials

## Controlled baseline

The primary general personal-data protection baseline is **Ley Estatutaria 1581 de 2012**, together with the applicable regulatory provisions consolidated in **Decreto 1074 de 2015**. The Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC), through its Delegatura para la Protección de Datos Personales, is the national data-protection authority for this regime.

The manual must preserve the constitutional habeas-data context and distinguish the general Ley 1581 regime from sector-specific or special regimes. It must not present SIC guidance, circulars, enforcement decisions, sector requirements, or organization practice as if they were the statutory text itself.

## Source-layer boundaries

Manual 37 must separately identify:

1. constitutional habeas-data/privacy rights;
2. Ley 1581 de 2012 statutory requirements;
3. binding regulatory provisions under Decreto 1074 de 2015 and other applicable decrees;
4. binding SIC circulars/instructions within scope;
5. SIC guidance, FAQs, enforcement materials and RNBD operational guidance;
6. special/sectoral data regimes where applicable;
7. organization-specific policies, procedures, risk decisions, contracts and evidence.

## Current-law / reform boundary

Official SIC material in 2025–2026 confirms that **Ley 1581 de 2012 remains the current general framework**, while legislative proposals seek to modernize it. Proposed reform text must remain a change-watch item and must not be treated as binding law unless enacted and effective.

SIC instructions issued in 2025–2026 also demonstrate that new context-specific obligations or interpretations can arise through circulars while the statutory baseline remains Ley 1581 and Decreto 1074. Release-time verification must therefore include current SIC circulars relevant to any control claim.

## Implementation boundaries

The controlled manual must cover only supported obligations and clearly scope controller/responsible and processor/encargado roles, authorization and lawful treatment, privacy notices/policies, data-subject consultation/complaint rights, security safeguards, incident response where applicable, transfers/transmissions, RNBD obligations where applicable, vendor governance, retention/deletion, sensitive and children/adolescent data, accountability, and evidence.

No blanket claim of universal RNBD registration, transfer restriction, consent requirement, breach-reporting duty, or sector applicability may be made without current source support and applicability analysis.
