## Current framework-state reconciliation

As of 1 September 2026, the controlled training baseline treats:

- the 2020 Model AI Governance Framework as the foundational organisational-governance reference;
- the 2024 Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI as the GenAI ecosystem extension;
- the AI Verify Testing Framework as the assurance/testing framework, with IMDA material identifying a 2025 update;
- the Agentic AI Model AI Governance Framework as the current agentic-governance extension, with IMDA's current ecosystem page identifying the May 2026 update as v1.5;
- the April 2026 ISO/IEC 42119-8 work as a proposed international standardisation effort, not a final published international standard unless separately verified before release.

## Agentic AI control dimensions

The current IMDA framework uses four operational dimensions:

1. assess and bound risks and capabilities upfront;
2. make humans meaningfully accountable at significant checkpoints;
3. implement technical controls and processes throughout the lifecycle; and
4. enable responsible end-user behaviour through transparency and education.

The May 2026 update adds practical case studies and additional best practices, including multi-agent systems, third-party agents and automation-bias risk.

## Controlled source note

The repository's central authoritative-source validator may not yet allow `www.imda.gov.sg`. Do not bypass the validator or weaken source controls. Treat any allowlist expansion as a separately reviewed shared-control change.

## Release-source rules

- Reverify current IMDA framework versions immediately before release.
- Distinguish Singapore governance guidance, assurance/testing tools and proposed standards from binding legal obligations.
- Verify any AI Verify testing claim against the current official IMDA/AI Verify material.
- Do not claim that AI Verify testing, framework alignment, or an internal assessment constitutes certification or legal compliance.
- Preserve explicit non-equivalence when mapping Singapore guidance to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF or other regimes.
