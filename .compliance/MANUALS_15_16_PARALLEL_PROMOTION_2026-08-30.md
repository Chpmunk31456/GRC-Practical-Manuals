# Manuals 15–16 — Parallel Promotion Controls

**Purpose:** keep downstream work moving while Manual 14 remains sequentially front-of-line.

## Manual 15 — SOC 2

Promote from baseline preflight into controlled-build preparation with these required workstreams:

1. Verify current AICPA Trust Services Criteria and applicable SOC reporting guidance at build/release time.
2. Preserve copyright boundaries: do not reproduce protected criteria text beyond permitted use.
3. Separate readiness/implementation guidance from CPA attestation, examination, and opinion requirements.
4. Define 32-chapter controlled architecture around governance, scope/system description, trust-service categories, risk/control design, evidence, operating effectiveness, vendor dependencies, change management, exceptions, remediation, management representations, and readiness-to-attestation transition.
5. Define evidence-object schema reusable from Manuals 13–14 but tailored to control design vs operating-effectiveness testing.
6. Pre-stage es-419 and pt-BR terminology architecture without claiming authorized AICPA translation status.
7. Define publication-candidate QA, rendered accessibility/visual QA, provenance, checksum, durable-artifact, and release-registry requirements before full build begins.

## Manual 16 — ISO/IEC 27001 & 27002

Promote from baseline preflight into controlled-build preparation with these required workstreams:

1. Verify ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 27002:2022, amendments/corrigenda, and relevant ISO/IAF certification guidance at build/release time.
2. Preserve ISO copyright boundaries and avoid reproducing protected normative text.
3. Separate implementation guidance from accredited certification requirements and certification-body decisions.
4. Define 32-chapter architecture around context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement, ISMS risk treatment, Statement of Applicability governance, control themes, evidence, internal audit, management review, corrective action, certification readiness, and maturity.
5. Explicitly track the relationship between ISO/IEC 27001 requirements and ISO/IEC 27002 implementation guidance without implying one-to-one legal equivalence.
6. Pre-stage es-419 and pt-BR terminology architecture and source-boundary controls.
7. Define exact-head QA, rendered artifact QA, provenance/checksum, durable staging, and release-registry reconciliation before full build begins.

## Parallel rule

Manual 15 may enter full controlled build while Manual 14 is in QA/publication preparation. Manual 16 may enter full controlled build while Manual 15 is under QA. Neither may be published ahead of its predecessor. A blocker on one lane does not freeze safe work on the others.
