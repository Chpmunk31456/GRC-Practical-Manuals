## Chapter design requirements

Every chapter must separate: (a) binding regulatory requirement, (b) dated DFS explanatory guidance or resource material, (c) organization-specific implementation practice, and (d) evidence expected to support an implementation decision. The controlled English master must not reproduce protected or unnecessarily lengthy regulatory text; requirements are to be paraphrased with authoritative citations and precise section references where appropriate.

Applicability, exemption, Class A, notice, certification, filing, and compliance-date statements are legal/regulatory decision points. The manual must not infer entity status or legal conclusions from generic facts. Any implementation example must be explicitly non-authoritative.

## Evidence-object model

The controlled build should produce reusable evidence objects including: applicability assessment; exemption determination; Class A determination; CISO mandate/report; cybersecurity program charter; policy register; risk assessment; asset inventory; access review; MFA exception record; vulnerability/penetration-test evidence; encryption decision; monitoring/log review; third-party risk record; training evidence; incident record; Superintendent-notification analysis; extortion-payment decision record; annual filing evidence; governing-body minutes/attestation evidence; remediation log; exception/risk-acceptance record; metrics/assurance pack; release-source verification record.

## Localization and review boundary

Controlled es-419 and pt-BR localizations must preserve New York legal terms, entity-status concepts, reporting thresholds/timelines, and non-legal-advice boundaries. Legal/regulatory meaning, localization semantics, editorial quality, and exact-PDF rendered accessibility/visual review must be planned before the final candidate reaches publication reconciliation. No automated check may substitute for a genuinely required human judgment record.

## Release gates
