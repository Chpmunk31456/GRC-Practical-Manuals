## Source-control rule

Manual 46 is framework-neutral. Sources support universal governance concepts and current legal/contextual references, but no single jurisdiction or framework defines the manual's structure.

Before release:
- Refresh all time-sensitive regulatory dates.
- Distinguish binding law from standards, voluntary frameworks, codes and guidance.
- Do not claim that alignment with a voluntary framework proves legal compliance or certification.
- Do not reproduce protected ISO text.
- Record source verification evidence and date.
