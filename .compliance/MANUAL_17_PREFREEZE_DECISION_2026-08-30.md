# Manual 17 — Pre-Freeze Source Decision

**Manual:** 17 — NIST Privacy Framework Controlled Implementation  
**Verification date:** 2026-08-30  
**State:** pre-freeze source decision; not publication authorization

## Verified authority state

The official NIST Privacy Framework 1.1 project page continues to identify Privacy Framework 1.1 as **Coming soon** and the April 14, 2025 release as the **Initial Public Draft (IPD)**. The page states that the IPD public-comment period closed June 13, 2025 and was last updated April 1, 2026.

Authoritative source:
- https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/new-projects/privacy-framework-version-11

## Controlled decision for ongoing construction

Until NIST publishes Privacy Framework 1.1 as final, Manual 17 construction shall use **Privacy Framework 1.0 as the stable normative baseline**. PF 1.1 IPD material may be used only as explicitly labeled draft-change intelligence and migration-planning input. It shall not be presented as final NIST content, a certification requirement, or a binding control requirement.

This decision permits controlled-English construction, evidence-model development, terminology preparation, graphics/tooling preparation, and QA planning to continue without waiting for PF 1.1 final.

## Freeze boundary

The English master may not be declared release-frozen until one of these states is evidenced against the exact candidate:

1. NIST publishes PF 1.1 final and the candidate is reconciled to that final publication; or
2. the candidate explicitly scopes itself to PF 1.0, excludes PF 1.1 draft material from normative treatment, and passes the required source/copyright/semantic QA for that scope.

Localization remains behind the exact controlled-English freeze. Publication remains fail-closed behind trilingual parity, rendered/accessibility QA, security/provenance checks, durable artifact staging, exact-head release QA, and catalog/release-registry reconciliation.

## Anti-halt consequence

PF 1.1's pending status is not a reason to idle the pipeline. Continue all safe Manual 17 pre-freeze work and keep Manual 18 and later manuals advancing through source verification, architecture, evidence design, terminology, QA preparation, and provenance preparation.
