# Manual 15 — SOC 2 Active QA Lane

**Status:** ACTIVE QA IN PARALLEL / NOT PUBLICATION AUTHORIZATION
**Series order:** 15
**Front predecessor:** Manual 14

Manual 15 is promoted into an active QA lane while Manual 14 remains front-of-line for publication. This lane exists to eliminate idle cycle time without bypassing sequential release order.

## QA scope

1. Verify the controlled architecture covers security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy without reproducing copyrighted AICPA Trust Services Criteria text.
2. Verify the manual clearly distinguishes implementation/readiness guidance from CPA attestation, examination, opinion, and report issuance.
3. Verify control/evidence mappings are original implementation guidance and do not imply AICPA authorization or certification.
4. Verify 32-chapter architecture completeness, Essential / Structured / Enhanced implementation paths, evidence objects, ownership, frequency, test method, exception/remediation, and reassessment triggers.
5. Verify English controlling-source terminology and es-419 / pt-BR localization architecture.
6. Verify accessibility, graphics, link, metadata, heading/table and rendered-page requirements are pre-staged.
7. Verify publication package requirements: durable DOCX/PDF, page QA, SHA-256 checksums, provenance, source verification, exact-head workflow security, release manifest, catalog and registry reconciliation.
8. Fail closed on substantive scope, copyright, authority, artifact-integrity, or source-version defects.

## Parallel handoff rule

As soon as Manual 15 reaches a stable QA candidate, Manual 16 advances to active QA while Manual 15 proceeds toward artifact generation and pre-publication. Manual 17 simultaneously advances from preflight to controlled build. This stagger repeats downstream so the next manual is always one stage behind the front lane rather than waiting for publication.
