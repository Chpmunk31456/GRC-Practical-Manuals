## Current application-date reconciliation

The 2026 AI Omnibus amendment changes the high-risk application schedule and related transition dates. The controlled training package must use the amended primary-law dates rather than the original 2024 Article 113 schedule where they differ.

- Chapters I and II apply from 2 February 2025, subject to the specific 2026 amendment exceptions that apply from 2 December 2026.
- Articles 102 to 110 apply from 27 July 2026.
- Chapter III, Sections 1, 2 and 3 (except Article 6(5)) apply from 2 December 2027 for Article 6(2) / Annex III high-risk systems.
- The same Chapter III sections apply from 2 August 2028 for Article 6(1) / Annex I high-risk systems.
- Providers of covered synthetic-content systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 have the Article 50(2) transition date of 2 December 2026.

## Controlled legal interpretation rules

- Primary legislation controls where explanatory materials conflict.
- Verify consolidated/amending legislation before publication.
- Distinguish rules currently applicable from rules with future application dates.
- Distinguish binding obligations from Commission guidelines, FAQs and voluntary codes.
- Do not infer provider/deployer status solely from procurement labels; analyze the statutory role and actual activity.
- Do not state that framework alignment proves compliance.
- Reverify every application date immediately before publication.
