# EU AI Act Manual — Graphics Correction and Accessibility Register

## Purpose

This register controls the legal, editorial, visual, linguistic, and accessibility review of every graphic before publication.

## Known correction items

| Item | Issue | Required action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter 83 data-governance figure | Incorrectly associates data governance with Article 17 | Correct to Article 10 and revalidate all labels | Correction required |
| Chapter 83 human-oversight figures | Duplicate or overlapping content | Consolidate and retain one accurate Article 14 figure | Correction required |
| Chapter 83 retention statement | Presents ten-year retention as broadly universal | Replace with legally precise, role-specific, and context-qualified wording | Correction required |

| Composite Chapters 80–91 poster | Legally unreliable and insufficiently traceable | Do not publish; recreate only from approved chapter content | Rejected |

## Legal review checklist

- Does every article, annex, deadline, threshold, role, and retention statement match the final legal audit?
- Are conditions, exceptions, and role distinctions visible or properly qualified?
- Does the graphic avoid converting recommendations into legal mandates?
- Is the source basis retained in the figure metadata or production record?
- Can the graphic remain accurate if viewed separately from the chapter text?

## Editorial review checklist

- Are title, caption, labels, punctuation, capitalization, and terminology consistent?
- Is the figure readable at normal PDF zoom and on a standard printed page?
- Are labels concise enough to avoid overcrowding?
- Does the figure add information rather than merely repeat a paragraph?
- Is the visual hierarchy clear?

## Accessibility review checklist

- Is alt text concise but sufficient to convey the figure’s purpose and relationships?
- Is information available without relying on color alone?
- Is contrast adequate?
- Are reading order and grouping logical?
- Are abbreviations defined in the surrounding text or caption?
- Is decorative content excluded from meaningful alt text?

## Translation review checklist

- Translate only from an approved English figure.
- Preserve article and annex numbers.
- Reflow labels instead of shrinking text below readable size.
- Recheck accents, punctuation, capitalization, and line breaks.
- Ensure the translated caption and alt text match the translated visual.

## Publication gate

No figure may enter the final DOCX or PDF until legal, editorial, accessibility, and language statuses are approved and the final asset path is recorded.
