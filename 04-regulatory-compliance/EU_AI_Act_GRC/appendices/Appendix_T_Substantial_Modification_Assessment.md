Use this assessment to determine whether a change to an AI system may constitute a substantial modification that changes compliance responsibilities, legal role, classification, or conformity obligations.

## 3. Change impact

Assess impact on:

- legal role and accountable entity;
- high-risk or prohibited-practice analysis;
- conformity and registration status;
- technical documentation;
- risk and fundamental-rights assessments;
- data governance;
- human oversight;
- transparency and instructions;
- cybersecurity and resilience;
- post-market monitoring.

## 4. Provider transition analysis

Document whether the modifying entity may assume provider responsibilities and what obligations must be completed before deployment.

## 5. Evidence reviewed

- original technical documentation;
- approved change plan;
- model-change assessment;
- validation and regression results;
- legal analysis;
- vendor notices;
- risk and impact assessments;
- release and configuration records.

## 6. Conclusion

- [ ] Not a substantial modification
- [ ] Not substantial because it is within the approved change plan
- [ ] Potentially substantial; additional evidence required
- [ ] Substantial modification
- [ ] Escalated for external legal or conformity advice

### Rationale

Document the facts, assumptions, legal interpretation, and evidence.
