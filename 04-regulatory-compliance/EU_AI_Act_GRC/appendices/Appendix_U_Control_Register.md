# Appendix U — AI Control Register

## Purpose

Use this register to document the controls applied to AI systems, their ownership, frequency, evidence, testing, and remediation status.

## Minimum control domains

- governance and accountability;
- inventory and intake;
- applicability and classification;
- prohibited-practice screening;
- risk and fundamental-rights assessment;
- data governance;
- technical documentation;
- accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity;
- transparency and human oversight;
- logging and traceability;
- vendor and contract management;
- change and release management;
- post-market monitoring;
- incident and complaint management;
- conformity and regulatory readiness;
- training and AI literacy;
- audit, findings, and corrective action.

## Control-design fields

For each control, record:

- trigger;
- required inputs;
- procedure;
- decision criteria;
- escalation path;
- evidence generated;
- retention period;
- dependencies;
- failure consequences;
- compensating controls.

## Control status

Use one:

- Designed
- Implemented
- Operating
- Partially effective
- Ineffective
- Not applicable
- Retired

## Review

Review the register after legal changes, system changes, incidents, audits, or material control failures.
