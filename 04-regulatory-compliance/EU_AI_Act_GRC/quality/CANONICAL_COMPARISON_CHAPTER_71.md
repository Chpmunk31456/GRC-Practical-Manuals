## Canonical determination

`71_AI_Vendor_Due_Diligence_CORRECTED_MASTER.md` remains the controlling Chapter 71 source because it:

- matches the approved Chapter 71–79 canonical topic map;
- separates vendor due diligence from contract clauses, ongoing monitoring, dependency risk, and exit planning;
- uses the corrected-master legal-modality convention;
- avoids treating every procurement control as a direct statutory requirement;
- preserves the required chapter pattern of requirement, explanation, example, control, evidence, and audit test.

## Useful content identified in the earlier detailed draft

The earlier detailed draft contains useful operational material not fully represented in the concise corrected master:

This content should be preserved during publication assembly, but it must be legally and editorially reviewed before incorporation. It may be placed in Chapter 71, Appendix O, Appendix P, the control register, or the audit programme depending on subject.

## Legal and editorial issues requiring correction before reuse

1. Statements using `must`, `shall`, or fixed review frequencies must be classified as statutory duties, organisation-imposed controls, or recommended practices.
2. Vendor tier labels conflict between the two detailed drafts and require one approved enterprise methodology.
3. Fixed annual-review language is an internal control choice unless a specific legal source requires that frequency.
4. Contract clauses cannot be represented as universally mandated by the AI Act; they are mechanisms for allocating and evidencing responsibilities.
5. GDPR, accessibility, employment, consumer, intellectual-property, cybersecurity, and sector-law statements require separate legal sourcing where retained.
6. Detailed questionnaires and control tables should be moved to appendices or structured tools when their length would make Chapter 71 repetitive.

## Preservation decision

- **Canonical file:** retain as controlling Chapter 71 source.
- **Earlier detailed draft:** preserve until useful operational content is migrated and verified.
- **Combined draft:** preserve until Chapter 72–79 and Appendix O/P comparisons confirm that all nonduplicative content is retained.
- **Deletion:** not authorised in this batch.
