# Manual 42 — Canada Privacy release-time source state

**State:** downstream prepublication source refresh.  
**Verified baseline:** the federal **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)** remains an active private-sector privacy baseline. Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada materials updated in 2026 continue to present PIPEDA and its ten fair-information principles as current compliance requirements.

## Controlled boundary
- PIPEDA, federal regulations, provincial substantially-similar/private-sector privacy statutes, sector-specific laws, OPC guidance/findings, contracts, and organization-specific practices remain distinct.
- Do not present OPC guidance or findings as statutory text, and do not state that PIPEDA applies universally across all Canadian private-sector processing.
- Applicability, interprovincial/international processing, consent, meaningful consent, breach reporting/recordkeeping, employee information, children/age assurance, biometrics, and provincial overlays require fact-specific analysis.

## Release requirement
Reverify PIPEDA status, applicable provincial laws, and current OPC guidance at candidate freeze. Project translations are unofficial.
