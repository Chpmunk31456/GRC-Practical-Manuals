## Continuous parallel lanes

For every wave, use the production model continuously:

- current manual: release/QC lane;
- next manual: full-build lane;
- +2 manual: authoritative-source verification and architecture lane;
- +3 manual: research/intake lane;
- shared lane: graphics, terminology, reusable tooling, document generation, QA automation, provenance, security, accessibility and project-state reconciliation.

## Training-grade minimum for every full manual

A future manual is not promoted merely because prose exists. Each full manual should include, where applicable:

- applicability and scoping logic;
- authoritative-source register with source-state classification;
- Essential / Structured / Enhanced implementation paths;
- governance roles and accountability;
- implementation procedures and practitioner workflows;
- evidence requirements and example evidence schemas;
- audit / assessment procedures and sampling considerations;
- findings, exceptions, remediation and validation;
- metrics, maturity and continuous-improvement guidance;
- realistic scenarios, exercises and knowledge checks;
- accessible instructional graphics with text equivalents;
- crosswalk boundaries to related laws, regulations, standards and frameworks;
- controlled English source master before localization;
- `es-419` and `pt-BR` localization only after terminology controls are established;
- human semantic review of localization;
- rendered-document accessibility and visual review;
- DOCX/PDF publication QA;
- repository/workflow security review;
- exact-head release manifest, provenance and checksums;
- Final Human Release Approval for the exact final candidate.

## Fail-closed boundaries

Planning, AI-assisted drafting, source discovery, automated QA, document generation, crosswalk generation and machine translation are supporting production activities. None substitutes for required legal/technical judgment, human semantic review, rendered accessibility/visual review, competent assessment, or Final Human Release Approval.

Draft/proposed/under-development sources must never be represented as final requirements. Copyrighted standards must be summarized and mapped without reproducing protected normative text beyond lawful quotation limits.
