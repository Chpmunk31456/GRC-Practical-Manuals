# Legal and Citation Audit — Batch 08, Chapters 115–138

**Scope:** Assurance, enforcement, regulatory response, implementation roadmaps, maturity, and continuous improvement  
**Status:** Corrected English masters created; consolidation and whole-manual cross-reference review remain open

## Audit objective

Confirm that Chapters 115–138 distinguish binding EU AI Act duties from internal audit, assurance, implementation, maturity, and programme-management practices; use the current amended legal baseline; and avoid presenting recommended methods as direct statutory prescriptions.

## Legal baseline

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended.
- Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 where applicable.
- Current consolidated EUR-Lex text.
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and other applicable privacy, cybersecurity, product-safety, employment, equality, consumer-protection, and sector requirements where relevant.
- Official guidance and standards must be identified as non-binding unless incorporated through a binding requirement.

## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Binding versus recommended language:** Corrected masters now identify internal audit, sampling, maturity, roadmaps, dashboards, and programme methods as recommended practices unless an applicable legal duty makes them necessary in context.
2. **Role specificity:** Duties must be assigned according to provider, deployer, importer, distributor, authorised representative, product manufacturer, or GPAI-provider status.
3. **System classification:** High-risk, GPAI, systemic-risk GPAI, Article 50 transparency, and other systems must not be treated as subject to identical requirements.
4. **Timelines:** Roadmap dates are internal target periods and must not be confused with statutory application dates.
5. **Authority interaction:** Cooperation, evidence access, corrective action, and notification must be tied to the competent authority, legal basis, jurisdiction, and procedural context.
6. **Other law:** GDPR, cybersecurity, equality, employment, consumer, product-safety, and sector duties may independently apply.
