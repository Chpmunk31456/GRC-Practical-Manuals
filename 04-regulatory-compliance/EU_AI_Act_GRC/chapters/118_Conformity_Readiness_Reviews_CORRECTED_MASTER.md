# Chapter 118 — Conformity Readiness Reviews

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master for consolidation. This file controls over conflicting earlier Chapter 118 draft language.

## Requirement

Providers of high-risk AI systems must complete the applicable conformity-assessment route before placing the system on the market or putting it into service. Internal readiness reviews should test whether the required legal, technical, quality, documentation, registration, declaration, and marking evidence is complete and internally consistent.

## Plain-English explanation

A readiness review is an internal gate; it does not replace a legally required conformity assessment, notified-body involvement, market-surveillance review, or authority decision. Its purpose is to identify gaps before formal release or assessment.

## Review requirements

Confirm at minimum:

1. legal role, intended purpose, and high-risk classification;
2. applicable conformity route and any notified-body trigger;
3. quality-management and risk-management evidence;
4. data governance, technical documentation, logs, instructions, and oversight;
5. accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, testing, and monitoring;
6. EU declaration of conformity and CE-marking readiness where applicable;
7. registration and authorised-representative obligations;
8. unresolved nonconformities and corrective actions;
9. version and release linkage;
10. authorized release decision.

## GlobalWay example

Before releasing a high-risk employee-allocation system under its own name, GlobalWay confirms its provider role, applicable assessment route, Annex IV documentation, testing, QMS evidence, registration, declaration, and post-market plan.

## Control activity

Compliance must operate a documented readiness gate before any high-risk system is submitted for conformity assessment or released. Missing mandatory evidence is a release blocker.

## Evidence

- conformity applicability analysis;
- readiness checklist;
- technical-documentation index;
- test and validation reports;
- QMS and monitoring evidence;
- declaration, registration, and marking records;
- gap log and closure evidence;
- release approval.

## Audit test

Select high-risk releases. Confirm that readiness reviews identified the correct legal route, did not substitute for required external assessment, tested complete version-linked evidence, and blocked release until mandatory gaps were resolved.
