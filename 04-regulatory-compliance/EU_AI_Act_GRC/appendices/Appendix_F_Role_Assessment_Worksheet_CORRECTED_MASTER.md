## 6. Contract and operating-model controls

Confirm that contracts and procedures address:

- required technical and compliance information;
- instructions for use and limitations;
- monitoring and incident cooperation;
- audit and assurance rights;
- change, modification, and repurposing notification;
- authority communications;
- evidence retention;
- suspension, withdrawal, recall, and exit;
- allocation of privacy, security, employment, consumer, and product responsibilities.

## 7. Review triggers

Reassess after any change in entity, jurisdiction, provider, model, system, intended purpose, branding, distribution, integration, modification, operational control, or applicable law.
