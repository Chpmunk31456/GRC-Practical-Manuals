# Manual 34 — ISO/IEC 27701:2025 Release-Time Source State

**Verified:** 2026-08-31

## Authoritative edition/status decision

ISO's current catalog identifies ISO/IEC 27701:2025, Edition 2, as the published Privacy Information Management Systems standard. ISO identifies ISO/IEC 27701:2019 as withdrawn and revised by the 2025 edition.

Manual 34 therefore uses ISO/IEC 27701:2025 as the sole controlled ISO/IEC 27701 edition for release architecture, terminology, implementation guidance, localization, evidence design, and provenance.

## Copyright and implementation boundary

ISO normative text is copyrighted and is not reproduced in this repository. The manual contains project-authored implementation guidance, evidence patterns, operating-model design, audit-readiness practices, and high-level references to concepts and public bibliographic facts.

The manual must not claim that implementation alone establishes certification, accredited conformity assessment, or compliance with any privacy law. Jurisdiction-specific privacy law, contractual obligations, regulatory guidance, and organizational implementation decisions remain separate source layers.

## Legacy reconciliation

No withdrawn 2019 clause structure, numbering, certification dependency, or controller/processor wording may be silently inherited. Any reusable legacy implementation concept must be independently restated against the 2025 Edition 2 boundary before inclusion.

## Localization boundary

The es-419 and pt-BR editions are unofficial project translations, not ISO-authorized translations. Privacy-management, PII controller/processor, management-system, audit, certification-readiness, and evidence terminology must retain the same controlled meaning across all three project editions.

## Release controls

Predecessor Manual 33 is published. Manual 34 remains unpublished until its controlled trilingual package passes structure/parity/release QA, a reproducible six-binary candidate is generated, exact artifact identities are bound to provenance, rendered/accessibility QA is complete, exact bytes are durably staged, and final catalog/work-product registry reconciliation passes on an exact validated head.
