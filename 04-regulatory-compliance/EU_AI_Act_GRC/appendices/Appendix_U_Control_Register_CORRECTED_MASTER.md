## Source types

- Binding statutory or regulatory duty
- Contractual duty
- Organization-imposed policy/control
- Standard or common specification
- Recommended governance/assurance practice
- Optional enhancement

A control must not be labelled legally required unless the cited provision applies to the recorded actor, system, use, jurisdiction, and application date.

## Design fields

Record scope, trigger, inputs, decision criteria, authority, segregation, dependencies, preventive/detective nature, manual/automated operation, evidence, escalation, failure consequences, fallback, compensating controls, and change triggers.

## Status values

Designed; implemented; operating; partially effective; ineffective; not applicable with rationale; suspended; retired.

## Review

Reassess after legal, actor-role, intended-purpose, system/model/version, data, supplier, jurisdiction, incident, audit, control-failure, or application-date changes.
