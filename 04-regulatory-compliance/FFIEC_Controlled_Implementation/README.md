# Manual 32 — FFIEC Controlled Implementation

Manual 32 provides a controlled implementation and examination-readiness method based on the active FFIEC Information Technology Examination Handbook and applicable member-agency overlays.

## Controlled editions

- `controlled/en/MANUAL_32_CONTROLLED_EN.md`
- `controlled/es-419/MANUAL_32_CONTROLLED_ES-419.md`
- `controlled/pt-BR/MANUAL_32_CONTROLLED_PT-BR.md`

Localized editions are unofficial project translations. They do not replace current FFIEC materials, binding U.S. banking laws/regulations, member-agency requirements, or institution-specific supervisory communications.

## Controlled boundary

FFIEC examination guidance, binding law/regulation, member-agency requirements and supervisory expectations, institution-specific findings/commitments, and organization practice are maintained as separate source layers. The sunset FFIEC Cybersecurity Assessment Tool is not represented as a current FFIEC examination or assessment baseline.

## Publication controls

Publication requires release-time source verification, 32-chapter trilingual parity, deterministic candidate generation, accessibility/rendered-page QA, immutable provenance, exact-byte staging, predecessor Manual 31 publication, and final catalog/release-registry reconciliation.
