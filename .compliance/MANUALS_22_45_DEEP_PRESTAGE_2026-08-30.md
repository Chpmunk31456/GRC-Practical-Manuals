# Manuals 22–45 Deep Pre-stage

Status: downstream preparation only. Publication order remains sequential and no manual may bypass its predecessor.

Purpose: carry source verification, controlled architecture, localization, evidence design, artifact-generation, rendered-QA, provenance and release-state preparation as far ahead as safely possible before each manual reaches the front of the publication queue.

## Shared prebuild controls for every manual

Each manual must prepare, before front-line publication work where feasible: current authoritative-source verification; a controlled 32-chapter architecture; English controlling source; es-419 and pt-BR localization architecture; evidence-object schema; graphics/accessibility plan; DOCX/PDF candidate-generation requirements; rendered accessibility/link/table/heading/language-metadata QA; SHA-256 checksums and provenance; durable exact-binary staging; catalog, registry, manifest and lifecycle reconciliation; and a release-time source-version recheck. Copyrighted standards must be paraphrased/originally operationalized rather than reproduced beyond permissible use.

## Queue

### Manual 22 — Cloud Security
Pre-stage NIST, CSA, cloud-provider and ISO-family source boundaries; shared-responsibility, identity, data, logging, workload, network, key-management, resilience, configuration, vulnerability, DevSecOps and multi-cloud evidence architecture. Distinguish provider-native guidance from normative standards.

### Manual 23 — DORA
Pre-stage current EU DORA regulation, RTS/ITS and supervisory guidance; ICT risk, incident reporting, resilience testing, third-party ICT risk, registers, subcontracting, exit and oversight evidence. Preserve EU legal-text authority boundaries and release-time delegated/implementing-act verification.

### Manual 24 — NIS2
Pre-stage Directive (EU) 2022/2555, national transposition watch, ENISA guidance and sector applicability. Architecture should cover governance accountability, risk measures, incident reporting, supply chain, vulnerability handling, BC/DR, cryptography, access, training and evidence of management oversight.

### Manual 25 — ISO 22301
Pre-stage BCMS architecture around context, leadership, BIA, risk, strategies, plans, exercising, evaluation and improvement. Respect ISO copyright and distinguish implementation readiness from certification/accreditation claims.

### Manual 26 — Incident Response & Cyber Crisis Management
Pre-stage NIST/CISA and other public incident-response guidance; preparation, detection, analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, communications, legal/regulatory escalation, executive crisis management, forensics, lessons learned and evidence preservation.

### Manual 27 — Data Governance & Privacy Engineering
Pre-stage NIST Privacy Framework, public privacy-engineering guidance, data lifecycle, classification, minimization, lineage, retention, quality, consent/legal-basis interfaces, access, de-identification, PETs, governance roles and audit evidence.

### Manual 28 — AI Privacy & Automated Decision Governance
Pre-stage privacy, automated-decision, profiling, AI governance and data-protection source families. Cover DPIA/AI impact interfaces, transparency, contestability, human oversight, training-data governance, inference/privacy risk, logging, monitoring and jurisdictional applicability.

### Manual 29 — Software / AI Supply Chain and Component Assurance
Pre-stage NIST SSDF, SLSA, SBOM/VEX, CISA secure-by-design, dependency, model/data/component provenance, build integrity, signing, CI/CD, third-party libraries, model supply chain and vulnerability-response evidence.

### Manual 30 — Enterprise GRC Integration & Crosswalks
Pre-stage original crosswalk methodology linking controls without asserting false equivalence. Cover canonical control objects, obligations, risk, policy, evidence, tests, exceptions, issues, owners, metrics, mappings and change-impact governance across the manual series.

### Manual 31 — NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500

### Manual 32 — FFIEC
Pre-stage current FFIEC handbooks/catalogs and banking-regulator guidance. Distinguish supervisory guidance from law/regulation and cover architecture, operations, development, resilience, authentication, third-party, cloud, cyber assessment and examination evidence.

### Manual 33 — SEC Cybersecurity Governance and Disclosure

### Manual 34 — ISO/IEC 27701

### Manual 35 — HITRUST CSF

### Manual 36 — Brazil LGPD

### Manual 37 — Colombia Data Protection / Habeas Data

### Manual 38 — FERPA
Pre-stage U.S. Department of Education/FERPA statutory and regulatory sources. Cover education-record scope, consent/exceptions, directory information, school-official controls, record of disclosures, rights, vendors, security and evidence without presenting the manual as legal advice.

### Manual 39 — COPPA
Pre-stage FTC COPPA rule/guidance and any current rulemaking/effective-date changes. Cover child-directed applicability, parental notice/consent, data minimization, retention, security, operators/service providers, safe-harbor interfaces and audit evidence.

### Manual 40 — CJIS Security Policy

### Manual 41 — UK GDPR / Data Protection Act

### Manual 42 — Canada Privacy

### Manual 43 — ISO/IEC 27017 & 27018

### Manual 44 — NIST SSDF / SP 800-218
Pre-stage current SSDF and related NIST secure-software guidance. Cover organizational preparation, protected software, secure production, vulnerability response, development environment, provenance, CI/CD, threat modeling, testing, dependencies and evidence.

### Manual 45 — Enterprise Compliance Training Capstone

Cover role-based learning paths, scenario exercises, control/evidence labs, cross-framework decisioning, audit simulations, incident/privacy/AI/supply-chain cases, assessment rubrics, accessibility and completion evidence.
