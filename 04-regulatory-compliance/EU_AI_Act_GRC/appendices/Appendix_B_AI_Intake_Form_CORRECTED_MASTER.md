# Appendix B — AI Intake Form

> **Legal status:** Corrected English master. This is an organizational intake control. Mandatory legal conclusions must be tied to the relevant actor, system, use, provision, and application date.

## 1. Request information

- Request title, business unit, requestor, executive sponsor, legal entity, countries, launch date, and project/procurement reference.

## 2. Use case

Document intended purpose, users, affected persons, decisions/actions influenced, degree of automation, consequences of error, expected benefit, non-AI alternative, and foreseeable misuse.

## 3. Technology and value chain

Record provider/vendor, system/model/version, hosting, APIs, tools, agents, plugins, open-source components, subprocessors, data providers, branding, integration, modification, and update process.

## 4. Data

Record input, training, tuning, retrieval, monitoring, personal, special-category, confidential, children’s, vulnerable-person, source, lawful-use, location, transfer, retention, deletion, and customer-data training information.

## 5. Legal and risk screening

Answer Yes, No, or Uncertain and cite evidence:

- Does the current statutory AI-system definition apply?
- Which legal entities and AI Act roles may apply?
- Could an Article 5 prohibited practice be involved?
- Could Article 6/Annex I or Annex III high-risk treatment apply?
- Could Article 50 or other transparency duties apply?
- Is a GPAI model or systemic-risk GPAI model involved?
- Could Article 27 FRIA, GDPR DPIA/Article 22, employment, equality, accessibility, consumer, product, cybersecurity, or sector duties apply?
- Could own-brand placement, intended-purpose change, or substantial modification transfer provider duties?
- Which provision-specific application dates and transitional rules apply?

## 6. Safeguards and evidence

Describe human oversight, challenge/appeal, testing, security, monitoring, incident response, fallback, transparency, accessibility, training, vendor evidence, and evidence repository.
