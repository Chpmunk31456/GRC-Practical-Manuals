# Appendix L — Conformity-Readiness Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist to determine whether an AI system and its supporting governance, documentation, controls, and evidence are ready for conformity-related review.

## 2. Governance and accountability

- [ ] Legal role and classification are documented.
- [ ] Accountable owners are assigned.
- [ ] Quality-management procedures are approved.
- [ ] Risk-management responsibilities are defined.
- [ ] Changes and releases are controlled.
- [ ] Supplier responsibilities are documented.
- [ ] Findings, exceptions, and corrective actions are tracked.

## 3. Risk management

- [ ] Foreseeable risks are identified.
- [ ] Fundamental-rights and safety impacts are assessed.
- [ ] Risk controls are implemented and tested.
- [ ] Residual risk is documented and approved.
- [ ] Monitoring and reassessment triggers are defined.
- [ ] Risk evidence matches the deployed version.

## 4. Data governance

- [ ] Data sources and provenance are documented.
- [ ] Lawful acquisition and use are assessed.
- [ ] Quality, relevance, and representativeness are evaluated.
- [ ] Bias and subgroup risks are tested.
- [ ] Preparation and transformation are reproducible.
- [ ] Privacy, retention, access, and security controls operate.

## 5. Technical documentation

- [ ] System architecture and dependencies are current.
- [ ] Model, configuration, and version information are complete.
- [ ] Intended purpose and limitations are clear.
- [ ] Development, training, tuning, and testing are documented.
- [ ] Accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity evidence is available.
- [ ] Human-oversight design is documented.
- [ ] Change history and known limitations are recorded.

## 6. Logging and traceability

- [ ] Required events are logged.
- [ ] Log retention and integrity are controlled.
- [ ] Inputs, outputs, overrides, and incidents are traceable.
- [ ] Production and documentation versions can be reconciled.
- [ ] Evidence can be retrieved promptly.

## 7. Transparency and human oversight

- [ ] User instructions and notices are complete.
- [ ] Limitations and expected performance are disclosed.
- [ ] Human reviewers are trained and competent.
- [ ] Override, escalation, and suspension are available.
- [ ] Automation-bias controls are implemented.
- [ ] Complaint and appeal channels are operational.

## 8. Validation and testing

- [ ] Acceptance criteria are approved.
- [ ] Design and operating effectiveness are tested.
- [ ] Performance is tested under expected and adverse conditions.
- [ ] Relevant subgroups and languages are evaluated.
- [ ] Security, misuse, resilience, and fallback are tested.
- [ ] Test limitations and unresolved defects are documented.

## 9. Post-market monitoring

- [ ] Monitoring indicators and thresholds are defined.
- [ ] Complaints, incidents, drift, and changes are reviewed.
- [ ] Serious-event escalation is documented.
- [ ] Corrective-action closure is validated.
- [ ] Regulatory commitments are tracked.
