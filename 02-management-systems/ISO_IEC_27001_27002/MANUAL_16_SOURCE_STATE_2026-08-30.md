**Verification date:** 2026-08-30  
**Purpose:** release-preparation source-state evidence; not publication authorization and not an ISO-authorized reproduction or translation.

## Current authoritative baseline

Official ISO catalog state was reverified immediately after Manual 15 publication:

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** remains **Published**, Edition 3, publication date 2022-10, stage 60.60. ISO identifies it as the requirements standard for information security management systems.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022/Amd 1:2024 — Climate action changes** remains **Published**, Edition 3, publication date 2024-02, stage 60.60, and explicitly applies to ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- **ISO/IEC 27002:2022** remains **Published**, Edition 3, publication date 2022-02, corrected English version 2022-03, stage 60.60. ISO identifies it as information-security controls guidance.

## Controlled interpretation boundary

Manual 16 must continue to distinguish ISO/IEC 27001 requirements from ISO/IEC 27002 implementation guidance. It must not reproduce protected clause, Annex A, control, table, or explanatory text beyond legally permissible identifiers and brief quotations. It must not imply ISO endorsement or certification. Statement of Applicability and control-selection decisions remain risk-, context-, and organization-specific.

The 2024 climate-action amendment must be reflected in organizational-context and interested-party analysis at the implementation level without copying protected amendment wording.

## Source-change rule

This verification supports continued controlled build against the 2022/2024 baseline. A release-time source check remains mandatory. Any later ISO lifecycle change, corrigendum, amendment, superseding edition, or material official interpretation affecting correctness reopens source-impact analysis before publication.
