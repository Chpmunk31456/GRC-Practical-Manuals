## Verified source state

- **NIST CSF 2.0 / NIST CSWP 29** remains the current published Cybersecurity Framework baseline. NIST continues to describe CSF 2.0 as applicable across industry, government, and organizations and organized around the six Functions GOVERN, IDENTIFY, PROTECT, DETECT, RESPOND, and RECOVER.
- **NIST SP 1308** is final, published March 2026, and provides current guidance connecting cybersecurity, enterprise risk management, and workforce management.
- **NIST SP 1347** is final as of 2026-08-25. It provides current guidance on CSF 2.0 Informative References, human- and machine-readable mappings, the CSF 2.0 Reference Tool, and the Online Informative References Program.

- Profiles, Tiers, Informative References, enterprise-risk integration, and workforce integration should remain clearly distinguished.
