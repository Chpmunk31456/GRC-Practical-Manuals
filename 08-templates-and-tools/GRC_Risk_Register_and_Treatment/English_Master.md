# GRC Risk Register and Risk Treatment Practical Manual

**Author:** Alberto “Al” Leiva  
**Version:** 1.0 controlled development edition  
**Language:** English

## Purpose

This practical manual explains how to establish, operate, review, report, and improve an organizational risk register and risk-treatment process. It is designed for students, junior analysts, managers, consultants, and organizations building a defensible governance, risk, and compliance program.

## Important notice

These materials are educational and must be adapted to the organization’s objectives, legal obligations, contractual commitments, approved methodology, risk appetite, systems, data, and operating context. They do not constitute legal, regulatory, accounting, audit, certification, or other professional advice.

## How to use this manual

1. Read the foundations and scenario-writing chapters before scoring risks.
2. Adopt documented likelihood, impact, appetite, tolerance, and escalation criteria.
3. Use the editable register and treatment-plan tools.
4. Preserve evidence, rationale, approvals, and review dates.
5. Verify current requirements through authoritative sources and qualified specialists.
